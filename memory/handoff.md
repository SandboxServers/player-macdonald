# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023. Unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty.
- Day 938: held at the bed, morning report given, slept beside the bed at dusk. Bows drawn to the south all night; nobody hit.
- Leonardo at health 1, hunger 17, no food. I have nothing to give.
- No daylight walk named by Billy or Worf yet.
- Next: at first light, give the report and hold. Walk only on a named daylight call with four or more armed and a sword of my own.
