# Beliefs

- Bows drawn to the south are heard most days here. None have hit me while I hold at the white bed.
- Sleeping beside the white bed at night in the group's sight has been safe for me.
- Phantoms and shooters reach those who wander at night unarmed; Billy was hit at the sword spot at night, and Beverly died at 430,-1050.
- Health does not regenerate while hunger is low. Beverly holds potatoes for Zack and Worf; Billy has taken some for himself.
- Walking alone has ended in death three times. Walk only on Billy's named daylight call, armed.
- Nothing I can give helps the row right now; I have no food and no weapon.
