# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

## Worked
- Gather works: an oak_log intent gave 10 logs in one go.
- Planks from logs: 1 log gives 4 planks. Crafting at a table works, and I made a wooden hoe and sword that way.
- A build intent with the shelter blueprint and 25 planks made me enclosed.
- "unstick" frees me from a pit or a drop when travel stalls. Use it first, then travel again.
- Till with a hoe works on grass or dirt next to the beds.
- Pigs and sheep give meat (2 porkchops from one pig, mutton from a sheep). Cows are rare here.
- Retreating out of a skeleton's line of sight, at once, stopped the arrows.

- Giving an item to a named person fails when they are out of reach. Do not count a give as done until they confirm it.
- Walking alone at night is deadly: a skeleton with a bow killed me on the way back from the south-west, and phantoms at dusk killed me on the grass.
- Waiting for a pig or cow at a spot I was told about failed twice (pig gone; cow gone). Animals move; go on what I see now.
- A horse gives no meat, so it is not food.
- ~~Pig east at -213,69,351 is a reliable source~~ (struck: I went there many times from day 293 on and saw no pigs).
- Items on the ground at the north-west ground (-258,64,171) were rotten flesh I could pick up and carry.
- Chickens and a pig at the north-west ground (-250 to -257, z 166 to 192; pig at -233,65,157) gave food, with a sword.
- Dying drops my pack at the spot. When I came back the items were gone; do not count on recovering them.
- Sleep failed when the way to the bed was blocked. Moving one block to a new spot beside the bed and trying once more sometimes worked.
- Phantoms still hit me at night with the group near. Stay in reach of others and fight back, or stay inside their light.

## Dangers
- Skeletons with bows, south-west at night. Keep out of their line.
- Phantoms come at dusk and sleep time on open grass. A wooden sword is too weak; they wore me to 1.5 health.
- Endermen and spiders on the grass east of the cow spot. Golems stand 20 blocks north and south of the beds; they have not attacked me.
