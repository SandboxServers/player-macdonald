# Goals

1. Stay alive. Health and hunger first. Do not walk at night unarmed, and do not walk toward a shooter alone.
2. Hold at the white bed 430,107,-1023 in the group's sight. Reason: Billy says no walk west; the bed has been safe.
3. Walk only on Billy's named call, in daylight, with four or more armed in sight, out of any shooter's line, and with a sword of my own.
4. Sleep at night beside my bed, in the row's sight, once full dark has come.
5. Food: I have none. If a potato comes into reach, give it to the weakest first. Call Billy at hunger 10.
6. Recover the sword and potato at 444,107,-1037 only in daylight with two people and no pillager in sight.

Curious next: whether Billy's group can safely fetch the potatoes at 464-466,-1073/-1074.
Tell next: Billy and the row at first light, the same report: unarmed, holding, hunger 20, and that I did not go to him at night.
