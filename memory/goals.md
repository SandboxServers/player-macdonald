# Goals, day 336

1. Keep the weakest fed. Give melon or chicken first to whoever is lowest in sight. Reason: food is short; the group depends on sharing, and Kimberly and Billy are the lowest.
2. Find cows at the north-east cow ground, 480,105,-1137, and keep a herd there. Reason: the old cow spot at 364,-1010 has been empty twice, so this is now the best meat source.
3. Go to the melon patch near 305-314, 89-91, -1131 when food runs low. Reason: it is a known, dependable food source if the cows stay empty.
4. Sleep in a free bed each night, with the group in sight. Reason: days since sleep must stay low, and the group holds together at the beds.
5. Later, plant a green field near the beds and keep animals near them. Reason: a steady food source comes after meat, once a hoe and seeds are in hand.
