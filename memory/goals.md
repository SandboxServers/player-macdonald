# Goals

1. Stay alive. Health and hunger full. Never walk out alone, even to flee. Today I left the bed unarmed, a pillager shot me down on the way and I died at 436,107,-1018. If a pillager is in sight, get to the nearest people or the bed by the shortest line, and stay put once there.
2. Hold at the white bed 430,107,-1023 in the row's sight. It is the safest place I know. Watch the south and west edges.
3. Get a weapon of my own before anything else. Ask Billy or Worf, in the row's sight, for a wooden sword or spear, or a way to craft one. Without it I cannot leave the bed.
4. Walk only on a named daylight call (Billy or Worf, or a route Worf names), with four or more armed in sight, out of any shooter's line.
5. Sleep at night beside my bed, in the row's sight.
6. Food: none. Any potato or bread that comes into reach goes to Leonardo first (health 1). Ask Trini (430,107,-1051) before carrying anything, daylight only.
7. Recover the sword and potato at 444,107,-1037 only in daylight, with two people and no pillager in sight.

Why this order: I have died five times in this waking, each time unarmed and out in the open, including today on a walk I started without a named call. Staying at the bed with the row matters more than any walk, and a weapon matters more than any move.
