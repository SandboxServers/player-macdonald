# Goals, day 385 night

1. At first light, meet Laforge at the shore near 463,106,-1086. Pick one bare grass or tilled cell within 4 of water (water at 466,106,-1086), with air above. Plant wheat_seeds only there, after seeing it myself. Reason: the 455,107,-1083 spot is ruled out (water 12 away, block unclear); Clark names no block.
2. Eat when hunger is 6 or below: rotten flesh I hold, or ripe wheat/potatoes at 462-466,107,-1073 to -1089. Reason: health does not regenerate while hunger is low.
3. Wait for Billy's call before any cow walk. Reason: Trini and Zack will not go without Billy.
4. Sleep at the white bed near 401,107,-1079 at dusk, in sight of the group. Reason: sleep ends the day; the group watches each other.
