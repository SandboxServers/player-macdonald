# Goals, day 384 night

1. At first light, go with Laforge to a grass or tilled cell beside water (within 4), with air above. Plant wheat_seeds only there, after seeing it myself. Reason: Clark names no block; the 455,107,-1083 spot is too far from water.
2. Eat when hunger is 6 or below: rotten flesh I hold, or ripe wheat/potatoes at 462-463,107,-1073 to -1087. Reason: health does not regenerate while hunger is low.
3. Wait for Billy's call before any cow walk. Reason: Trini and Zack will not go without Billy.
4. Sleep at the white bed near 401,107,-1079 at dusk, in sight of the group. Reason: sleep ends the day; the group watches each other.
