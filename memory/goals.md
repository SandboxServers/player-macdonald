# Goals
1. Stay alive: be inside the group at the beds before dusk, away from skeleton lines and phantom ground. Reason: death is permanent, and I have lost my pack twice.
2. Find food: go with the scouts to the north-west ground for chickens and pigs, and check the ground for dropped food. Reason: I have had no food since day 293, and this is where food came from.
3. Give food to the hungriest first, and hand it over only when the person is in reach and confirms. Reason: gives that fail leave people hungry.
4. Get a sleep spot: try a bed once, then sleep with the group. Reason: most bed clicks fail.
5. Keep the wheat growing by the white beds and break it by hand when ripe. Reason: it is the only steady food on the grounds.

Curious: whether the village has farms or a steady animal ground.
