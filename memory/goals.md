# Goals

1. Stay alive. Health and hunger full. No walking at night, no walking toward a shooter alone.
2. Hold at the white bed 430,107,-1023 in the row's sight. Safe through day 1087; hold on.
3. Walk only on a named daylight call (Billy or Worf, or a route Worf names), with four or more armed in sight, out of any shooter's line, and with a sword of my own.
4. Sleep at night beside my bed, in the row's sight.
5. Food: none. Any potato or bread that comes into reach goes to Leonardo first (health 1). Trini holds the row's potatoes at 430,107,-1051; ask her before carrying anything, and only in daylight.
6. Recover the sword and potato at 444,107,-1037 only in daylight, with two people and no pillager in sight.

Curious next: whether Worf names a daylight route, and whether Trini hands out the potatoes. Who could I tell: Leonardo and Troi, first, when anything changes.
