# Goals, day 384 night

1. At first light, check the block near 455,106,-1083 myself. Plant wheat_seeds only where I can see bare tilled farmland, air above, and water within 4. Reason: Clark will not name a block and says plant only where I have seen it; planting on a taken or unwatered block fails.
2. Eat when hunger is 6 or below: rotten flesh I hold, or ripe wheat/potatoes at 462-463,107,-1073 to -1087. Reason: health does not regenerate while hunger is low.
3. Wait for Billy's call before any cow walk. Reason: Trini and Zack will not go without Billy.
4. Sleep at the white bed near 401-402,107,-1078 at dusk, in sight of the group. Reason: sleep ends the day; the group watches each other.
