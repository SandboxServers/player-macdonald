4. Plant the field outside the bed house with dirt and wheat_seeds using the hoe. Reason: a steady food source is still missing.
5. Sleep in bed 401,107,-1079 at dusk. Reason: sleep ends the day.
6. Stay in sight of the group at the beds. Reason: the group shares food and watches each other.
