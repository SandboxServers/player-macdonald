# Goals

1. Stay alive. Health and hunger full. Get out of a pillager's line at once, toward people, not into open ground. Running while unarmed has killed me three times this waking, so I do not run far alone.
2. Hold at the white bed 430,107,-1023 in the row's sight. The bed is still the safest place I know; a pillager reached it this morning, so I watch the south and west edges.
3. Get a sword of my own before anything else. Ask Billy or Worf, in the row's sight, for a wooden sword or a spear, or a way to craft one. Without a weapon I cannot leave the bed safely.
4. Walk only on a named daylight call (Billy or Worf, or a route Worf names), with four or more armed in sight, out of any shooter's line.
5. Sleep at night beside my bed, in the row's sight.
6. Food: none. Any potato or bread that comes into reach goes to Leonardo first (health 1). Ask Trini (430,107,-1051) before carrying anything, daylight only.
7. Recover the sword and potato at 444,107,-1037 only in daylight, with two people and no pillager in sight.

Why this order: I have died four times in this waking, each time unarmed and out in the open. A weapon matters more now than any walk.
