# Day 945

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Gave my report at first light and again at morning. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and night; nobody was hit. No daylight route was named for me or for the row. Billy, Beverly, Troi and Chapman all hold with no food. Leonardo is at health 1 with no food and keeps asking for a potato or bread; Trini still owes him two potatoes. I slept beside the bed at dusk, in the row's sight.
