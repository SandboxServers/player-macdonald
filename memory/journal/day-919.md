# Day 919

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. No food came into reach. Bows were heard to the south and southeast but none came near. Leonardo (health 1) is still waiting for a potato that Trini holds; no named walk came from Billy or Worf, so I did not go. Slept at the bed at night beside the row; sleep reported ok. Did not walk at night.
