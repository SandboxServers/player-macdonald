# Day 569

Woke at the row beside the bed at 443,107,-1036 with Lewis near. At dawn I reported to Billy, sword in hand, health 20, hunger 20, and held in his sight. No walk was named. Zack got his sword from Billy, Leonardo got one, and Tommy is waiting for one. Riker's 2 potatoes went to him from Troi in the end. Billy named the 11 others to wait for a daylight escort to the potato field. At dusk I slept at the bed beside Lewis in the row's sight. Nothing was lost, nothing gained. The day was for holding.
