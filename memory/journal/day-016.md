# Day 1016

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. No walk was named and no food came into the row. Bows were drawn to the south several times and nothing came near. Troi spoke from her bed that she would sleep in the row's sight tonight. I said my goodnight by say and slept beside the bed in the row's sight. Leonardo is still low on health with no food.
