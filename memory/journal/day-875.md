# Day 875

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 through the night and the morning, with Trini in reach. Health stayed at 20, hunger at 20, no food, nothing in hand. Bows were drawn to the south all day and night and none hit me. Billy did not call. Leonardo tried to come to Billy, then held at 443,-1037. Beverly still holds 10 potatoes and waits on Billy's word for a give. Slept beside the bed at night with the row in sight.
