# Day 792

Held at the row all day beside bed 443,107,-1051 in Billy's sight, sword in hand. Health 15, hunger 16, no food. Billy named no walk. Picard reports ripe potatoes at 463,107,-1073, east of the line, unseen by anyone else; Billy wants a sighting and an escort before anyone walks. Reported to Billy at dawn and dusk, slept at dusk beside the bed. Nothing new learned except the potato report.
