# Day 1122

Night at the white bed 430,107,-1023. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. No named call from Billy or Worf yet. Heard bows drawn to the south and southeast, none reached us. Bcrusher said he was going to his bed holding nothing. Sent a short say, journaled, and lay down beside the bed in the row's sight. Still no sword, no food; the goals stand.
