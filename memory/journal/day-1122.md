Day 1122. Woke at the white bed, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed. Said I need planks or sticks for a wooden sword. The hold intent ended at once, so I stopped re-setting it and just waited. Beverly, Leonardo, Kimberly and Troi all held still, waiting on a named call from Billy or Worf. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and none reached us. Night came; I stayed by the bed and slept.
