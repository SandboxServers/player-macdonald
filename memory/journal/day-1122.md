# Day 1122

Held at the white bed at 430,107,-1023 all day, in the row's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. No named call from Billy or Worf yet. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and night, but none came close. Troi (Deanna), Beverly and Leonardo are holding at the bed too, waiting for a route; Leonardo is hungry and waits on Trini's potatoes. I said my steady status to Deanna in the morning. My intents to hold kept finishing at once, so I stopped re-setting them and simply waited. Went to bed beside the bed at night.
