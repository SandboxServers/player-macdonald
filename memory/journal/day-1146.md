# Day 1146

Held all day at the white bed 430,107,-1023 in the row's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, still unarmed. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and night; none reached us. No named call from Billy or Worf, so no walk. Troi, Beverly, Kimberly and Leonardo are holding at their beds, all short of food. Nothing reached me to eat or carry. Slept beside the bed at night.
