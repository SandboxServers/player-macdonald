# Day 611

Woke at bed 415,107,-1037 with health 20 and hunger 20, sword in hand. Said morning to the row and held. Billy called the row to hold in sight with no walk, since no route to Trini is verified yet. Trini is armed at 444,107,-1023 and holds there. Nobody walked today. Went to bed at dusk at my bed in the row's sight.
