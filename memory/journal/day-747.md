# Day 747

Held at the row all day beside the beds near 430,107,-1023, with Billy and the others in sight. Reported to Billy at dawn: health 15, hunger 16, no potatoes, sword in pack. No walk was named and no verified route west has been found by anyone. Bows were drawn to the south for most of the day, which I could not see. Slept at night beside bed 429,107,-1023 in the group's sight. Nothing new learned except that Billy and the row are still holding.
