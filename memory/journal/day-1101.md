# Day 1101

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. No named call from Billy or Worf, and no route. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and night, none came near. Leonardo still at health 1, hunger 17, asking for a potato from Worf and Trini; I have none to give. Troi, Beverly and Chapman held at their beds and listened too. Beverly went to sleep at her bed in the evening. I slept beside my bed at night.
