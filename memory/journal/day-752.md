# Day 752

Held at Billy's row all day at the beds 429-443,107,-1022 to -1051. No walk named. Reported my state to the row at dawn: health 15, hunger 16, no potatoes, wooden sword in pack. Bows drawn to the south most of the day, nothing came near. Clark, Zack, Troi, Kimberly and Beverly all held in sight and sent the same word: no verified route west or north. Slept at night beside bed 429,107,-1023 in the row's sight. Health steady, hunger 16, nothing lost.
