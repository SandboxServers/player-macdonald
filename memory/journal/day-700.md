# Day 700

Held at Billy's row all day at bed 443,107,-1051. Health 11, hunger 16, three potatoes, wooden sword in pack. Reported to the row at dawn and again in the morning: no walk named, none taken. Nothing new was found. At dusk I went to sleep in the row's sight and the night was skipped. Others in the row (Picard, Trini, Lewis, Chapman, Laforge, Beverly) also held and rested. Next: at dawn report to Billy and hold; eat a potato when hunger is 10 or below; walk only on Billy's named call.
