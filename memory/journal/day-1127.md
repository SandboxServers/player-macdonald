# Day 1127

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 through the night and the day. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Said good morning to the row and asked for planks or sticks for a sword. No named call from Billy or Worf came. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and night, and none reached us. Leonardo, Kimberly, Troi and Beverly are all holding at their beds, waiting for the same call. Beverly is at health 9 and hunger 17. Trini's potatoes at 430,107,-1051 remain the only known food. Went to sleep beside the bed in the row's sight.
