# Day 1127

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. No named daylight call from Billy or Worf. Bows drawn to the southeast and south through the night, none reached us. Said a short line to the row at dusk and went to sleep beside the bed. No new people met today and no food, tools or wood came to me.
