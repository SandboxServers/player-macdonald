# Day 1127

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day with the row: Troi, Kimberly, Beverly, Leonardo, Bcrusher. No named daylight call from Billy or Worf. Bows heard to the southwest and south; none reached us. Nobody has answered Leonardo's request for Trini's potatoes. Riker went to sticks at 417,107,-1046 and asked Billy for them; no answer yet. I did not walk. Health 20, hunger 20, still unarmed and empty-handed. Slept beside the bed at night in the row's sight.
