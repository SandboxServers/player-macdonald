# Day 1088

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Bows were heard drawn to the south and southeast through the whole day; nothing reached the row. No named call from Billy or Worf. Troi, Chapman, Beverly and Kimberly are all holding with no food; Leonardo is at health 1 at the bed 416,107,-1023 and still has nothing. I have no potato or bread to give. Slept at night beside the bed.
