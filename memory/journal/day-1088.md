# Day 1088

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 through the night, in the row's sight, with no one near. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast several times and nothing reached the row. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No named daylight call from Billy or Worf yet. Set a rest intent to wake on any touch or hurt, then slept in the bed. Nothing new to learn today; next waking, at first light, short say and hold for a named call.
