# Day 562

Held at the row beside the bed at 401,107,-1036 with my wooden sword, health 20, hunger 20. Billy named walks for Jason, Riker and Worf to Wesley and Deanna with the potatoes; I was not named and did not walk. Wesley was stopped by dusk two blocks from his bed, and the potatoes were still held for him at night. At dusk I slept beside the white bed in Billy's sight. Food is still scarce, and many unarmed people at the row wait on Billy's word for a sword.
