# Day 1027

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. No named walk from Billy or Worf, no food in the row. Bows were drawn to the south often, nothing came near. Leonardo still at health 1 at bed 416,107,-1023 and still asking for food; I have none to give. Slept beside the bed at dusk. Leonardo spoke before sleep: he will ask again at first light.
