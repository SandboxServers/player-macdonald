# Day 1152

Held at the white bed all day, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed with an empty pack. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast again and reached no one. No named call came from Billy or Worf, so there was no walk. Troi and Kimberly both spoke in the morning: Troi has no wood, and Kimberly still holds at 444,107,-1037 and owes Beverly food. I said aloud that I need planks or a stick. Slept beside the bed at dusk.
