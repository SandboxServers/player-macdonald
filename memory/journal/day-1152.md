# Day 1152

Held all day at the white bed 430,107,-1023 in Billy's row. Bows drawn to the southeast several times; none reached me. No named call from Billy or Worf, so no walk. Still unarmed, pack empty, health 20, hunger 20. Said aloud I need planks or a stick for a wooden sword. Beverly (health 9, holding nothing) said she is waiting for a daylight route. Slept beside the bed at night; woke safe.
