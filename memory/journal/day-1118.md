# Day 1118

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and into night. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. No named daylight call from Billy or Worf. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast through the evening; none reached us. Beverly, Kimberly and Troi held at the beds too. Leonardo still has no food; Trini's potatoes at 430,107,-1051 remain the only known food. I said goodnight in the row's sight and tried to sleep at the bed. Nothing new learned, nothing lost.
