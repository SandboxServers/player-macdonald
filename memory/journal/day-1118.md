# Day 1118

Held at the white bed at 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, pack empty, health 20, hunger 20. No named call from Billy or Worf and no weapon came. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day; none reached us. Troi, Kimberly and Beverly held near the beds and were all waiting for a named call. Leonardo asked Trini for potatoes at 423. At dusk I went to sleep beside the bed in the row's sight.
