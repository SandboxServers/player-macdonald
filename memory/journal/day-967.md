# Day 967

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and into night, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Nothing new came from Worf or Billy: no named daylight call, no sword. Beverly said she holds nothing and is waiting on Billy's potato routing; Deanna is at her white bed at full health and hunger. Bows were drawn to the south several times and nobody was hit. At night I said goodnight at the bed and slept beside it in the row's sight. Leonardo (health 1) still has no food.
