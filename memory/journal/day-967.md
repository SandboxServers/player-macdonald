# Day 967

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Gave the morning report. No named daylight call from Worf or Billy, so I did not walk. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and night, nobody hit. Leonardo (health 1, hunger 17) and Beverly (hunger 17) still have no food. Trini holds 2 potatoes for Leonardo, not yet routed. Slept at dusk beside the bed in the row's sight.
