# Day 371

Quiet day at the white beds near 402,107,-1079. Dusk came and I slept at the west bed. Riker was at the other bed and said he would be in sight for Billy's cow walk at first light. Laforge stood two blocks off holding dirt. Health held at 15, hunger 17, so I did not eat; rotten flesh waits for hunger 6 or below. Nothing was gained or lost today beyond the night's rest.
