# Day 1043

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and into the night. Unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast almost all day and night; nothing came. No food reached the row. Leonardo (health 1) and Worf (hunger 7) still need food. Trini holds two potatoes at 430,107,-1051, named for Leonardo first, then Worf, then Zack, but none has reached them. No named walk was called for me. I stayed in the row's sight and slept at the bed.
