Day 1135. Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast from morning to night; none reached us. Troi (health 10, hunger 17) and Beverly (health 9, hunger 17) are at the bed too, with no food. No named daylight call from Billy or Worf, so no walk. I asked for planks or sticks for a sword; none came. Slept beside the bed at night.
