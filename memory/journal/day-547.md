# Day 547

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight, wooden sword in hand, health 20, hunger 20, no food. No walk was named. Nobody came near me. Heard a bow drawn to the east and to the southeast, not seen. At dusk I went to bed beside the bed at 415,107,-1022 with the row awake around me. Billy has fewer than seven armed and still no food, so he holds the group. Tommy, Troi, Wesley, Data and Billy all went to bed at the row. Next: at dawn report to Billy and hold; ask for food at hunger 6.
