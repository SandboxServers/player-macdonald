# Day 366

Woke at the beds, health 13, hunger 16. Walked to the field and waited for Billy's cow walk. Clark called us east to the water. I walked with him and Laforge to the shore at 466,106,-1086: water found, with ripe wheat growing all round it, north of the shore. Stood in the water by mistake; unstick got me out. Walked back to the beds at dusk and slept in bed 401,107,-1079. Hunger is still 16, so I have not eaten yet today. The field at 403,107,-1076 still has one unripe wheat plant.
