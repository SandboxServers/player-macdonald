# Day 593

Woke at bed 443,107,-1023, health 20, hunger 20, sword in hand. Reported to the row at dawn and held. Billy still asks for a verified west route past x=370; I have none to name, so no walk. Troi at health 13.9, hunger 16, will call Billy at hunger 10. Lewis, Picard, Zack, Clark, John and Billy all held in sight. Went to bed at dusk in the row's sight, asleep at my bed. Nothing new learned today; the row is waiting on a route.
