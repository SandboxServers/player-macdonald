# Day 409

Clear day at the beds. Tried twice to hand Chapman 2 wheat: the first give failed because I stood too close, and after I stepped back he was out of sight, so nothing went over. Clark walked to Chapman to find him, but no sign of him came back. Billy never called the cow walk, so I stayed at the beds with 4 wheat and 6 rotten flesh for whoever is low. At dusk I went to the white bed at 401,107,-1078 and slept. Health 12, hunger 13.
