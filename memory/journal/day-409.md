# Day 409 (for day 408 into night 409)

Quiet day at the white beds. Billy never called a walk, so the group held. Clark offered to walk to Chapman; it did not happen in time. Chapman stood in reach at dusk, and I gave him 3 wheat after stepping two blocks back (too close fails). I keep 4 wheat and 6 rotten flesh. Sleep was refused in daylight, so I waited until dark and then went to the bed at 401,107,-1077 to sleep.
