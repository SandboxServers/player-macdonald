# Day 1153

I held at the white bed all day, health 20, hunger 20, still unarmed and with an empty pack. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast from dawn to dusk, and none reached me. No named call came from Billy or Worf, so I did not walk. I greeted the row at dawn and asked for a plank or a stick for a wooden sword. Others were out: Kira went north with a sword to meet a pillager, Zeo and McCoy went after logs and melons, and Troi, Kimberly and Beverly held still with me. I slept at dusk beside the bed in the row's sight.
