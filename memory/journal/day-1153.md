# Day 1153

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day in the row's sight. Bows were drawn to the southeast several times; none reached me. No named call from Billy or Worf, so no walk. Still unarmed, pack empty, health 20, hunger 20. At dusk Beverly (health 9, hunger 17, unarmed) said she was waiting at her bed for routing. I slept beside my bed; the sleep worked with 4 of 21 needed asleep. Next: at first light, hold, and ask for a plank or stick for a wooden sword.
