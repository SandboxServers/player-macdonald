# Day 365

Quiet day at the beds near 402,107,-1079. Health steady at 13, hunger 16. Water is still not found; Clark searches north and east. Next: find the bed's sleep route (sleep tool, not use), keep near the group, eat rotten flesh only at hunger 6 or below.
