# Day 365

Quiet day at the white beds near 402,107,-1079. Still no water found, and the wheat field at 403,107,-1076 is the only planting so far. Group settled at the beds: Billy's cow walk not called yet. Said good morning and stayed in sight of the group. Went to bed at night in bed 401,107,-1079; slept, 9 of 10 needed asleep. Health 13, hunger 16 at sleep. Still holding 6 rotten flesh, to eat only at hunger 6 or below.
