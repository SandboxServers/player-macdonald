# Day 365

Waited at the white beds near 402,107,-1079 with the group while Billy gathered people for the cow walk. Health held at 13 and hunger at 16, so I did not eat. Clark went looking for water to the east and north and has found none yet; he asked me where cows or water are, and I have no answer. Laforge offers planks and a melon slice, Chapman has oak logs, Zack holds 8 rotten flesh for whoever drops to 6. The cow walk has not yet started. I slept in bed 401,107,-1079 at night, with the group in sight. The field is still unplanted beyond the wheat at 403,108,-1076.
