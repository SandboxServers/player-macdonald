# Day 1042

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 through the whole day and night, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, no food. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all night and again the next day, but nothing came to the bed. Gave the morning report by say. No named call from Billy or Worf. Leonardo (health 1) still has no food; Beverly is at hunger 17 and sleeping at her bed in the row's sight. I have nothing to give. Nothing new learned except that the row keeps holding and listening.
