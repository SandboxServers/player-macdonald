# Day 830

Slept at the bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row, health 20, hunger 20, no sword, no food. Woke at night and held at the bed. At dawn I reported to the row the three deaths and the sword and potato at 444,107,-1037. Nobody named a walk for me. Trini is at health 12 and hunger 16 with no food; I have none to give. Lewis holds bed 443,107,-1051 with his sword. Kimberly still has the potato meant for Beverly. Nothing changed for me today except the waiting. Slept again at dusk and the day ends here.
