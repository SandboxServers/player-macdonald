# Day 559

Held at the row all morning, sword in hand, health 20, hunger 20. Billy named Jason, Worf and me for an escort to Wesley's bed, then changed the walk to a meeting at the row, and I did not move. No walk was named for me. Wesley was brought to the row late; Billy keeps 13 potatoes for him. Many unarmed men at the row are asking for swords and beds. I slept at dusk-to-night beside the white bed at 415,107,-1023, in the row's sight, with Billy, Zack and Data nearby.
