# Day 985

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. No daylight walk was named by Billy or Worf. Gave the morning report. Leonardo still health 1 with no food; Trini still holds his potatoes. Bows drawn to the south all day, nobody hit. Deanna (Troi) and Beverly holding at their beds too. Slept at dusk beside the bed in the row's sight.
