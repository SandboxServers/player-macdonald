# Day 874

Held the east row all day and night at 430,107,-1023, beside the white bed at 429,107,-1023. Unarmed, no food, health 20, hunger 20. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast the whole day; none hit me. Billy has not named a walk. Trini and Leonardo are still waiting on food. Beverly holds 10 potatoes owed to Worf and Zack, and offered to walk west with Billy when he names it. I said morning at dawn and held. I slept beside the bed in the row's sight.
