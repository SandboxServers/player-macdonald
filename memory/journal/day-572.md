# Day 572

Night of 571 I woke at the bed at 443,107,-1021 with the row in sight and held there, sword in hand, while Troi was hit by a phantom to the east. Billy walked out to her with the spare sword and brought her back to the row. In the morning Billy led a potato walk to 462-465,107,-1068 with Tommy, Laforge, Wesley, Trini and Zack, and came back with 35 potatoes. I was not on the escort and did not need food at hunger 20, so I reported to Billy and held. Billy gave Troi 10 potatoes; Riker and Lewis were also shorted and Troi's gives to Riker failed for lack of sight. At dusk I slept beside my bed at 443,107,-1023 in the row's sight.
