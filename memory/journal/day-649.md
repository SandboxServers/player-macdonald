# Day 649

Held at bed 429,107,-1051 in Billy's row all day. No walk was named. Hunger stayed at 17, so I ate nothing. Health stood at 9. Tommy, Deanna and Bcrusher said goodnight and slept in the row's sight. Slept at dusk beside my bed in the group's sight. Nothing new learned; the west path is still blocked by oak planks.
