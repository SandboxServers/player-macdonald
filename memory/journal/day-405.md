# Day 405

Sleep at the white bed failed at dusk because it was not yet night. I waited by the bed, then walked to the shore farm in daylight (462,106,-1084). The ground there is untilled grass; no bare farmland is verified, so I told Laforge I will till one cell and read back exact x y z before he plants. Walked back at dusk and reached the bed (401,107,-1077). Health 12, hunger 13. Still owe the bare-cell check and the planting with Laforge. Laforge, Zack, Clark, Johnny and Jason are all at the beds; Billy has not called the cow walk.
