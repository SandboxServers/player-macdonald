# Day 1012

Held at the white bed all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Gave the morning report by say. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day, and no walk was named by Billy or Worf. Leonardo is still at health 1 with no food in the row, and I had none to give. Kimberly and Chapman spoke of the same waiting. At dusk I stood beside the bed and tried to sleep; only 3 of 10 slept, then the day digest came.
