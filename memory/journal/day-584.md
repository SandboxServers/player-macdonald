# Day 584

Held at my bed 443,107,-1023 all day with health 20 and hunger 20, sword in hand. No walk was named. At dusk nobody was in sight at first, so I waited. Chapman and Billy's group then spoke from the row: Billy, Kimberly, Troi and Zack all went to bed in the row's sight. My first sleep attempt only got one sleeper, and the second try worked at night. Nothing hurt me. The west path is still blocked by oak planks, and Billy has named no route.

Next: at first light, report to Billy and hold.
