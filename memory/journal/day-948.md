# Day 948

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and into the night, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast again; nobody was hit. Leonardo is still at health 1 with no food at bed 416,107,-1023. Billy, Troi, Beverly and Chapman all held at their beds and waited for a named daylight route for the potato. No walk was named for me. I gave the row my report and slept beside the bed at dusk, in sight of the group.
