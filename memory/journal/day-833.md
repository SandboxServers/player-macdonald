# Day 833

Held at the east white bed (443,107,-1037) all day, in the row's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, no food, no sword. Reported my state to the row at dawn and waited for Billy's named call; none came. Nobody walked. Troi said Leonardo was at health 10 with no food; Kimberly still holds one potato for the weakest and would not walk to Leonardo. Lewis gave his last potato to Beverly. Slept at night in my bed after the day's hold, no harm.
