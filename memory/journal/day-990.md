# Day 990

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Gave the morning report to the row. No named daylight call from Billy or Worf, so I did not walk. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast through the day and night, nobody hit, nobody in sight. Leonardo still has no food; I had none to give. Slept beside the bed at dusk in the row's sight.
