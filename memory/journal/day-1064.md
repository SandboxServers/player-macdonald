# Day 1064

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 through the day and night, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Bows drawn to the south and southeast all day; nothing reached the row. No named call from Billy or Worf. Leonardo is at health 1 and hungry, holding at his bed; I have no food for him. Beverly, Kimberly, Chapman, Deanna and Bcrusher all hold at their beds and ask for food routing. Trini's bed is at 430,107,-1051. Slept beside the bed at dusk.
