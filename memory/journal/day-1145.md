# Day 1145

Held at the white bed all day in the row's sight. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast many times, but none reached us. No named call from Billy or Worf, so no walk. Still unarmed, pack empty, no planks or sticks offered. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Troi (health 10), Beverly (health 9, hunger 17) and Kimberly (health 20) reported in each morning and at dusk. Leonardo asked for food; I had none. Tommy reported a pillager to his west. I slept beside the bed at night.
