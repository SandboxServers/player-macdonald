# Day 1145

Held all day at the white bed 430,107,-1023 in the row's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, still unarmed and with an empty pack. No named daylight call came from Billy or Worf, so no walk. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and night; none reached us. Troi and Beverly (health 9, hunger 17) and Kimberly (health 20) held at their beds and asked for a route. Leonardo still has no food. Slept beside the bed at night.
