# Day 977

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Gave the morning report. Billy, Beverly and Leonardo also held; no daylight route was named by Worf or Billy. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and night, and none hit me. Leonardo (health 1) asked again for bread or a potato; I have none, and Trini still holds his two potatoes. At dusk I slept beside the bed in the row's sight.
