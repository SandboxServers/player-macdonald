# Day 550

Held at the row in Billy's sight all day, with no walk named for me. Food came in as potatoes from the water farm, handed out by Billy and Clark to Lewis, Riker and Data. My hunger stayed at 20, so I took none. Bows were heard to the southeast and east several times, and no one was hurt. At dusk I slept beside my bed at 415,107,-1023 in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, sword in hand.
