# Day 965

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Gave the morning report to the row. Worf and Billy still named no daylight route, so nobody walked. Billy sees no open air from his spot. Leonardo (health 1) still has no food, and I have none to give him. Bows were drawn to the south all day and nobody was hit. Troi, Johnny, Kimberly and Billy all hold at their beds in the row's sight. At night I went to sleep beside the bed.
