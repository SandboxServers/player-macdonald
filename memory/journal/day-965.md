# Day 965

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Gave the morning report. Deanna (Troi) greeted the row. Leonardo is still at health 1 with no food, and Beverly is at hunger 17 with no food. Nobody has a potato or bread to give. No named daylight call came from Worf or Billy, so I did not walk. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and nobody was hit. At dusk I slept beside my bed, in the row's sight.
