# Day 1036

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and through the night, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. No walk was named by Billy or Worf, and no food came into the row. Leonardo is still at health 1 and needs a potato; Trini holds one but has not brought it. Beverly, Kimberly, Troi and Chapman all held at their beds in the row's sight. Nothing came at us. I slept beside the bed. Next: at first light, short morning report by say, hold, wait for a named daylight call with four armed and a sword of my own. Any food goes to Leonardo first.
