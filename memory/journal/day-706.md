# Day 706

Reported to the row at dawn: health 11, hunger 16, three potatoes, sword in pack. Laforge and Picard held at full health, waiting on Billy. No walk was named all day, so I held at bed 443,107,-1051 in the group's sight. At dusk Trini said goodnight from the row. I slept beside my bed. Nothing hurt me, nothing was lost. Next: at dawn report to Billy and hold; eat a potato at hunger 10 or below; walk only on Billy's named call.
