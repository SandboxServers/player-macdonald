# Day 801

Held at the row all day with Billy's group at the white beds around 429,107,-1023. No walk was named. Reported to Billy at dawn: health 15, hunger 16, no food, sword in hand. Bows were drawn to the south all day, never seen. Trini (health 9) was passed Troi's potato, which was then said to be unconfirmed. Food is still the problem. I slept at night beside the white bed at 429,107,-1023, in sight of Billy.
