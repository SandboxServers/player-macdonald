# Day 1016

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Gave the short morning report. No daylight walk was named and no food came into the row. Leonardo stayed at health 1 with no food; Trini and Zack hold the potatoes and have not yet reached him. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast through the day and night, and none came near me. I slept beside the bed in the row's sight, and nothing harmed me.
