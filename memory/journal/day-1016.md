# Day 1016

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. No walk was named by Billy or Worf and no food came into the row. Leonardo is still at health 1 with hunger 17 and no food; I have none to give. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast through the day and night, but nothing came near. Gave the morning report by say, then slept at dusk beside the bed in the row's sight.
