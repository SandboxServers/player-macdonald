# Day 1016

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, in the row's sight. No walk was named and no food came into the row. Bows were drawn to the south several times, and nothing came near. Near dusk Troi said from the next bed that they had no food to share but were there. I slept beside the bed at night. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Leonardo is still low with no food, and I have none to give.

Nothing new was learned today. The row holds. Still waiting for a named daylight call from Billy or Worf.
