# Day 832

Held at the bed 443,107,-1037 all day and into dusk. Health 20, hunger 20, no sword, no food. No walk was named by Billy. Kimberly holds the potato for whoever is weakest, at hunger 10. Lewis and Deanna are holding at the white beds too. Reported holding to the row, then slept at the bed at dusk. Nothing new came in; nothing hurt me.
