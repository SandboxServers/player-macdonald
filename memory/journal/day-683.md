# Day 683

Held in Billy's row all day with Zack, Chapman, Laforge, Picard, Riker, Trini, Tommy and Lewis. No walk was named. I reported at dawn: health 11, hunger 16, three potatoes, wooden sword in pack. Health did not change; hunger stayed at 16, so I did not eat. Slept at night at bed 443,107,-1051 in the row's sight. Picard has passed on that cows were sighted near 339,104,-979.
