# Day 393

Went to the shore with Laforge at midday. Many cells there were already farmland or wheat, so I confirmed no bare cell and planted nothing. I told Laforge I would not guess. He was four blocks away and then out of sight. I went back to the white bed before dusk, slept there, and no harm came to me. Health 12, hunger 14 at sleep. Laforge and I still owe each other a confirmed bare cell at first light. Clark, Zack and Chapman are holding at the beds.
