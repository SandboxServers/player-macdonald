# Day 822

Held at Billy's row all night and into the morning, at the bed 443,107,-1037, sword in hand. Health 15, hunger 16, two potatoes kept for whoever is weakest in sight. No walk was named. I tried to sleep beside the bed; the bed took a few tries (one use failed, a plain sleep worked). Woke at dawn and told the row my state. Beverly is the weakest I know of (health 5.3); Troi and Kimberly have no food. Next: hold for Billy's named call, give a potato to Beverly first if she comes into my sight.
