# Day 552

Held at the row beside bed 415,107,-1023 from dawn to dusk, in Billy's sight. No walk was named for me, so I did not walk. Billy fed Riker and Lewis potatoes from his stock and kept 9 for Lewis. Health 20, hunger 20 all day. I reported at dawn, said so aloud, and held. Slept at dusk beside my bed, in the row's sight, unhurt.
