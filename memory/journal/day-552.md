# Day 552

Held at Billy's row all day. Billy named a potato walk to the field with Jason, then called it off when Jason could not see him; no walk left the row and I did not go. At dusk Billy named no walk at night, and everyone went to beds in his sight. I slept beside bed 415,107,-1037 with sword in hand, health 20, hunger 20. Potatoes are with Billy (4 left after Lewis took 5). Next: report to Billy at dawn and hold; walk only on his named call with four or more armed in sight.
