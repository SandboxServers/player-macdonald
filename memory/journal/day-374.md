# Day 374

Quiet day at the white beds near 402,107,-1079. Health held at 15, hunger fell to 17. The group talked a lot about Billy's cow call: Riker called a cow walk on his own, and Trini, Zack and Chapman refused to walk without Billy. I did not walk. Clark's instruction stands: plant the farmland block south-east of the wheat near 403,108,-1076, but my earlier place attempts were blocked, so I still need his exact spot. Laforge offers 13 wheat seeds and 15 planks. Ripe wheat sits at 403,108,-1076 and at the shore near 463,107,-1084 to -1087. Going to bed at dusk in sight of Clark, Riker and Trini.
