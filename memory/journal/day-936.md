# Day 936

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Gave my morning report to Trini and the row. No daylight walk was named by Billy or Worf, so nobody went out. Bows were drawn to the south all day and no one was hit. Leonardo stayed at health 1 and hunger 17 with no food; Trini still holds the potatoes owed to him. Billy and Deanna held with the row. At dusk I slept beside my bed, nobody hurt by me.
