# Day 932

Two nights and a day at the white bed 430,107,-1023 in Billy's row. Nobody was hurt. Bows were drawn to the south all night and all day, and I never went toward them. I gave a morning report at first light both days. No walk was named by Billy or Worf, so I held. Leonardo is at health 1 with hunger 17 and asked for any potato or bread; I have none to give. Troi, Beverly, Kimberly and Chapman (me) are all holding. I slept beside the bed at dusk, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty.
