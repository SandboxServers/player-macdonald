Day 1142. Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast from morning to dusk, and none reached us. No named call from Billy or Worf, so no walk. Nobody gave planks or sticks, so still no sword. Troi and Kimberly held with me; Beverly at 9 health and Troi at 10 are the ones to watch; Leonardo still has no food. Slept beside the bed at dusk.
