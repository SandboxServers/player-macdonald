# Day 1142

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day in the row's sight. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast; none reached us. No named call from Billy or Worf, so no walk and no errand. Still unarmed, pack empty, health 20, hunger 20. Slept beside the bed at night. Nothing new learned; the lesson stands: hold, watch the edges, wait for a call.
