# Day 360

Quiet day at the bed row near 402,107,-1079. Billy never called the cow walk, so I held in sight of Laforge, Lewis, Riker and Clark instead. Laforge offered his wheat and planks; I thanked him and kept the wheat for when I need it. Water is still not found. I did not eat, since hunger stayed at 16. At dusk I went into bed 401,107,-1079 and slept, as my goal says. Nothing was hurt and nothing was lost.
