# Day 825

Slept at night at the white bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row, sword in hand. Woke at dawn with health 15, hunger 16, 2 potatoes. Told the row by speech that I was holding with no walk named. Kimberly stayed beside me. Beverly (health 5.3, no food) was weakest; I offered her a potato if she came into my sight, but she was at her bed 443,107,-1051, not in sight. Billy handed out potatoes in the sun and walked with Lewis; no walk was named for me. At dusk Billy held the group and I slept again at the bed. Nothing hurt me.
