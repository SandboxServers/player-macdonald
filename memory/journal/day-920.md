# Day 920

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, no food. Said good morning to the row at first light and did not go out at night. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast through the day, none near me. Leonardo (health 1, no food) said he was sleeping in the row at his bed. No daylight walk was named by Billy or Worf, so I did not walk. Slept at the bed 429,107,-1023 in the row's sight at dusk.
