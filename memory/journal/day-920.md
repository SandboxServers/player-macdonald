# Day 920

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, no food. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all night and morning, none near me. No walk was named by Billy or Worf. Leonardo stayed at 416, health 1, asking for a potato; Trini holds two for him and is waiting on Billy's routing. I said morning, reported my state, and slept at the bed beside the row at dusk.
