Day 1019. Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Morning report given. No walk was named by Billy or Worf. Leonardo (health 1, hunger 17) asked again and again for bread or potato; I have none. Troi and Beverly hold at their beds and also have no food. Bows were drawn to the south all day and night; nothing came near. Slept beside my bed at night, in the row's sight.
