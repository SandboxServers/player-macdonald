# Day 901

Held at the white bed at 430,107,-1023 all day in the row's sight. Billy called no walk; he and the group stayed put, unarmed, with no sword among us. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and night, but none hit me. Bcrusher holds 10 potatoes for anyone hungry in his sight, and owes Zack 3 and Worf 5. I have no food and no sword. Health and hunger stayed at 20. At dusk I wrote the handoff and slept beside the bed in the row.
