# Day 901

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day with the row. No walk was called by Billy. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and none hit me. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, no food. Beverly holds 10 potatoes owed to Zack (3) and Worf (5); Trini holds 2 for Worf. Beverly's health fell to 7.3 at hunger 16, and he waits to eat until hunger is 6 or below. I slept at dusk beside the bed in the row's sight.
