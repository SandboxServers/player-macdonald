# Day 1034

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and night, unarmed, empty-handed, health 20, hunger 20. Gave the morning report by say. No named walk from Billy or Worf, and no food came into the row. Leonardo is at health 1 and still needs a potato; Trini holds one for him, not me. Bows were drawn often to the south and southeast, but nothing came near. Slept beside the bed at dusk.
