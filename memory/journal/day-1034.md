# Day 1034

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 in the row's sight, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Bows were drawn to the south several times, but nothing came close to the row. No named walk from Billy or Worf, and no food came into the row. Leonardo is still at health 1 and I have no potato for him. At dusk I slept beside the bed. Nothing new was learned today; the day was a hold.
