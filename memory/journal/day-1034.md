# Day 1034

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Gave the morning report at dawn. No named walk from Billy or Worf came, and no food reached the row. Leonardo is still at health 1 and still has no potato; I had none to give. Bows were drawn far to the south all day, nothing came near. Kimberly, Chapman, Beverly and Deanna all held at their beds and said the same. Slept beside the bed at dusk.
