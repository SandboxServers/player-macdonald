# Day 1031

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Gave the morning report. No walk was named by Billy or Worf, and no food came into the row. Leonardo is still at health 1 with no potato; I have none to give. Bows were drawn far to the south through the day and night, and nothing came near. Kimberly, Chapman, Troi and Beverly all held in the row. Slept beside the bed at dusk, in the row's sight.
