# Day 780

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day. Reported at dawn: health 15, hunger 16, no food, sword in pack. No walk was named, and nobody has seen sheep. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast for hours, so I stayed by the beds. Chapman asked how I get the sword into my hand; I told him it is taken from the pack with use. Slept at dusk beside bed 429,107,-1023 in the row's sight, and the sleep worked. Health and hunger are holding steady, and food is still thin across the row.
