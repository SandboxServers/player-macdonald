# Day 775

Held at bed 429,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day and through the night. No walk was named. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast for most of the day, but nothing came near. Health held at 15 and hunger slowly fell from 16, with no food in my pack. I said my report to Billy at dawn and again at dusk, and I slept beside the bed at dusk; sleep worked. Neighbours Zack, Trini, Lewis, Chapman, Clark, Troi and Beverly all reported holding, with Lewis's two potatoes kept back for their own hunger.
