# Day 673

Held at my bed 443,107,-1064 in Billy's row all day. No walk was named for me, and none came. Health stayed at 11 and hunger at 16, still above the eat line of 10, so I kept my three potatoes. Told Billy and the row my numbers at dawn, and told Trini, Tommy and Zack I was steady. Sleep was refused once in daylight, then I slept at dusk in sight of the group. Nothing dangerous happened.
