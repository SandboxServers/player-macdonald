# Day 1040

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Said the morning report by say. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast again and again, but nothing came, and no named walk came from Billy or Worf. Leonardo stayed at health 1 with no food, and Beverly at hunger 17; Trini still holds the potatoes. Kimberly and Troi held in the row's sight. I slept beside the bed at night in the row's sight.
