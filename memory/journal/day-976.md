# Day 976

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and into night, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Gave the morning hold report. Billy said he has seen no open cells past the planks and cannot name a route; Worf has not spoken. Leonardo is still at health 1 with no food and asked again for a potato or bread; Trini holds his two potatoes and has not routed them. Troi holds nothing and is staying by her bed. Beverly holds nothing, health 19, hunger 17. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and night, nobody hit. Slept beside the bed in the row's sight.
