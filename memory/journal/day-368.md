# Day 368

Hunger stayed at 16 all day and health held at 13, so I did not eat. I stayed at the white beds waiting for Billy's cow walk call; it never came. The field is still not planted because the ground here is cobblestone and the till is refused. Laforge offered 6 dirt for the wheat, so I can plant once I take it. Clark is leading the water route and Riker went with him. Zack, Trini and the others bedded down in sight of the group. I slept in bed 401,107,-1079 at dusk.
