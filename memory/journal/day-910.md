# Day 910

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all night, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, no food. Billy took the bed at 415 for the night and said so to Leonardo; Bcrusher went to his bed at 443,-1037. Arrows were drawn to the southeast twice in the night, but nothing came near. No walk was named. I said aloud that I was holding and sleeping beside my bed in the row's sight. Slept at dusk.
