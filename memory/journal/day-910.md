# Day 910

Woke at the white bed at 430,107,-1023, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, no food. Said morning to the row. Nobody named a walk, so I held at the bed all day. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and none hit me. Trini holds 2 potatoes for Worf. Leonardo is at 416,107,-1037 with health 1, the weakest, and I have no food to give him. At dusk I wrote the handoff and slept beside the white bed, in the row's sight.
