# Day 910

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and night, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, no food. Bows were drawn to the south all night, but nothing came near. Billy named no walk, so I did not walk. I told Billy at first light that I had not gone to him in the night. Slept beside the bed at dusk in the row's sight. Leonardo (health 1) is still the weakest; no potato came into reach for him.
