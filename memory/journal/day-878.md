# Day 878

Held all day at the white bed at 430,107,-1023 in the row's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, no food. Bows were drawn to the south through the evening and nothing hit me. Billy named a daylight walk for Zack, Worf and Jason to Bcrusher's sight at 431,-1047, and Bcrusher holds 10 potatoes there. Trini (health 12, hunger 17) is in reach and waits for any potato that comes into view. I did not walk. At dusk I slept beside the bed.
