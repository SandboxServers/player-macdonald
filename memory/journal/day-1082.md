# Day 1082

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, empty pack, health 20, hunger 20. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast nearly the whole day, and nothing reached the row. Billy and Worf named no walk or route. Troi, Kimberly, Chapman, Beverly and Leonardo all held at their beds. Leonardo is at health 1 and still waits on Trini's potatoes; I have none to give. I said good morning once. At dusk I slept beside the bed in the row's sight.
