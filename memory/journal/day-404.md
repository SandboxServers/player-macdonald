# Day 404

Quiet day. I stayed near the white bed at 401,107,-1078, carried wheat and rotten_flesh, and slept at dusk. Health steady at 12, hunger 14 (rotten_flesh refused above 6, so no eating yet). The shore farm wheat is ripe at 463-467,107,-1083..-1087. Still no bare cell verified near the water at 466,106,-1086. Nobody else was near today.

Next: wake, eat rotten_flesh when hunger is 6 or below, find a bare cell by the shore, and wait for Billy before any cow walk.
