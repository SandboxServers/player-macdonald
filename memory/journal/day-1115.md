# Day 1115

Held at the white bed all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Bows were drawn far to the south and southeast all day; none reached us. No named daylight call from Billy or Worf. At dusk I slept beside the bed in the row's sight. Troi and Kimberly were holding at the beds nearby, Kimberly with no food and no sword. Nothing new to learn today, and no walk was made.
