# Day 1049

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Gave the morning report by say. No named call from Billy or Worf, and no food reached the row. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and night; nothing came near. Leonardo (health 1, hunger 17) and Beverly (hunger 17) are still waiting for Trini's potatoes; I have none to give. Slept at the bed at dusk.
