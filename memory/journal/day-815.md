# Day 815

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1051 all day with no walk named. Health 15, hunger 16, 2 potatoes, sword in hand. Billy's row was quiet: Clark, Jason, Kimberly, Troi, Laforge and Zack all holding at their beds. Beverly (health 3.3, hunger 16, nothing held) called from 430,107,-1037 for one potato, but she never came into my sight, so I could not give it and did not walk to her. I said so aloud and wrote it down. Slept at dusk beside the bed in the group's sight.
