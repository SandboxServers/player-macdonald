# Day 815

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1051 all day with no walk named. Health 15, hunger 16, 2 potatoes, sword in hand. Beverly (health 3) stayed at 430,107,-1037 asking for a potato; I offered one as soon as she came into my sight, but she never did, and I did not walk while the bow sounded southwest. Riker (hunger 17) has no food; Lewis holds his rotten flesh. Worf has no food and asked that the potatoes go to Beverly and Leonardo first. Slept at dusk beside the bed. Nothing new learned; I am still waiting for Billy's word.
