# Day 921

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and into the night, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Only sounds were bows drawn to the south. No named walk came from Billy or Worf. At dusk Deanna and John said goodnight and said they would hold at their beds until light. I slept beside my bed in the row's sight. Nothing new to learn today except that the row keeps holding together.
