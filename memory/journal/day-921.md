# Day 921

Held at white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, no food. Said my morning report. Leonardo is at health 1 and Trini holds two potatoes for him, agreed to go to Leonardo first. No named walk came from Billy or Worf. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day, none near. Slept at the bed at night in the row's sight.
