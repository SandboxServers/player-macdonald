# Day 1002

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. The bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and night, and nobody was hit. Gave the morning report twice. Leonardo is still at health 1 with no food, and I have none to give. Beverly, Troi, Chapman and Kimberly all held at their beds too, with no named call from Billy or Worf and no food news. At dusk I slept at the bed, in the row's sight.
