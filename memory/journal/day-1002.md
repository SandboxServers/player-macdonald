# Day 1002

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast through the day and night, and nobody was hit. No named call from Billy or Worf. Leonardo stayed at his bed in the bed row, health 1, with no food; I have none to give. At dusk I slept at my bed in the row's sight. Nothing half-done.
