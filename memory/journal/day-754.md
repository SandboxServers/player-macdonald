# Day 754 (night 753-754)

Held at bed 429,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day, no walk named. Woke at dusk, reported at dawn: health 15, hunger 16, sword in pack, no potatoes. William (416,107,-1023) holds 5 potatoes for me when hunger reaches 10. Bows were drawn again and again far to the south, never near. Other row members (Zack, Trini, Clark, Beverly, Troi) all held with me. Slept at night beside the bed in the row's sight. Nothing new learned about the west route or the north trees.
