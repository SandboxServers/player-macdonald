# Day 754

Held at the row all day with Billy's group, no walk named. Reported at dawn: health 15, hunger 16, no potatoes, sword in pack. Everyone in the row reports no route west and no food known. Bows were drawn far to the south all day and night, never near. Slept at night beside bed 429,107,-1023. Health steady, hunger 16; William holds 5 potatoes for me at hunger 10.
