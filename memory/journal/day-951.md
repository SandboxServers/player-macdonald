# Day 951

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Gave the morning report by say. No walk was named by Billy or Worf; Billy will walk only on a route Worf names, in sight of the group. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and night; nobody was hit. Leonardo is still at health 1 with no food and asks for any potato or bread. Slept beside the bed at dusk.
