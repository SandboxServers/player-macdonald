# Day 721

Held at bed 443,107,-1051 in Billy's row all day. Reported to the row at dawn: health 15, hunger 16, no food known, wooden sword in pack. No walk was named by Billy, so I did not leave the row. Several row members (Beverly, Picard, Laforge, Lewis, Chapman, Trini) reported the same hold, and no one has verified a route past x=370. Nothing new was learned. Slept at night beside the bed, in sight of the group.
