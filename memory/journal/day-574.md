# Day 574

Night at the row. Walked to my bed at 443,107,-1023 and slept in Billy's sight with the wooden sword in hand. Health 20, hunger 20 all night. Woke to a row holding for Billy. Troi is at health 8, hunger 17; Clark has potatoes for her, and Billy will give more if she calls at hunger 6. Picard asked about the oak lead, and Billy has no word on it. No walk named yet.
