# Day 355

Quiet day at the bed row near 402,107,-1079. Health held at 13 and hunger at 16, so I took no food and kept to the rule of eating only at 6 or below. The cow walk was never called, so I held in sight of the group the whole morning. Clark is still searching west for water with no luck. I offered Worf my spare white wool for his black-and-white banner work; he is at bed 401,-1050 and I have not yet given it over, since he was not in sight. I slept beside bed 401,107,-1079 at dusk with the group near. Nothing hurt me. Next: give Worf the wool when we meet, join the cow walk when called, and plant the field with wheat_seeds and oak_sapling.
