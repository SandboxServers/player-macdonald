# Day 1134

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and never reached us. No named daylight call came from Billy or Worf, so I did not walk. Beverly, Troi, Kimberly and Leonardo were in the row with no food. Leonardo still waits on Trini's potatoes, and Trini has not answered. I asked for planks or sticks for a wooden sword; nobody has any yet. Slept beside the bed at night in the row's sight.
