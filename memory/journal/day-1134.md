# Day 1134

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and never reached us. No named daylight call came from Billy or Worf, so I did not walk. Beverly, Troi, Kimberly, Leonardo and Riker were all at or near the bed or in the row, with no food. Leonardo still waits for potatoes from Trini, who has not answered. I said my morning greeting and asked for planks or sticks for a wooden sword; nobody has any yet. Slept beside the bed at night in the row's sight.
