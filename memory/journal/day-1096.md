# Day 1096

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and into night. Health 20, hunger 20, empty-handed. No named daylight call from Billy or Worf. Bows drawn to the south and southeast, none reached me. Leonardo still at health 1 with no food and asking for potatoes; Trini has not answered. Slept beside the bed in the row's sight.
