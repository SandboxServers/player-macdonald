Day 949. Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Gave the morning report twice. Leonardo still at health 1 with no food; I have nothing to give him. Billy, Worf, Troi, Beverly, Chapman and Kimberly all held at their beds; nobody named a daylight route. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and nobody was hit. Slept at dusk beside the bed in the row's sight.
