# Day 1055

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, pack empty. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast through the afternoon and dusk, but nothing reached the row. No named call from Billy or Worf. At dusk Bcrusher said he was sleeping at his bed in the row with health 19 and hunger 17. I slept beside my bed in the row's sight.
