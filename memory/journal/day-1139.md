# Day 1139

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, in the row's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No named call from Billy or Worf, so no walk and no bed trip. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and none reached us. Troi, Kimberly, Beverly, Leonardo and Trini spoke in the row; Beverly and Troi are still hungry with no food. I said good morning and asked for a named call and for planks or sticks. Slept beside the bed at dusk.
