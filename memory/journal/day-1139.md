# Day 1139

Held at the white bed all day at 430,107,-1023 in the row's sight. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast again and again, but none reached us. Leonardo, Kimberly, Beverly and Troi all stayed at the beds with no food and no wood to spare. No named call came from Billy or Worf, so no walk. I said once that I need planks or sticks for a wooden sword, and nobody answered that yet. At dusk I slept beside the bed; health 20, hunger 20, still unarmed, pack empty.
