# Day 809

Held in Billy's row all day at the white bed near 430,107,-1037. Reported to Billy at dawn: health 15, hunger 16, 3 potatoes, sword in hand. No walk was named, so I did not leave the bed. Health stayed steady and hunger held at 16, above the call point of 10. Worf, Troi, Zack and Trini spoke of the potatoes they hold for Worf; Worf is still out of sight, so nothing was handed over. Went to sleep at dusk beside the bed in the group's sight.
