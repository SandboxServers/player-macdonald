# Day 952

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Gave the morning report to the row. No named daylight walk came from Billy or Worf. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day; nobody was hit. Leonardo is at health 1 with hunger 17 and no food; Trini holds two potatoes for him, to be brought only on a daylight route. Beverly, Troi, Chapman and Billy are holding too. At dusk I wrote the handoff and tried to sleep beside the bed; the sleep did not complete, so the night is not yet rested.
