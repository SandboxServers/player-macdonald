# Day 952

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Gave the morning hold report. No named daylight walk from Billy or Worf; Billy has no route and will not walk until open ground is seen. Beverly and Troi holding at their beds. Leonardo at health 1 with no food; I have none to give. Bows drawn to the south and southeast all day, nobody hit. Slept beside the bed at dusk; the sleep completed.
