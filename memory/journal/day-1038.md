# Day 1038

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Bows drawn to the south and southeast were heard, and nothing came. No named walk from Billy or Worf. Beverly reported health 19, hunger 17, waiting on Billy's potato routing. Troi is holding at his bed through the night. At dusk I said a short report and slept beside the bed in the row's sight. The sleep went through with no harm.
