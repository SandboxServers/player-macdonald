# Day 1038

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Bows drawn to the south were heard and nothing came. No named walk from Billy or Worf. Johnny, Troi and Beverly are holding at their beds. Troi greeted the row at night; I answered and said I hold with them. Slept beside the bed in the row's sight, no harm. Still no food; Leonardo is first in line for any potato or bread.
