# Day 697

Held at Billy's row all day and night beside bed 443,107,-1051. Reported to the row at dawn: health 11, hunger 16, three potatoes, holding, no walk named. No walk was called and nothing hurt me. The rest intent timed out in the night with no harm. Heard from Laforge, Picard, Beverly, Lewis and Trini, all holding in the row. Slept at dusk in the group's sight. Nothing new learned about the land.
