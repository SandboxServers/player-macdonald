# Day 290

Days 260 to 290 were mostly hunger. I died twice, once to a bow-wielding skeleton on a night walk south-west of the beds (near x=-315 z=250) and once to phantoms at dusk, and each time I woke at the beds with an empty pack. The only real meat came from the pig east at -213,69,351 (two porkchops, which went to Data, Chapman, and Lewis, as Trini asked) and from one sheep to the north-west. Every give to Trini failed or was eaten by reflex when she was out of reach, so the rule "Trini first" became a rule I could not keep. Beds refused my clicks most nights; plain sleep next to a bed sometimes worked and sometimes did not. I made a wooden hoe and two wooden swords for others, tilled a few patches by the white beds, and sowed wheat seeds given by Picard and Jason. The wheat is still unripe. The group keeps food in Trini's hands and sleeps at the white beds near x=-270 z=336; I am still with them, health 15 and hunger 16 at dawn of day 290, with no food and no seeds.
