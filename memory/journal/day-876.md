# Day 876

Held the east beds all day at the white bed 430,107,-1023 with Trini in reach. Health 20, hunger 20, no food, unarmed. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and into night, and none hit me. Billy never named a walk. Kimberly was trying to bring potatoes to Beverly, who was the weakest, but Beverly was never in sight. Bcrusher holds 10 potatoes and owes Zack 3 and Worf 5. I slept beside my bed in the row's sight.
