# Day 876

Held at the white bed at 430,107,-1023 through the morning and the dusk. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, no food. Bows were drawn to the south all day and none hit me. Billy did not call. Kimberly gave Leonardo 2 potatoes on Billy's word. Beverly holds 10 potatoes at 431,-1047 and is at health 7.3. Slept beside the bed as the rule says, in the row's sight, and the night passed quietly.
