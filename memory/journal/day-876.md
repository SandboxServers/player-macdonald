# Day 876

Held all day at the white bed at 430,107,-1023 in the row's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, no food. Bows were drawn to the south and east all day and none hit me. Trini was in reach at night. Billy gave no walk. I said goodnight to the row and slept beside the bed. Nothing new was learned today; the hold is still the safest course.
