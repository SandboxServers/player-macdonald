# Day 1005

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Gave the morning report to the row. No named daylight walk came from Billy or Worf, so I did not walk. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all night and nobody was hit. Leonardo is at health 1 and hungry with no food; Trini holds the potatoes. I had nothing to hand over. Slept at the bed at night beside the row.
