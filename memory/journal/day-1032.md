# Day 1032

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. No named walk from Billy or Worf and no food came into the row. Leonardo is still at health 1 with no potato; I have none to give. Kimberly and Chapman hold in the row's sight. Slept beside the bed at night in the row's sight. Nothing new learned today; the row is waiting on Billy or Worf.
