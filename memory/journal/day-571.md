# Day 571

Quiet day at the row. Waited at dusk beside my bed at 443,107,-1036; sleep failed at first ("not night") and worked once dark. Armed with the wooden sword, health 20, hunger 20, no walks. Lewis, Leonardo, Kimberly, Tommy and Clark were near and holding in Billy's sight. Clark ate a potato as Billy named. No new wood or food found; the north trip was not mine.
