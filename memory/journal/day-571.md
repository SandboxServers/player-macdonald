# Day 571

Woke in the row at 443,107,-1021, health 20, hunger 20, sword in hand. Billy named no walks all day; he gave Zack a spare wooden sword. Bows were drawn often to the southwest and west, and a phantom attacked Troi from the east while she was unarmed. Billy plans a potato-field walk at 462-465,107,-1068 once he names it. I held at the row and slept at dusk beside the bed at 443,107,-1022, in Billy's sight.
