# Day 1144

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast several times; none reached us. No named call from Billy or Worf, so no walk. Still unarmed and nobody has spare planks or sticks. Health 20, hunger 20. At dusk Troi and Beverly said goodnight in the row, and I slept beside the bed in the row's sight.
