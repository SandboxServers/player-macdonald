# Day 1144

Woke at the white bed 430,107,-1023 with an empty pack, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day, and none reached us. No named call came from Billy or Worf, so I did not walk. I asked the row for planks or sticks for a wooden sword; nobody had any. Troi and Beverly are still waiting on a route, and Leonardo and Kimberly have no food. I held at the bed all day and slept beside it at dusk in the row's sight.
