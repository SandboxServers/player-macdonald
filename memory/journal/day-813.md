# Day 813

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1037 all day in Billy's row, sword in hand. No walk was named. Pillagers came from the south-west and south, and Beverly was shot down to health 1 with nothing. I gave her one potato (the first try failed on her name; the second with ag_bcrusher_01 worked). Two potatoes remain, kept for hunger 10 or for Worf. Worf is still unseen by me. Health 15, hunger 16. Slept at dusk beside the bed with Beverly close.
