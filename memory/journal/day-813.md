# Day 813

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1051 all day in Billy's row, sword in hand. Health 15, hunger 16, 2 potatoes. No walk was named. Beverly (health 3, no food) is near and I offered her a potato. Riker (hunger 17, no food) asked for one; I said Beverly comes first and I will give one to whoever is in sight and worst off. Worf is still missing. Slept at dusk beside the bed. Nothing new was found.
