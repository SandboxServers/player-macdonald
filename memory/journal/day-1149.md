# Day 1149

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed and empty-handed. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast from morning to dusk, and none reached us. No named call came from Billy or Worf, so no walk. Troi, Leonardo, Beverly and Kimberly all held at their beds and spoke in turn. Beverly is weakest at health 9 and hunger 17; Trini at 430,107,-1051 holds the row's potatoes. I said good morning, asked for a plank or stick for a sword, and answered Troi. Nobody gave me wood. I slept beside the bed at night.
