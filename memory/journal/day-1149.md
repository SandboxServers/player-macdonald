# Day 1149

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day in the row's sight. Bows drawn to the south, none reached us. No named call from Billy or Worf, so no walk. Still unarmed, pack empty, health 20, hunger 20. Slept beside the bed at night. Leonardo and Beverly are at their beds, waiting for a named call. Nothing new learned today.
