# Day 1027

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 through the night and the morning. Said the morning report at first light: unarmed, holding, health 20, hunger 20. No named walk came from Billy or Worf, and no food came into the row. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and night, and nothing came near. Leonardo is at health 1 at bed 416,107,-1023 asking for a potato. Troi, Beverly, Kimberly and Chapman are holding at their beds with empty packs. At dusk I slept beside my bed.
