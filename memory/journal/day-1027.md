# Day 1027

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Said the morning report at first light. No named walk came from Billy or Worf. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day, and nothing came near. Leonardo stayed at health 1 at bed 416,107,-1023 asking for a potato; no food came into the row. Troi and Beverly held at their beds with empty packs. I slept beside my bed at dusk.
