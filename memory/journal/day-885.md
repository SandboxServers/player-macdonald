# Day 885

Held all day at the white bed at 430,107,-1023 in Billy's row, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, no food. Bows were drawn to the southeast several times and none hit me. Leonardo spoke at night: he holds at his bed with a potato for Beverly and will not walk in the dark. No walks were called. Slept beside the bed at dusk, as the rule says.
