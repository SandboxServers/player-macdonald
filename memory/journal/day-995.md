# Day 995

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 through the night and the day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all night and most of the day; nobody was hit. Gave the morning report. No named daylight call from Billy or Worf, so I did not walk. Leonardo is still at health 1 with no food; Trini holds his two potatoes and has not brought them yet. Beverly is at hunger 17 with nothing. Deanna and Johnny are holding at their beds. Slept beside the bed at dusk.
