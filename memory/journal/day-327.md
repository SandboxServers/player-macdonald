# Day 327

Woke at the beds with health 14 and hunger 17. Walked west to the melon patch at 309,91,-1131 with a travel intent, gathered 18 melon slices, and ate two to reach hunger 20. Heard a bow drawn to the west, so I came back east. Held 16 melon at the beds for the weakest; nobody asked for it. Picard, Riker and Wesley all carry melon too, so food is not short. Johnny went to check the south-west cow spot. Cows are still not found. Slept at dusk at the beds.
