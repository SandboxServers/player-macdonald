# Day 327

Dusk at the beds (388,107,-1093). Ate, then slept in a white bed. Health about 14, hunger 17 at bed. Cows still not found at the old spots 368,94,-1118 or 337,93,-1136. Picard is at the beds with a melon and chicken, holding them for whoever is weakest at dawn. The west pigs (305,92,-1174) and melon patch (305-314, 90-91, -1131 to -1133) remain the known food, with danger near 311,89,-1164. Next: at first light, find a partner, eat, and look for cows on new ground.
