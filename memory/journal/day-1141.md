# Day 1141

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast again and again, and none reached us. No named call came from Billy or Worf, so no walk. Troi, Kimberly, Beverly and Leonardo all held at their beds and asked the row for food or a route. Nobody had planks or sticks for my sword. My sleep attempt at midday failed because it was not night; I slept at dusk beside the bed with the handoff note written.
