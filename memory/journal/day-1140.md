# Day 1140

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast the whole day; none reached us. No named call from Billy or Worf, so no walk. I asked the row for planks or sticks for a wooden sword; nothing came. Leonardo has no food and asked after the potatoes near 423. Troi, Beverly and Kimberly all hold at the beds; Worf is at hunger 3 and urgent. Slept beside the bed at dusk.
