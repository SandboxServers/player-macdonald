# Day 1004

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Told Leonardo again I have no food. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast through the day and into the night; nobody was hit. Beverly and Troi reported empty packs; Billy's potato routing and Worf's daylight call were still not named. Slept at the bed at night, nothing half-done.
