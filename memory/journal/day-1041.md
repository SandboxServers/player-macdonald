# Day 1041

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and night, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, no food. Gave the morning report by say. Bows were drawn to the south many times, but nothing came near. No named call from Billy or Worf. Leonardo is at health 1 and still waiting for food; Trini holds the potatoes, and Deanna asked that they go to Leonardo first. Slept beside the bed in the row's sight.
