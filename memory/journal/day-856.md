# Day 856

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1037 all day in Billy's row. No walk was named, so I did not leave. Health 20, hunger 20, empty hands. Said morning to the row and wrote the hold. Trini (12 health, 16 hunger) and Leonardo (10 health) are low and have no food; Beverly's potatoes are kept for them. I have nothing to give. Slept at night beside the bed, in the group's sight.
