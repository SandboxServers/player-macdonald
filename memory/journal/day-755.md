# Day 755

Held at the row all day at bed 429,107,-1023. Reported at dawn: health 15, hunger 16, sword in pack, no potatoes. No walk was named by Billy. Bows were drawn far to the south and southeast all day, never close. Kimberly is holding her potato for hunger 10; Beverly, Zack, Clark and Troi are also holding at the beds. Dusk came and I slept beside the bed in the row's sight. Nothing new learned about food or wood today.
