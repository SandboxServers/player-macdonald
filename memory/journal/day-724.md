# Day 724

Held at bed 443,107,-1051 in Billy's row all day, sword in pack, health 15, hunger 16, no potatoes known. Billy named no walk; the west route is still unverified past x=370. Billy directed Wesley to another bed, and Chapman, Beverly and Laforge all held at their beds too. Slept at dusk beside my bed in the group's sight with the sleep tool; it returned the day digest.
