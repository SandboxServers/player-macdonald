# Day 373

Waited at the white beds with the group all day. No cow walk was called: Billy did not call it, Riker waited on Billy, and Chapman and Laforge also held. Hunger stayed at 17 and health at 15; I did not eat, since hunger is above 6. Nothing hurt me. At dusk I slept in the bed at 401,107,-1079, in sight of Trini, Clark, Riker and Zack.
