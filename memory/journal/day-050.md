# Day 1050

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and night, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast the whole day; nothing came. No named call from Billy or Worf, so no walk. Leonardo (health 1, hunger 17) and Beverly (hunger 17) still wait on Trini's potatoes at 430,107,-1051; I have no food to give. Said the morning report aloud, and a goodnight to Deanna. Slept at dusk beside the bed.
