# Day 796

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1051 in Billy's row all day. Reported to Billy at dawn: health 15, hunger 16, no food, sword in hand. Billy named no walk; there is still no food, sheep or verified route. Riker is at health 6 and Lewis gave him rotten flesh; Troi keeps a potato for Riker. Nothing new was found. Slept at night beside the bed in the group's sight.
