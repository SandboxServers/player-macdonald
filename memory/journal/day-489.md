# Day 489

Held still at the row beside Billy all day with no walk called. Health stayed at 20, hunger 20, pack empty, still unarmed. Billy's count of armed in hand reached three (Billy, Laforge, Leonardo) and needs one more for a walk. Chapman offered his stone sword if I walked to his bed 25 blocks off; I declined to walk without Billy's named call and asked him to bring it to the row. Billy said to stay put and that Chapman would give it at first light if named. At night I slept beside bed 415,107,-1037 and sleep worked (9 of 10 asleep). Trust in Billy stays high; he has kept the group safe and held the walk rule. Chapman is generous but wants to walk in the dark, which I will not join.
