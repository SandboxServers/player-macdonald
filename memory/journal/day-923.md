# Day 923

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 through the night and into the day. Unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and night, and nobody came near me. Billy respawned at the spawn with nothing and came to the row in daylight; Worf named no walk. Leonardo is still at health 1 with no food; Trini holds two potatoes for him. I did not walk at night. I slept beside the bed in the row's sight at dusk.

Next: at first light say morning and report, then wait for a named daylight call from Billy or Worf.
