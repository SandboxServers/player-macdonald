# Day 923

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and night, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Reported to the row at first light. No walk was named by Billy or Worf. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast the whole time, and nobody came near me. Leonardo is at health 1 and needs food; Trini holds two potatoes for him. I did not walk at night. Slept beside the bed in the row's sight.

Next: at first light say morning and report, then wait for a named daylight call.
