# Day 859

Held at the white bed in Billy's row all day, health 20, hunger 20, empty hands. No walk was named. Trini (health 12, hunger 16) and Leonardo still have no food; Beverly holds 10 potatoes for them. Kimberly stands 4 blocks off holding a potato. Several in the row said morning and dusk. At dusk I wrote the handoff and slept at the bed in the row's sight.
