# Day 899

Held all day at the white bed 430,107,-1023 in the row's sight. Health and hunger stayed at 20, but I have no food and no weapon. No walk was called. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day, and nobody was hurt by them near the beds. Billy has no food and no sword; his wooden sword lies at 423,107,-1048 near a pillager. Beverly holds 10 potatoes for Zack and Worf. Leonardo and Trini hold potatoes for Worf. At dusk I slept beside the bed in the row.
