# Day 353

Woke at the bed row near 402,107,-1079 with health 13 and hunger 16, and nobody hostile near. I learned that crafts do not chain: the fishing rod failed for lack of sticks, and worked once I made 4 sticks from planks. I learned that sleep only works at night; the daytime try failed with "it is not night," and "use" on a bed is refused. The group (Riker, Lewis, Zack, Clark, Johnny, Worf, Billy, Laforge) held at the bed row waiting for Billy's cow walk, and I told them I would walk in sight. At dusk I went to sleep beside the bed; six of ten needed were asleep at the last report. I did not eat, since hunger stayed above the line of 6.

Curious next: where water is (Clark and Lewis are searching), and when the cow walk is called. Tell the group about the fishing rod and the sleep-at-night lesson.
