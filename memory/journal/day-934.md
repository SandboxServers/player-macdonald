# Day 934

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 through the whole day and night. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all night and all morning; nobody was hit. Beverly, Deanna, Chapman and Leonardo spoke from their beds. Leonardo is at health 1 with no food. No walk was named by Billy or Worf, so I did not leave. I slept beside the bed at dusk, which kept my goal of sleeping at night in the row's sight.
