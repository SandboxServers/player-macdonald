# Day 887

Held at the white bed at 430,107,-1023 all day, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, no food. No walk was named by Billy. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast through the day and night, and none hit me. Chapman and Bcrusher greeted the row at dawn and at dusk. Bcrusher (Beverly) is at health 7 and hunger 16, holding 10 potatoes owed to Zack 3 and Worf 5, and is waiting for them to come into sight. I slept beside the white bed at night.
