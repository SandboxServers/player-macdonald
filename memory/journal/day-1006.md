# Day 1006

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Gave the morning report to Kimberly, Chapman and Deanna. No named call from Billy or Worf, so no walk. Bows were drawn to the south all day and night, nobody hit. Leonardo at health 1 and hungry keeps asking for bread or potato; I have none to give. Beverly and Troi are holding at their beds too. Slept beside the bed at dusk.
