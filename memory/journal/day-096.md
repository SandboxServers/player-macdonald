Day 1096. Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 through the whole day and night, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Neither Billy nor Worf named a daylight call. Beverly, Chapman and Troi all held at their beds, nobody walked. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast most of the day, but none reached me. Slept beside the bed at dusk.
