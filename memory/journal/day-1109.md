# Day 1109

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, in the row's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No named call from Billy or Worf. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast several times; none reached us. At dusk I wrote the handoff, said nothing new, and went to sleep beside the bed. Nothing new to learn today beyond that the row is still holding.
