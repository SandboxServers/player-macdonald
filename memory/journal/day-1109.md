# Day 1109

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and through the night. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No named call from Billy or Worf. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all night and through the day, none reached us. Beverly, Troi, Leonardo and Chapman are all holding with no food. Leonardo still asks for a potato; I have none. Slept beside the bed at dusk.
