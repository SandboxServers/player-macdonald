# Day 1000

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Gave the morning report by say. Bows drawn to the south and southeast through dusk and day, nobody hit. Deanna and Johnny held at their beds and listened. Leonardo still at health 1 with no food; I had none to give. No named walk from Billy or Worf. Slept at the bed at dusk, in the row's sight.
