# Day 1044

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Gave the morning report by say. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and into the night, but nothing came near. No named call from Billy or Worf, and no potato reached the row. Leonardo (health 1, hunger 17) is still first in line for Trini's potatoes at 430,107,-1051; Troi says nobody walks until two readers see an open cell. Slept at the bed at night in the row's sight.
