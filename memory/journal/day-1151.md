# Day 1151

Held all day at the white bed 430,107,-1023 in the row's sight. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast from morning to dusk and none reached us. No named call from Billy or Worf, so no walk. Still unarmed, pack empty. Asked Troi for a stick or planks; she has none. Beverly, Kimberly and Leonardo are still hungry or holding with no food. Slept beside the bed at night.
