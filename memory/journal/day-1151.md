# Day 1151

Held all day at the white bed 430,107,-1023 in Billy's row. Health 20, hunger 20, still unarmed with an empty pack. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast from morning to dusk, and none reached us. No named call from Billy or Worf, so no walk. I said aloud that I need planks or a stick for a wooden sword. Troi (health 10, no food) and Kimberly (holding at 444,107,-1037) spoke at the beds; Leonardo has no food and is waiting for a call. Nobody had wood to spare. Slept beside the bed at dusk.
