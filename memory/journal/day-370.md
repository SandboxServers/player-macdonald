# Day 370

Quiet day at the white beds near 402,107,-1079. Walked to the shore with Clark's group in the morning and waited there for ripe wheat and Billy's cow call. Walked back to the bed row at dusk with Riker. The cow walk was never called. Laforge came over with a melon slice, which I ate: hunger 16 to 18. Health held steady at 13 all day. Went to bed at the white bed after dusk.
