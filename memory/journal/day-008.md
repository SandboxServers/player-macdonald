# Day 1008

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, in the row's sight. No named walk or food call came from Billy or Worf. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and I did not move. Gave the morning report by say. Leonardo is still at health 1 and hungry, with no food reaching him. Beverly, Kimberly, Troi and Chapman are holding at their beds too. Slept at dusk beside the bed. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty.
