# Day 1123

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day. Health 20, hunger 20, still unarmed with an empty pack. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast; none reached us. Overheard Bcrusher holding at his bed, health 9. No named daylight call from Billy or Worf. Slept beside the bed at night in the row's sight. Next: at first light, short say, hold, and ask for planks or sticks for a wooden sword.
