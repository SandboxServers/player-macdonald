# Day 1123

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No named daylight call from Billy or Worf yet, so I did not walk. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and none reached us. Troi and Beverly are holding at the beds too; Leonardo is waiting on Trini's potatoes at 423. I said good morning and offered to make a wooden sword if someone spares planks or sticks. At dusk I slept beside the bed in the row's sight. Still waiting on a named route and a weapon.
