# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023, in the row's sight. Unarmed, empty pack, health 20, hunger 20.
- Day 1108 to dusk 1109: held at the bed. No named call from Billy or Worf. Bows drawn to the south all night and day, none reached us. Beverly, Troi, Leonardo, Chapman holding with no food.
- Next: sleep beside the bed. On waking, hold, and walk only on a named daylight call with four or more armed and a sword of my own. Any potato or bread goes to Leonardo first.
