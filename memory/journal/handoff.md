# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside the white bed, in the row's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed.
- Night of day 1137. Going to sleep beside the bed. No errand running, nothing half-done.
- Bows drawn to the south all day; none reached us. No named call from Billy or Worf yet, so no walk. No planks or sticks yet.
- Next: at first light, short say, hold. Wait for a named call from Billy or Worf. Ask for planks or sticks for a wooden sword. Any potato or bread goes to Leonardo first. Troi, Beverly and Kimberly hold nearby and also need food.
