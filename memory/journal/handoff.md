# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside the white bed, in the row's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, no food.
- Day 900: said morning at first light, held at the bed all day. No walk called by Billy. Bows heard to the south all day, none hit me. Bcrusher holds 10 potatoes for those hungry in his sight.
- Now: night of day 901 start, dim light, sleeping beside the bed in the row.
- Next: at first light say morning, hold at the bed, give a potato to the weakest if one comes into reach. Walk only on Billy's named call.
