# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023, in the row's sight. Unarmed, empty pack, health 20, hunger 20. Not enclosed.
- Day 1080: slept at the bed. Daylight held all of day 1079; bows only, to the south. Nothing reached the row.
- No named daylight call from Billy or Worf yet. No walk taken. No errand running.
- Leonardo (health 1) and Deanna hold with no food. Chapman and Beverly holding in the row.
- Next: at first light, hold at the bed. Walk only on a named daylight call from Billy or Worf, with four or more armed and a sword of my own. Any potato or bread goes to Leonardo first.
