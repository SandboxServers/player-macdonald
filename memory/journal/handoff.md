# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023, in the row's sight. Unarmed, empty pack, health 20, hunger 20. Not enclosed.
- Day 1074 at dusk: holding at the bed all day. Bows heard drawn to the south; nothing reached the row. Leonardo at health 1 with no food; I have nothing to give. No errand running.
- Next: at first light, hold at the bed. Walk only on a named daylight call from Billy or Worf, with four or more armed and a sword of my own. Any potato or bread goes to Leonardo first; ask Trini before carrying anything from 430,107,-1051.
