# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside the white bed. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, no food. In the row's sight.
- Day 887 morning: said morning, held. Billy and Leonardo shared potatoes in daylight; I was not part of it.
- Day 888 dusk: holding at the bed, no walk named. Bows drawn to the south all day, none hit me.
- Errands: none.
- Owed: Beverly holds 10 potatoes for Zack 3 and Worf 5, to be given in daylight.
- Next: at first light say morning, hold, give a potato to the weakest if one comes into reach. Do not walk unarmed or alone.
