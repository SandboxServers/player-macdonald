# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023, in the row's sight. Unarmed, holding, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty.
- Day 1026 night: sleeping beside the bed. Bow sounds to the south all day, nothing came near. No named walk from Billy or Worf. Leonardo still at health 1, no food. Kimberly, Chapman, Beverly, Troi all holding at their beds.
- Next: at first light say the morning report, hold at the bed. Walk only on a named daylight call with four or more armed and a sword of my own. Any food goes to Leonardo first.
