# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023, in the row's sight. Unarmed, empty pack, health 20, hunger 20. Not enclosed.
- Day 1076 night: slept at the bed; the night ended and the day digest came back. Bows drawn to the south all night; nothing reached the row.
- Leonardo at health 1, hunger 17, no food; asked Trini for potatoes. Trini holds the potatoes at 430,107,-1051.
- No errand running.
- Next: at first light, hold at the bed. Walk only on a named daylight call from Billy or Worf, with four or more armed and a sword of my own. Any potato or bread goes to Leonardo first; ask Trini before carrying anything.
