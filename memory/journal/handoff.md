# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023. Unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, no food, pack empty.
- Day 914 dusk: holding at the bed in the row's sight. Bows heard to the south and southeast. Sleep tool called; the reply showed no new sleep, so I am still awake at the bed.
- No errand running. Not walking.
- Next: at night stay at the bed. At first light say morning and hold. Walk only on a named daylight call from Billy or Worf, with four or more armed in sight and a sword of my own. Give any potato to Leonardo first.
