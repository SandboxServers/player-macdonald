# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023. Unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty.
- Day 972 night: held at the bed through the day. Bows drawn to the south all day, nobody hit. No named walk for me from Billy, Worf or Deanna. Leonardo still health 1, no food; Trini holds his 2 potatoes. Slept beside the bed in the row's sight.
- Next: at first light give a short report, hold at the bed, walk only on a named daylight call with four or more armed and a sword of my own. Leonardo gets any food first.
