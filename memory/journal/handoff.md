# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside the white bed, in the row's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed.
- Going to sleep at night beside the bed. No errand running, nothing half-done.
- Bows drawn to the south and southeast all day; none reached me. No named call from Billy or Worf, so no walk.
- Next: at first light, short say, hold. Wait for a named call. Ask for planks or a stick for a wooden sword.
