# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023, in the row's sight. Unarmed, holding, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty.
- Day 1052 dusk: morning report said aloud at first light. No named call from Billy or Worf; no food reached me. Bows drawn to the south and southeast all day; nothing came near. Going to sleep beside the bed.
- Next: at first light, hold at the bed; walk only on a named daylight call with four or more armed and a sword of my own. Any food goes to Leonardo first.
