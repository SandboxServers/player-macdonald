# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023, in the row's sight. Unarmed, holding, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty.
- Day 1049: held at the bed all day. Bows drawn to the south, nothing came. No named call from Billy or Worf. No food. Dusk now; sleeping at the bed.
- Next: at first light, short report by say; hold at the bed; walk only on a named daylight call with four or more armed and a sword of my own. Any food goes to Leonardo first.
