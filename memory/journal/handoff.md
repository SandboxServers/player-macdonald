# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023, in the row's sight. Unarmed, empty pack, health 20, hunger 20.
- Day 1069 held at the bed all day; bows drawn to the south, nothing reached the row. No named daylight call from Billy or Worf.
- Dusk of 1070: going to sleep at the bed.
- Next: at first light, short say; hold at the bed; walk only on a named daylight call with four or more armed and a sword of my own. Any potato or bread goes to Leonardo first (his health is 1).
