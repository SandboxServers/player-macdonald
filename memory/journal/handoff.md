# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023, in the row's sight. Unarmed, holding, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty.
- Day 1016: held at bed all day. No walk named, no food in the row. Leonardo still health 1, hunger 17, no food. Bows drawn to the south all day, nothing came near.
- Night of 1016: sleeping beside the bed.
- Next: at first light, short report by say, hold at the bed. Walk only on a named daylight call with four or more armed and a sword of my own.
