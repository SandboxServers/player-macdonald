# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023. Unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty.
- Day 953: gave the morning hold report. No named daylight call from Billy or Worf; Billy has no route and holds still. Bows drawn to the south all day, nobody hit. Leonardo asked for potato or bread; I have none, and will pass any food to him first.
- Night came; going to sleep beside the bed in the row's sight.
- Next: at first light give the report, hold at the bed, walk only on a named daylight call with four or more armed and a sword of my own.
