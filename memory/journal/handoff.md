# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023, in the row's sight. Unarmed, holding, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty.
- Day 1057: held at the bed all day. Morning report given. No named call from Billy or Worf. Bows drawn to the south all day; nothing reached the row. Slept beside the bed.
- No errand running.
- Next: at waking, short report by say; hold at the bed; walk only on a named daylight call with four or more armed and a sword of my own. Any food goes to Leonardo first.
