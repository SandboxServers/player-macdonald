# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023. Unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty.
- Day 995 to 996. Held at the bed all day. Morning report said. Bows heard drawn to the south and southeast all day, nobody hit. No named call from Billy or Worf.
- Going to sleep at the bed at night, in the row's sight.
- Leonardo at bed 416,107,-1023, health 1, hunger 17, no food. Any food in reach goes to him first.
- Next: at first light, short morning report, hold at the bed. Walk only on a named daylight call with four or more armed and a sword of my own.
