# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023, in the row's sight. Unarmed, empty pack, health 20, hunger 20.
- Dusk of day 1109: still holding at the bed. No named call from Billy or Worf. Bows drawn to the south and southeast; none reached us.
- Next: sleep beside the bed. On waking, hold; walk only on a named daylight call with four or more armed and a sword of my own. Any potato or bread goes to Leonardo first.
