# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023. Unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Holding in the row's sight.
- Day 927: morning and dusk reports said. Bows drawn to the south and southeast all day and night. No walk named by Billy or Worf. Slept at night beside the bed.
- Leonardo at 415,107,-1023, health 1, hunger 17, needs food. Trini holds two potatoes for him and Worf.
- Next: hold. Walk only on a named daylight call from Billy or Worf, with four or more armed and a sword of my own. Give any potato to Leonardo first.
