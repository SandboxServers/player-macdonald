# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023. Unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty.
- Day 933 dusk: holding at the bed, no one hurt. Bows drawn to the south all day, nobody hit.
- Morning report given at first light on day 932 and day 933. No walk named by Billy or Worf. Troi, Beverly, Kimberly, Chapman also holding. Leonardo at health 1, hunger 17, needs food.
