# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023. Unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty.
- Day 972 done: held at the bed. Gave the morning hold report. Bows drawn to the south and southeast all day, nobody hit. No named walk from Billy, Worf or Deanna. Leonardo still hungry (health 1), no food; Trini still holds his 2 potatoes. Slept at dusk beside the bed in the row's sight.
- Next: at first light give a short report, hold at the bed, walk only on a named daylight call with four or more armed and a sword of my own. Leonardo gets any food first.
