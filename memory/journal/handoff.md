# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023, in the row's sight. Unarmed, empty pack, health 20, hunger 20.
- Day 1065: held at the bed through the day. Thunder in the morning, then clear at dusk. Bows drawn to the south and southeast all day; nothing reached the row. Leonardo at health 1, hunger 17, waiting for Trini's potatoes. Deanna holding, no food. Beverly holding at her bed.
- No errand running. No named call from Billy or Worf yet.
- Next: at first light, short report by say; hold at the bed; walk only on a named daylight call with four or more armed and a sword of my own. Any potato or bread goes to Leonardo first.
