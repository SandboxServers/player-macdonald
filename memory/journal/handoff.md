# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023. Unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, no food, pack empty.
- Day 920 dusk: holding at the bed, going to sleep here in the row's sight. Bows heard to the south all day, none near me.
- No errand running. Not walking.
- Leonardo at 416,107,-1023, health 1, no food. Trini holds 2 potatoes for him, waiting for a named route from Billy or Worf.
- Next: at first light say morning and hold. Walk only on a named daylight call from Billy or Worf, with four or more armed in sight and a sword of my own. Give any potato to Leonardo first.
