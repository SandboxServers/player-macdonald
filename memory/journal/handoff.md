# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023. Unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty.
- Day 999, dusk. Morning report given by say. Held at the bed all day. Bows drawn to the south and southeast all day, nobody hit. No named call from Billy or Worf. Leonardo still hungry; I have no food to give.
- Going to sleep at the bed at dusk, in the row's sight.
- Next: at first light, short morning report, hold at the bed. Walk only on a named daylight call with four or more armed and a sword of my own.
