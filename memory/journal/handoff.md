# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023, in the row's sight. Unarmed, empty pack, health 20, hunger 20.
- Day 1102: held all day, no named call from Billy or Worf. Bows sounding south and southeast, none near. Going to sleep at the bed at dusk.
- Leonardo at health 1, no food, asking for potato. Any potato or bread goes to him first.
- Next: at first light, short say, hold at the bed. Walk only on a named daylight call with four or more armed and a sword of my own.
