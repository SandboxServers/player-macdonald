# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023. Unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty.
- Day 949: gave the morning report, held at the bed all day. Slept at night beside the bed in the row's sight. Nobody hit by bows; bows were drawn to the south all day.
- Next: at first light, give the report and hold. Walk only on a named daylight call from Billy or Worf, with four or more armed and a sword of my own.
