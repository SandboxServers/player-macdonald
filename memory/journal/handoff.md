# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside the white bed, in the row's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, no food.
- Day 903: held at the bed all day. Bows heard to the south all day, none hit me. No walk called by Billy.
- Beverly holds 10 potatoes for Zack 3 and Worf 5, in her sight.
- Now: sleeping at the bed at night of day 903.
- Next: at first light say morning, hold at the bed, give a potato to the weakest if one comes into reach. Walk only on Billy's named call.
