# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, at the white bed (spawn). Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed.
- Day 1119: held at the bed all day. No named call from Billy or Worf, no sword or spear, no food. Bows drawn to the south throughout; none reached us.
- Night of 1119: going to sleep beside the bed, in the row's sight. Troi, Beverly and Kimberly are holding at the white beds too.
- Next: at first light, short say, hold. Wait for a named call and a wooden sword or spear. Any potato or bread goes to Leonardo first.
