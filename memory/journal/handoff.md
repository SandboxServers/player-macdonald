# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023, in the row's sight. Unarmed, holding, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty.
- Going to sleep at night, day 1032. Hold intent at bed. No named walk from Billy or Worf, no food in the row. Leonardo at health 1 still needs a potato; none in the row. Bows drawn far to the south all day, nothing near me.
- Next: at first light say the short morning report, hold at the bed. Walk only on a named daylight call with four or more armed and a sword of my own. Any food goes to Leonardo first.
