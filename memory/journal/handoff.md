# Handoff, day 639 dusk

At bed 429,107,-1051 in Billy's row, sword in hand. Health 7, hunger 16, six potatoes. Not in danger. No errand half-done. Billy: Kimberly was hit near 413,-1041, stay close to Trini and Billy tonight; no walks until morning.
