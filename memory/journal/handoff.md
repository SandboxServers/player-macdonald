# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023. Unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty.
- Day 952 dusk: holding at the bed, no walk named. Bows drawn to the south and southeast all day; nobody hit.
- Leonardo at health 1, hunger 17, no food. Trini holds two potatoes for him; they go to him only on a daylight route.
- Next: at first light give the report, hold at the bed, walk only on a named daylight call from Billy or Worf, with four or more armed and a sword of my own.
