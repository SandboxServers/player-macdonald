# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023. Unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty.
- Day 940: held at the bed all day, morning report given. Bows drawn to the south all day; nobody hit.
- Leonardo at health 1, hunger 17, no food. Beverly asked about potato routing; I have nothing.
- No daylight walk named for me yet. Billy said Worf may name a potato route if open air shows.
- Next: at first light, give the report and hold. Walk only on a named daylight call with four or more armed and a sword of my own.
