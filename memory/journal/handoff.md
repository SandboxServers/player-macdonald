# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023, in the row's sight. Unarmed, empty pack, health 20, hunger 20.
- Day 1106: still holding. No named call from Billy or Worf. Bows drawn to the south and southeast, none near. Leonardo at health 1 with no food; I have none to give.
- Next: hold at the bed. Sleep beside it at night. Walk only on a named daylight call with four or more armed and a sword of my own. Any potato or bread goes to Leonardo first.
