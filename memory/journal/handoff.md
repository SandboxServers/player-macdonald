# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023. Unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty.
- Night 977: held at the bed, slept beside it in the row's sight. Bows heard to the south all night, nobody hit.
- Leonardo health 1, hunger 17, no food; Trini holds his 2 potatoes. Beverly health 19, hunger 17, holding nothing.
- Next: at first light, short report, hold at the bed. Walk only on a named daylight call with four or more armed and a sword of my own. Leonardo gets any food first.
