# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, at the white bed (spawn). Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed.
- Day 1117: held at the bed through the day. Greeted the row at morning. Bows drawn to the south all day; none reached us. No named call from Billy or Worf yet.
- Night of 1117: going to sleep beside the bed, in the row's sight. Beverly and Kimberly are also holding at the white bed.
- Next: at first light, short say, hold. Wait for a named call and a wooden sword or spear. Any potato or bread goes to Leonardo first.
