# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023, in the row's sight. Unarmed, empty pack, health 20, hunger 20.
- Day 1073 dusk: held at the bed all day. No named daylight call from Billy or Worf. Bows drawn to the south all day, nothing reached the row. Leonardo at health 1, no food, still waiting on Trini. Chapman, Beverly and Troi holding too.
- Going to sleep beside the bed.
- Next: at first light, hold at the bed; walk only on a named daylight call with four or more armed and a sword of my own. Any potato or bread goes to Leonardo first.
