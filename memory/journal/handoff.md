# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023. Unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Slept at dusk of day 929 in the row's sight.
- Day 928 night held at the bed; morning report given to the row. Bows drawn to the south and southeast all night and day, no one hurt.
- No walk named by Billy or Worf. Leonardo at health 1, hunger 17, needs food; Trini holds two potatoes for him.
- Next: at first light, morning report, hold. Walk only on a named daylight call from Billy or Worf with four or more armed and a sword of my own. Give any potato to Leonardo first.
