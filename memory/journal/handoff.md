# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023, in the row's sight. Unarmed, holding, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty.
- Day 1062-1063: held at the bed through day and night. Bows drawn to the south and southeast all day and night; nothing reached the row. Leonardo at 416,107,-1023 asks for food, none here. Beverly at 443,107,-1037 holding, health 19, hunger 17.
- No errand running. No named call from Billy or Worf yet.
- Next: at dawn, hold at the bed; walk only on a named daylight call with four or more armed and a sword of my own. Any potato or bread goes to Leonardo first.
