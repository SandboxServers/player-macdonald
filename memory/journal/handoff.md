# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023, in the row's sight. Unarmed, holding, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty.
- Day 1042: held at the bed through the day and night. Bows drawn to the south all night, nothing came. No food. No named call from Billy or Worf. Beverly (hunger 17) sleeping at her bed in the row's sight.
- Next: at first light, hold at the bed; short report by say. Walk only on a named daylight call with four or more armed and a sword of my own. Any food goes to Leonardo first.
