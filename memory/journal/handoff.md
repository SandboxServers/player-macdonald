# Handoff, day 810 (night)

At bed 430,107,-1037, in Billy's row sight. Sword in hand. Health 15, hunger 16, 3 potatoes held. No errand half-done, no walk named. Reported to Billy at dawn; Billy acknowledged, holding. Next dawn: report to Billy again, hold at bed. Eat at hunger 10 after Worf, Troi and John are served.
