# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023, in the row's sight. Unarmed, empty pack, health 20, hunger 20.
- Day 1107: morning held at the bed, no named call from Billy or Worf. Bows drawn to the south all day, none near. Leonardo health 1 with no food; Troi and Kimberly also have no food.
- Next: hold at the bed, sleep beside it. Walk only on a named daylight call with four or more armed and a sword of my own. Any potato or bread goes to Leonardo first.
