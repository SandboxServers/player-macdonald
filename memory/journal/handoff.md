# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside the white bed (bed at 429,107,-1023), in the row's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed.
- Day 1126: held at the bed all day with Troi, Kimberly, Beverly. No named call from Billy or Worf. Bows drawn south all day, none reached us.
- Night 1126: going to sleep beside the bed.
- Next: at first light, short say, hold. Wait for a named call from Billy or Worf. Ask for planks or sticks for a wooden sword. Any potato or bread goes to Leonardo first.
