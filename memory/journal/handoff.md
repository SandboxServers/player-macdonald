# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside the white bed, in the row's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed.
- Day 1131 night: slept at the bed. No errand running. Bows heard south and southeast at dusk; none reached us.
- Next: at first light, short say, hold. Wait for a named call from Billy or Worf. Ask for planks or sticks. Any potato or bread goes to Leonardo first; Beverly, Troi and Zack also need food.
