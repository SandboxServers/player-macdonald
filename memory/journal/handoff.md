# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023. Unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty.
- Day 1005: morning report given. No named call from Billy or Worf all day. Bows heard to the south, nobody hit. Leonardo at health 1, hungry; I have no food to give.
- Slept at the bed at night (day 1005 dusk). Not sheltered in an enclosure; in the row's sight.
- Next: at first light, short report, hold at the bed. Walk only on a named daylight call with four or more armed and a sword of my own.
