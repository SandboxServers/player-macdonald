# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023. Unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty.
- Day 980: held at the bed all day. Billy has no open route; planks still wall the row. Sleeping at dusk beside the bed in the row's sight. Bows heard south, nobody hit.
- Leonardo health 1, hunger 17, at bed 416; Trini holds his 2 potatoes. Beverly health 19, hunger 17.
- Next: at first light, short morning report, hold at the bed. Walk only on a named daylight call with four or more armed and a sword of my own. Leonardo gets any food first.
