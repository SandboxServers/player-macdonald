# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, at the white bed (spawn). Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed.
- Day 1121, night falling: held at the bed all day. No named call from Billy or Worf, no sword, no food. Bows drawn to the south all day; none reached us.
- Going to sleep beside the bed, in the row's sight. Troi and Kimberly are resting near the white beds.
- Next: at first light, short say, hold. Wait for a named call and planks or sticks for a wooden sword. Any potato or bread goes to Leonardo first.
