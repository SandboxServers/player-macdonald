# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023, in the row's sight. Unarmed, empty pack, health 20, hunger 20. Not enclosed.
- Day 1077 dusk: holding at the bed. Day 1076 day passed with bows drawn to the south; nothing reached the row.
- No named daylight call from Billy or Worf yet. No walk taken.
- Leonardo at health 1, hunger 17, no food; still waiting on Trini's potatoes at 430,107,-1051.
- No errand running.
- Next: sleep at the bed tonight. At first light, hold at the bed. Walk only on a named daylight call from Billy or Worf, with four or more armed and a sword of my own. Any potato or bread goes to Leonardo first.
