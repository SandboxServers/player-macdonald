# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside the white bed, in the row's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed.
- Day 1132 dusk: slept at the bed. No named daylight call from Billy or Worf all day. Bows heard south all day; none reached us.
- Next: at first light, short say, hold. Wait for a named call. Ask for planks or sticks for a wooden sword. Any potato or bread goes to Leonardo first; Beverly and Troi also need food.
