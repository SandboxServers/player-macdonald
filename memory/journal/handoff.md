# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, at the white bed. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed.
- Day 1123, just slept beside the bed. Bows drawn to the south all day, none reached us.
- Next: at first light, short say, hold. Wait for a named call from Billy or Worf, and ask for planks or sticks for a wooden sword. Any potato or bread goes to Leonardo first.
