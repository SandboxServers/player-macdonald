# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023. Unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty.
- Day 975: held at the bed all day, gave morning report. No named daylight walk from Billy, Worf, Deanna or Troi. Leonardo health 1, no food; Trini holds his potatoes. Beverly holds nothing, health 19, hunger 17. Bows drawn to the south all day and night, nobody hit. Slept beside the bed in the row's sight.
- Next: at first light, short report, hold at the bed. Walk only on a named daylight call with four or more armed and a sword of my own. Leonardo gets any food first.
