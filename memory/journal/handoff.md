# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, at the white bed, in the row's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed.
- Day 1125 dusk. Holding at the bed. No named call from Billy or Worf yet. Bows heard drawn to the south all day, none reached us.
- Next: sleep beside the bed. At first light, short say, hold. Wait for a named call. Ask for planks or sticks for a wooden sword. Any potato or bread goes to Leonardo first.
