# Handoff, night 568

Armed: wooden sword in hand, health 20, hunger 20. Asleep at 443,107,-1023 beside Picard and Zack, in Billy's sight.
No errand half-done. Billy's oak trip was blocked; I did not walk further.
Next: at first light, report to Billy and hold. Walk only on his named call, daylight, four or more armed.
