# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023, in the row's sight. Unarmed, empty pack, health 20, hunger 20.
- Day 1104, morning: held at the bed. No named call from Billy or Worf. Bows drawn south and southeast, none near.
- Leonardo at health 1, hunger 17, still asking for a potato. Any potato or bread goes to him first.
- Next: hold at the bed. Walk only on a named daylight call with four or more armed and a sword of my own.
