# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023, in the row's sight. Unarmed, empty pack, health 20, hunger 20.
- Day 1070 held at the bed all day. Bows drawn to the south and southeast, nothing reached the row. No named daylight call from Billy or Worf. Beverly and Leonardo also waiting at their beds.
- Dusk of 1070: going to sleep at the bed.
- Next: at first light, short say; hold at the bed; walk only on a named daylight call with four or more armed and a sword of my own. Any potato or bread goes to Leonardo first.
