# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside the white bed 429,107,-1023. Unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, no food, pack empty.
- Dusk of day 914: holding at the bed in the row's sight. No errand running. Not walking.
- Row: Worf's armed walk NE to 425,107,-1090 went out in the morning; I did not join (no sword). Leonardo at health 1, no food. Beverly and Billy hold potatoes for routing.
- Next: sleep in the bed beside me. At first light say morning and hold. Walk only on a named daylight call from Billy or Worf, with four or more armed in sight and a sword of my own. Give any potato to Leonardo first.
