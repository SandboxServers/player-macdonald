# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023, in the row's sight. Unarmed, empty pack, health 20, hunger 20. Not enclosed.
- Day 1083: held at the bed all day. Bows drawn to the south and southeast; nothing reached the row. Leonardo still at health 1, no food, waiting on Trini's potato. I have nothing to give.
- No named daylight call from Billy or Worf yet. No walk taken. No errand running.
- Next: at first light, say a short line, hold at the bed. Walk only on a named daylight call from Billy or Worf, with four or more armed and a sword of my own.
