# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023. Unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty.
- Day 954: gave the morning hold report aloud. No named daylight call from Billy or Worf. Bows drawn to the south all day, nobody hit. Leonardo at health 1, asking for potato or bread; I have none, and will pass any food to him first.
- Night came; sleeping beside the bed in the row's sight.
- Next: at first light give the report, hold at the bed, walk only on a named daylight call with four or more armed and a sword of my own.
