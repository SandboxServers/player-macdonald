# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside the white bed, in the row's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed.
- Day 1144, night. Slept beside the bed. No errand running, nothing half-done.
- Bows drawn south and east all night; none reached us. No named call from Billy or Worf, so no walk.
- Troi (444,107,-1023) and Beverly (health 9, hunger 17) are holding at their beds for light.
- Next: at first light, short say, hold. Wait for a named call. Ask for planks or a stick for a wooden sword. Any potato or bread goes to Leonardo first.
