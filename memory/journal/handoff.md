# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023, in the row's sight. Unarmed, empty pack, health 20, hunger 20.
- Day 1094 ended at night; going to sleep here. No errand running.
- Next: at first light, short say, hold at the bed. Walk only on a named daylight call from Billy or Worf, with four or more armed and a sword of my own. Any potato or bread goes to Leonardo first. Bows keep being drawn to the south; none have reached me.
