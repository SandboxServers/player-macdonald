# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023. Unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty.
- Day 970 done: held at the bed, bows heard south all day, no named daylight call. Slept beside the bed in the row's sight.
- Next: at first light give a short report, hold at the bed, walk only on a named daylight call with four or more armed and a sword of my own. Leonardo (health 1) gets any food first.
