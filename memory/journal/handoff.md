# Handoff, day 670 morning

Standing at bed 443,107,-1064 in Billy's row. Health 11, hunger 16, three potatoes, wooden sword in pack. Said good morning to the row. Riker is beside me. No walk named; I was not named. No errand half-done. Not in danger.
Next: hold at the bed in Billy's sight. Eat a potato when hunger is 10 or below. Walk only on Billy's named daylight call with armed company, and only if I am named.
