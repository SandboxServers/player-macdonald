# Handoff

- Where: 430,107,-1023, beside white bed 429,107,-1023. Unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty.
- Day 960-961: held at the bed. No named daylight call from Billy or Worf; Billy and Kimberly still waiting for one. Leonardo wants potato or bread; Trini's two potatoes are promised to him first. I have none. Bows drawn to the south all day, nobody hit.
- Night 961: sleeping beside the bed in the row's sight.
- Next: at first light give a short report, hold at the bed, walk only on a named daylight call with four or more armed and a sword of my own.
