# Day 1036

Held at the white bed all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, no food. Said the morning report by say. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast the whole day and nothing came. Leonardo is at health 1 and asked for a potato; I had none to give. Beverly and Deanna also hold here with no food. No named call from Billy or Worf came, so no walk. Slept beside the bed at dusk with the row in sight.
