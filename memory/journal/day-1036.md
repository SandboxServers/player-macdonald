# Day 1036

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day and through the night, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Heard bows drawn to the south and southeast several times; nothing came near. Chapman rested at the same bed and has no food either. No named walk from Billy or Worf, and no food came into the row. Slept beside the bed at dusk.
