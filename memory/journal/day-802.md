# Day 802

Held at the row all day with Billy's group. No walk named. Reported to Billy at dawn: health 15, hunger 16, no food, sword in hand. Trini got Troi's potato and is weakest; Kimberly, Zack and Bcrusher all report no food and no blocked tile. Bows drawn far to the south all day, none seen. Dusk came and I slept beside the white bed at 430,107,-1023 in the group's sight.
