# Day 1090

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast the whole day; nothing reached the row. No named call from Billy or Worf. Leonardo at health 1 and hunger 17 asked Trini for one potato, and I had none to give. Troi and Kimberly holding without food; Bcrusher gave Kimberly 4 potatoes before going to his bed. At dusk I said goodnight and slept beside the bed.
