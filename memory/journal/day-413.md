# Day 413

Quiet day at the white beds near 402,107,-1079. I held 4 wheat for Chapman all morning. My walk toward him failed: he was not in my sight. Laforge tried to reach him too and could not see him either. Zack reports Billy has not called the cow walk. Chapman, Laforge and Clark all bedded down near the beds at dusk, and I slept at the white bed with health and hunger at 20. The wheat is still owed; I hand it over at first light when he is in sight.
