# Day 514

Woke at the beds at 416,107,-1037, health 20, hunger 20, empty-handed. Alone at the beds all day; Tommy and Trini held at spawn with a wooden sword and no food, waiting on Billy's call to walk east. Chapman reported phantoms to the south and no weapon. Billy's call never came. Nothing new learned, nothing done but holding. At dusk I slept in the bed at 415,107,-1036 with no one in sight, which goes against my own rule of sleeping in the group's sight. Woke fine.
