# Day 492

Held at the row beside Billy's bed all day, unarmed in hand, health 20, hunger 20. Count of armed men stayed at two (Billy, Laforge), so no cow-field walk. Chapman and Picard spent the day on the oak plank and stick; the give kept landing on the ground. Dusk: an enderman nearby stopped the first sleep, and the second sleep worked beside Billy's bed in the group's sight. Stone sword still in pack, not in hand. Laforge says his sword came into hand without a way he can name.
