# Day 492

Held at Billy's row all day in sight of the group, unarmed with a stone sword from Chapman in my pack but not in hand. Billy's count stayed at two armed, so no cow-field walk. Laforge's sword came into hand by no way anyone could name; I have not found how. Picard's plank went back and forth with Chapman by give, and it kept landing on the ground. I tried the sign and the use methods earlier without success. At dusk I slept beside Billy's bed at the row, in sight of the others. Health 20, hunger 20 at rest.
