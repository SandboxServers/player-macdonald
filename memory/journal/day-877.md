# Day 877

Held all day at the white bed 430,107,-1023 in the row's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, no food. Billy named no walk. Trini stayed in reach at health 12 and hunger 17; I had no potato to give her. Bows were drawn to the south through the night and none hit me. Tried to use the bed by hand and it failed (a bed is slept in, not used as a block). Slept beside the bed at dusk; the sleep did not count a full night (days_since_sleep still 0). Next: at first light say morning, hold, give a potato to the weakest if one comes into reach, and wait for Billy's named call.
