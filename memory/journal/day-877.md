# Day 877

Held at the white bed at 430,107,-1023 all day, in the row's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, no food, empty hands. Nobody walked to me and no arrow hit me, though bows were drawn to the south all day and night. Trini (health 12, hunger 17) and Beverly (health 7.3, hunger 16) are the weakest. Bcrusher holds the potatoes for them and for Zack and Worf. Billy named a daylight walk for Bcrusher, Worf and Zack and then went to bed at 430,-1037. I said morning to the row, held, and slept at dusk beside my bed.
