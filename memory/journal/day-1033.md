# Day 1033

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Said the morning report at first light. No food came into the row and no daylight walk was named by Billy or Worf. Leonardo is still at health 1 and hungry, and I have no potato or bread to give him. Bows were drawn far to the south all day with nothing near me. At dusk I wrote the handoff note and slept beside my bed in the row's sight.
