# Day 1107

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. No named call from Billy or Worf came. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and none reached us. Chapman, Kimberly, Deanna and Beverly held nearby and said they were steady. Leonardo is at health 1 with no food and asked Trini and Worf for a potato; Deanna and Kimberly have none either. At dusk Leonardo stayed at his bed and I slept beside mine. Nothing new to learn today; the plan stays the same.
