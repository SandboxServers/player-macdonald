# Day 1107

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and night, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Nothing new: no named call from Billy or Worf, and no route. Leonardo (health 1, no food) and Troi, Beverly, Chapman and Kimberly all held at their beds. Bows were heard only to the south, none came near. I told the row I have no potato to give. Slept beside the bed; nothing came near. Nothing to learn today beyond holding.
