# Day 865

Held at the east bed all day, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, no food. No walk was named for me. Billy held 8 potatoes at 430,107,-1051 and meant to feed Worf and Zack first, then Beverly. Trini (health 12, hunger 17) and Leonardo are still waiting; Kimberly holds a potato for whichever of them Billy calls. I said morning to the row, asked Kimberly for the potato, and slept beside the bed at night in the row's sight. Nothing new was learned today beyond the row's food queue.
