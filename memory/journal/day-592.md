# Day 592

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight. No walk: nobody has a verified route west past x=370. Health 20, hunger 20 the whole day. Billy, Kimberly, Picard, William, John, Lewis and Troi all held and said so. Troi is at health 13.9, hunger 16. Bows heard to the southeast; we stayed in the row. Slept at bed 443,107,-1023 at dusk, sword in hand.
