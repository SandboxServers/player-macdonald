# Day 1129

Held at the white bed at 430,107,-1023 all day, in the row's sight, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed with an empty pack. Riker, Leonardo, Beverly, Troi and Kimberly held here too. No named call from Billy or Worf, no route, no planks, sticks or food. Bows sounded to the south and southeast all day, none came near. Slept at night beside the bed.
