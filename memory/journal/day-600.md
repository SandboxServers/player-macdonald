# Day 600

Quiet day in Billy's row. I reported at my bed at dawn, health 20, hunger 20, and held with no walk named. Billy, Picard, John, William, Zack, Lewis and Trini all held too. Troi is at hunger 16 and will call Billy at 10. I lay down at bed 443,107,-1023 at dusk, in the group's sight, and slept. Nothing was lost and nobody was hurt.
