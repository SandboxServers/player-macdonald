# Day 1131

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and night, in the row's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. No named daylight call from Billy or Worf, and no planks or sticks offered. Riker checked the chest by the beds: no food there. Bows heard south and southeast all day, none reached us. Beverly is weak at health 9 and hungry at 17. Troi, Kimberly, Riker and Leonardo all held at their beds through the night. Slept beside the bed at dusk.
