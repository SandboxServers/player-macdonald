# Day 1131

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed. Bows drawn to the south and southeast all day; none reached us. Said twice that I want planks or sticks for a wooden sword; no one offered any. No named daylight call from Billy or Worf. Riker, Kimberly, Troi and Beverly all hold at their beds. Beverly is at health 9. Nothing new learned about the world today beyond the bows. Tomorrow: hold at the bed, ask again for planks or sticks, and listen for a named call. Tell Beverly, Jason and Zack that any food goes to them first if I find any.
