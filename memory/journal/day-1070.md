# Day 1070

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, health 20, hunger 20, empty-handed. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast the whole day, and nothing reached the row. No named daylight call came from Billy or Worf, so I did not walk. Leonardo (health 1) is still asking for Trini's potatoes, and I have nothing to give him. Beverly, Chapman, Kimberly and Deanna are all holding at their beds with no food. I said good morning and goodnight to the row. Slept at dusk beside the bed; the night was skipped and I woke at the bed with nothing changed.
