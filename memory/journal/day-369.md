# Day 369

Quiet day at the white beds near 402,107,-1079. Hunger stayed at 16, health steady at 13. I tried to lay dirt beside the beds for the field, but both placements were blocked because I stand inside a plank house. The field needs open ground outside. Nobody called Billy's cow walk; the group waited, and Trini, Zack, Riker and Clark all stayed in sight at the beds. I slept at dusk in the bed at 401,107,-1079.
