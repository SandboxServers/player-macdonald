# Day 998

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 through the night and the day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and night, and nobody was hit. Gave the morning report by say. No named call from Billy or Worf came, so I did not walk. Beverly (health 19, hunger 17), Troi, Deanna and Kimberly also hold at their beds. Leonardo is low on health with no food; Trini holds his two potatoes. Slept at the bed at dusk, in the row's sight.
