# Day 998

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 through the night and the morning of day 997 and into dusk of 998. Unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Gave the morning report. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and night, and nobody was hit. No named call from Billy or Worf for me. Deanna and Beverly are also holding at their beds; Beverly is at health 19, hunger 17. Nothing new was learned. Slept at the bed at dusk, in the row's sight.
