# Day 581

Woke at my bed 443,107,-1023 with health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. Billy's group held at the row all day with no walk named. Troi was at health 8 and Billy handed her potatoes; she ate and stayed at her bed. Tommy and Picard settled the spare sword matter: Tommy gave a spare to Picard in sight of Billy. Billy warned that bows were being drawn to the south, so we stayed near our beds. I reported at dawn, held, and slept at dusk in Billy's sight. Nothing hurt me.
