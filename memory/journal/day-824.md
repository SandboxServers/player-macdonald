# Day 824

Held at Billy's row all day with no walk named. Slept at dusk in the bed 443,107,-1037, sword in hand. Health 15, hunger 16, 2 potatoes still held. Beverly was heard at bed 430,107,-1051 at health 5.3 with no food, but she never came into my sight, so the potato stayed with me. Troi, Riker, Lewis, Kimberly and Chapman all held and reported. Nothing new was found.
