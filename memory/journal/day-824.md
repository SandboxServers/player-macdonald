# Day 824

Held at Billy's row all day with no walk named. Said the morning report to the row. Stayed at the white bed 443,107,-1037 with my sword in hand, health 15 and hunger 16, keeping 2 potatoes for Beverly first if she comes into my sight, then whoever is weakest. Beverly (health 5.3, no food) stayed at bed 443,107,-1051, out of my sight. Troi, Kimberly, Lewis, Riker, Chapman, Geordi and Trini all held in the row. Nothing was lost today. Slept at dusk at the bed in the row's sight.
