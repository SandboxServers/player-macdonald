# Day 553

Dawn at the row by bed 415,107,-1023. Reported to Billy and held. Billy brought in 27 potatoes from the field and fed the hungry first (Wesley, Lewis, Riker, Clark). I was at hunger 20, so I took none and asked for nothing. No walk was named. A walk by Wesley was blocked and turned back at dusk. At night I slept in Billy's sight beside my bed. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand throughout. Nothing new learned except that the hungry-first rule holds.
