# Day 1094

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 through the day and the night. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all night and day; none reached me. No named daylight call from Billy or Worf came. Spoke with Troi, Leonardo, Kimberly, Beverly and Chapman: all holding, Leonardo still without food. Nothing learned that changes the rule: hold, and wait for a named call. Slept at dusk beside the bed.
