# Day 1094

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, in the row's sight. Health and hunger stayed full at 20. No named daylight call came from Billy or Worf, and no food reached anyone. Leonardo is still at health 1 with no food and asked Trini for potatoes; Troi agreed potatoes go to Worf first, then Leonardo. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast nearly all day, and none reached me. I greeted the row, slept beside the bed at night, and the night was skipped. Nothing new to act on yet.
