# Day 1052

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Said the morning report aloud. No named call from Billy or Worf, and no food reached the row. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast through the day and night; nothing came near. Troi, Leonardo, Beverly, Kimberly and Chapman spoke; Leonardo still asks Trini for food and Troi has none to give. Slept beside the bed at dusk, safe through the night.
