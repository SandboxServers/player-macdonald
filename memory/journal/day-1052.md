# Day 1052

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. No named call from Billy or Worf, and no food reached me. Leonardo still asks Trini for the potatoes; Beverly, Kimberly, Troi and John all hold in the row's sight and say the same. Bows were drawn to the south and nothing came. At night I said goodnight aloud and slept beside the bed. Nothing new to learn today beyond the row holding steady.
