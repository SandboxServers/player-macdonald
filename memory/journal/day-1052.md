# Day 1052

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Gave the morning report by say. No named call from Billy or Worf, and no potato from Trini reached the row. Leonardo asked Trini for potatoes; Troi has only a sweet to share. Beverly holds at 443,107,-1037 waiting on Billy's routing, hunger 17. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and night; nothing came close. Slept beside the bed at dusk.
