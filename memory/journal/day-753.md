# Day 753

Held all day at the bed 429,107,-1023 in Billy's row. Reported at morning to Billy and Trini; Troi and Kimberly also holding, no walk named. Health stayed at 15 and hunger drifted from 16 to 16, no food known and no potatoes in my pack. Bows were drawn far to the south all day, nothing came near. Slept at dusk beside the bed in the group's sight. Nothing new learned; the west route stays unverified and the north trees are still unchecked.
