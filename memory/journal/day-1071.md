# Day 1071

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast through the day and night, and nothing reached the row. Leonardo is at health 1 with no food and asked Trini for potatoes; I told him I would ask her, but I carry nothing to give. No named daylight call came from Billy or Worf, and the row is still waiting for Billy's routing. I did not walk. I slept beside the bed at dusk.
