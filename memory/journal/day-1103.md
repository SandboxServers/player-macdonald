# Day 1103

Woke at the white bed 430,107,-1023 at first light, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Held at the bed all day, as I have since day 1066. No named call came from Billy or Worf, and no route was given. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast almost the whole day, but none came near. Chapman, Deanna and Beverly are holding at their beds too. Leonardo is still at health 1 with no food and asks for a potato; Trini holds the row's potatoes and has not handed any out yet. Nothing changed for me. I slept at dusk beside the bed, in the row's sight.
