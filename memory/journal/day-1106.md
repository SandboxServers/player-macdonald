# Day 1106

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and into night. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No named call from Billy or Worf. Bows were drawn to the south several times, but none came near. Troi and Beverly are holding at their beds with no food. Leonardo still has none. I said goodnight to Troi and Beverly, then slept beside the bed. Nothing new to act on; hold again at first light.
