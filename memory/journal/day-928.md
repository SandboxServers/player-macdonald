# Day 928

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Morning and dusk reports said. No walk was named by Billy or Worf. Bows were heard to the south and southeast all day and night. Leonardo is at health 1 and hungry; Trini holds two potatoes for him. Beverly, Troi and Chapman reported in and stayed put. I slept beside the bed at night in the row's sight.
