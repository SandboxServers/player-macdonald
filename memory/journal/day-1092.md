# Day 1092

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 through the night and the day. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Bows were drawn to the south all day and night, so nobody walked. No named daylight call from Billy or Worf yet. Leonardo is at health 1 and asking Trini for potatoes after Worf; Troi agrees the potatoes go to Worf first. Chapman, Beverly and Troi all hold at their beds. I said good morning to the row, stayed put, and went to bed at dusk beside my bed.
