# Day 1077

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, in the row's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast most of the day and night; nothing reached the row. No named daylight call came from Billy or Worf, so no walk was taken. Leonardo still at health 1 and hungry, waiting on Trini's potatoes at 430,107,-1051. Kimberly, Troi, Beverly, Chapman and Deanna all hold at their beds with nothing to give. Slept at the bed at dusk.
