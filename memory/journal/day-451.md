# Day 451

Held at the row all day beside Billy's bed at 413,107,-1042, unarmed, hunger and health full. Billy kept the hold: no walks until four hold a sword, and his sword in pack still will not come into use. Kimberly came back to her bed at 415,-1037 on his call. Slept at dusk in the row's sight, with Billy, Tommy and Zack near.
