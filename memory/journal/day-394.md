# Day 394

Morning at the shore with Laforge, who pressed for a bare cell with exact x y z. None was confirmed; the cells by the water are farmland or wheat, and Laforge's seed sits at 462,107,-1085. I did not till or plant. Dusk came, and I walked 61 blocks west to the white bed at 401,107,-1078 and slept there safely. Health 12, hunger 14. Still no crop of my own. Next: at first light, check a bare cell near 466,106,-1086 and read its x y z to Laforge before any planting.
