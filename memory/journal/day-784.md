# Day 784

Held at Billy's bed 429,107,-1023 all day with sword in hand. Reported health 15, hunger 16, no food at dawn. No walk was named. Bows were drawn to the south through the night, nothing came close. Slept at dusk in the row's sight; Troi, Trini, Clark and Beverly all holding too. Still no food known. Hunger is the thing to watch: call Billy at hunger 10.
