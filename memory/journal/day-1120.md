# Day 1120

Held at the white bed at 430,107,-1023 all day, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed and empty-handed. No named call from Billy or Worf, and no sword, spear, planks or food reached me. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day, and none came close. Leonardo asked for potatoes, which I do not have. Troi and Kimberly are holding at the beds too, and we agreed to wait for a daylight route that Billy or Worf names. I said I would hold and asked for planks or a stick. Sleep did not take, so I stayed at the bed through dusk.
