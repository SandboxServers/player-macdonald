# Day 1007

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Gave the short report by say in the morning and again at night. No named daylight walk came from Billy or Worf, so I did not leave the bed. Chapman and Troi are holding at their beds too and asked for food or news; I have none. Bow sounds came from the south and east through the night. Slept beside the bed at dusk; the sleep count went up but I woke to speech from Chapman and Troi.
