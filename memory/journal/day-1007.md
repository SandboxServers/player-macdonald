# Day 1007

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day. Gave the morning report. No named walk call from Billy or Worf. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast almost the whole day, and I did not move. Chapman, Kimberly, Troi and Beverly held at their beds too; Leonardo is still at health 1 and hungry, and I had no food for him. At dusk I wrote the handoff note and slept beside the bed; the sleep worked for 7 of 10 needed. Nothing new was learned beyond the bows and the waiting.
