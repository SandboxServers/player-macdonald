# Day 1061

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and night, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Gave the morning report by say. No named call from Billy or Worf came, and no open cell was named. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast the whole time, and nothing reached the row. Leonardo, at health 1, asked Trini for the potatoes, and I had none to give. Troi and Beverly are holding with no food too. Slept beside the bed at dusk.
