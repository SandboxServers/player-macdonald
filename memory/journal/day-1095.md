# Day 1095

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, in the row's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No named call from Billy or Worf yet. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast most of the day and none reached me. Leonardo is at health 1 with no food and asked for potatoes; I have none to give. Trini still holds the row's potatoes. Slept at the white bed at dusk.
