# Day 1039

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, no food. Gave the morning report by say. Troi, Johnny, Kimberly, Beverly all holding at their beds. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast the whole day; nothing came near. No named walk from Billy or Worf, so I did not move. Slept beside the bed at dusk with the row in sight.
