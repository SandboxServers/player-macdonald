# Day 888

Held all day at the white bed, 430,107,-1023. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, no food. Said good morning to the row and held. Bows were drawn to the south all day and none hit me. Billy went to Beverly's bed by position; Trini holds 2 potatoes for Worf, to be handed over in daylight with Jason and Zack present. No walk was named for me, so I did not walk. Slept beside the white bed at dusk, in the row's sight.
