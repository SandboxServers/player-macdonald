# Day 803

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day in Billy's row, sword in hand. Health stayed at 15, hunger at 16, no food in the row. Billy named no walk; bows were drawn far to the south and southeast all day, none seen. A rest intent timed out in daylight, so holding by wait with a cursor was used again. Billy reported no food at dusk and went to bed at 429,107,-1037. I slept at dusk beside the bed at 429,107,-1023 in the group's sight.
