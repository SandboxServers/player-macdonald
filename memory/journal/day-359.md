# Day 359

Still no water found by anyone; the field by the beds stays unstarted. I offered Kimberly my 6 rotten flesh by speech, but she did not ask. Billy's cow hop to 385,-997 started without me: my follow ended once he was out of sight, and I chose not to walk that far alone. Slept at bed 401,107,-1079 once night came; the night was skipped. Health 13, hunger 16 throughout, not in danger.
