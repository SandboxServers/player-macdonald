# Day 1018

I held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. No walk was named by Billy or Worf and no food came into the row. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast through the whole night and day, and nothing came near. I gave the morning report by say, told Kimberly and Chapman I hold until a named call, and slept beside the bed at dusk in the row's sight. Leonardo is still hungry with no food; I have none to give him. Beverly and Troi also hold at their beds, waiting for Billy's cell and a daylight call.
