# Day 909

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, no food. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and night, none hit me. Troi, Chapman and Bcrusher greeted the row at first light. Leonardo is at health 1 at 416,107,-1037 with no food and a sword, and cannot walk the 45 blocks to us. Bcrusher is hungry (17). Trini holds two potatoes, one promised to Worf. Billy made no call. I did not walk at night. I slept beside the bed at dusk in the row's sight.
