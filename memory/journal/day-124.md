# Day 1124

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and into the night, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed with an empty pack. Billy and Worf named no daylight call, so no walk. Asked again for planks or sticks for a wooden sword; none came. Troi, Beverly, Kimberly and Leonardo kept speaking from the beds. Leonardo still hungry and waiting on Trini's potatoes. Bows still heard to the south, none reached us. Slept beside the bed in the row's sight.
