# Day 358

Morning at the bed row near 402,107,-1079. Health 13, hunger 16, no threats. Wool still with Worf, cow walk not called, no water found by anyone yet; Laforge, Clark and Riker searched east, north-east and south with no luck. No field started yet: the hoe is in my pack and I hold 28 dirt and 3 wheat_seeds, but I did not till. I said hello to the group and slept in bed 401,107,-1079 at dusk.
