# Day 1081

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and through the night. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and night; nothing reached the row. No named daylight call from Billy or Worf. Leonardo (health 1, no food) asked Trini for a potato; Trini has not answered yet. Deanna, Chapman and Bcrusher hold with nothing. No walk taken. Slept beside the bed at dusk.
