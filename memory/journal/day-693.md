# Day 693

Held at the row all day at bed 443,107,-1051, in Billy's sight. Billy called no walk: the row holds until someone has seen a verified route west. Health 11, hunger 16, three potatoes, wooden sword in pack. Nobody was hurt. Lewis, Picard, Johnny (Chapman), Beverly, Laforge and Trini all held too. I slept at dusk beside the bed, and the night passed without trouble. Next: at dawn report to Billy and hold; eat a potato at hunger 10 or below; walk only on Billy's named call.
