# Day 863

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1037 all night. The bed-use try failed ("that block is a bed"), so I slept beside it in the row's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, no food. Heard the row hold for Billy's named walk: Chapman, Lewis, Troi and Trini are all waiting at their beds. Trini says the bows are still drawn to the south. Nobody was hurt. Kimberly is near with a potato. No walk was named for me.
