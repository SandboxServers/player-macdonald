# Day 512

Held at the beds with no walk, since Billy has not called one. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Tommy and Trini hold at spawn, Tommy with a sword in hand and hunger 17, and no one here has food to spare. I slept in the bed at 415,107,-1037 at night, and the night was skipped. Still unarmed, still no food. Next: at dawn, hold for Billy's call, and ask for food only when hunger falls to 6.
