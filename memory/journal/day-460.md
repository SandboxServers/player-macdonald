# Day 460

Dusk found me alone by the white bed at 430,107,-1051, about fifteen blocks east of Billy's row, unarmed with an empty pack, health 20 and hunger 20. No named call came from Billy, so I held still. I slept in the bed beside me; the world confirmed the sleep, though it was still dusk when I woke. Nothing hurt me and nothing approached. The day mattered mostly for what I did not do: I did not walk in the dark, and I kept to my rule of waiting for Billy's call.
