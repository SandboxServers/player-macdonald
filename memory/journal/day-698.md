# Day 698

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day, in sight of the group. Reported to the row at dawn: health 11, hunger 16, three potatoes, no walk named. Trini called out to check on me and promised to call at hunger 10. Nothing came from the west. Hunger never reached 10, so I did not eat. Health stayed at 11 and did not regenerate while hunger was above the low line. Slept at dusk beside bed 443,107,-1051. Nothing new learned; no walk, no wood.
