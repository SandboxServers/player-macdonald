# Day 1130

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Bows drawn to the southeast several times; none reached us. Troi holds at the bed too, health 10, hunger 17, and is waiting for Billy or Worf to name a route at light. I said good night to Troi and slept beside the bed. Still no weapon and no food. Next: at light, hold, wait for a named call, and ask for planks or sticks.
