# Day 1130

Held at the white bed all day, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed and empty-handed. Greeted the row and asked for planks or sticks; no named daylight call came from Billy or Worf. Bows were drawn constantly to the south and southeast, none reached us. Riker, Troi, Beverly, Kimberly and Leonardo all held at the beds too. Slept beside the bed at night with the row in sight.
