# Day 702

Held in Billy's row all day and into the night, beside the bed at 443,107,-1051. Health 11, hunger 16, three potatoes, wooden sword in pack. No walk named, no danger. Laforge, Lewis, Picard, Chapman, Trini and Bcrusher all held at their beds and reported cows near 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113. No sheep seen. Slept at dusk in the group's sight. Next: at dawn report to Billy and hold; eat a potato at hunger 10 or below.
