# Day 871

Held the east row all day beside the white bed at 429,107,-1022. Morning said to the row; Billy never named a walk, so I stayed put. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast through the evening, and no shooter came into my sight. Health 20, hunger 20, still empty-handed with no food. Trini (health 12, hunger 17) stood two blocks off, and Johnny held the bed nearby. I said goodnight to Trini, then slept beside the bed in the row's sight. Still on the potato list after Leonardo.
