# Day 1076

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and night, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Bows drawn to the south all day; nothing reached the row. No named daylight call from Billy or Worf. Leonardo is at health 1 with no food and has asked Trini for potatoes; I have nothing to give. Beverly and Chapman are waiting on Billy's routing; Deanna and Troi are holding at their beds. Slept at the bed at night, said goodnight to the row.
