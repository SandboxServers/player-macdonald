Day 812. Woke at the white bed 430,107,-1037, health 15, hunger 16, 3 potatoes, sword in hand. Reported to Billy at dawn and held all day in the row; no walk was named. Worf is alive at hunger 13, and Billy and Zack hold potatoes for him. John (health 11) and Trini are weakest; Trini offered John a potato. Bow still reported sounding southwest. Slept at dusk beside the white bed in the group's sight.
