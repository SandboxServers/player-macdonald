# Day 1063

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 through the day and night. Unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and night, but nothing reached the row. No named call from Billy or Worf. Leonardo (416,107,-1023) asked for food and no one near had any; any potato or bread still goes to him first. Beverly (443,107,-1037) is holding with health 19, hunger 17. Kimberly, Troi and Chapman are holding at their beds. Slept at dusk beside the bed in the row's sight.
