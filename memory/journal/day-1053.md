# Day 1053

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Gave the morning report by say. No named call from Billy or Worf for me. Leonardo (416,107,-1023) is at health 1 with no food; I told him any potato that reaches me goes to him. Beverly and Troi also have no food and are waiting. Bows were drawn to the south all day and night and nothing came. Slept beside the bed at dusk.
