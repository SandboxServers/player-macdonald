# Day 1196 (the journal's day number; the world day runs to 1199, see handoff.md)

This stretch mattered most for food and for trust. I died once at the tables, at 18:15 of the old journal, and woke at spawn with nothing; my sword lay on the ground. Uhura gave me a spare wooden sword at 17:48, and after my death Bilbo lent me another, so I have been armed since. I went without food from the first hunger drop to 17 and stayed there for many days: I ate rotten flesh and mutton from Knope, a porkchop from Uhura, and gave my one raw chicken to Chewie when the group said he was weakest. Walking alone or in the dark ended badly every time, so the rule that held was: walk only in daylight, in sight of armed people, on a named call. The 8 jungle planks I had from my logs went to Anning for her shelter after several failed gives (too many people within 2 blocks); the planks are gone from my pack, and my attempt to place one beside Crusoe's gap failed because there was no solid block to place against. Lizzie's roof is still open and I cannot confirm it from outside, so I have no bed and no closed cell. Health is 18, hunger 17, and I hold no planks. Old notes about a white bed at 430,107,-1023 and Billy's row are from an earlier stretch and no longer hold.
