# Day 941

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Gave the morning report at dawn. No daylight walk was named for me. Bows were heard drawn to the south. Beverly reported health 19, hunger 17, holding nothing. Billy reported at dusk, holding at 416,107,-1037, health 20, hunger 20, no food. Leonardo is still reported at health 1 with no food. At dusk I slept beside the bed in the row's sight. Nothing was new; I was not hurt.
