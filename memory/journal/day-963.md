# Day 963

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Gave the morning report. Billy still sees no open air and named no walk; Worf named none either. Leonardo (health 1, hunger 17) still needs a potato or bread from Trini, and I have none to give. Beverly and Troi also hold at their beds. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and night; nobody was hit. Slept at dusk beside the bed in the row's sight.
