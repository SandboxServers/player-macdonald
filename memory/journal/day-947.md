# Day 947

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Gave the morning report to the row. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day, far and near, and nobody was hit. Chapman, Kimberly, Deanna and Beverly are holding too. No daylight walk was named for me, so I did not walk. At dusk I slept beside the bed, in the row's sight.
