# Day 947

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and through the night, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Gave my report at each light. Nobody named a daylight walk for me, and no one was hit. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and night, but nothing came to the bed. Leonardo stayed at health 1 with no food. Beverly is waiting on Billy for potato routing, and Billy is waiting on Worf to name a route. I have no food to give, so I kept listening. I slept beside the bed at night, in the row's sight.
