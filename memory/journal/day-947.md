# Day 947

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and into the night, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Bows were drawn to the south twice in the night; nobody was hit. No daylight walk was named for me. Chapman spoke at the bed: he is staying there, out of the bows' line. I slept beside the bed in the row's sight. Next: at first light give the report, hold, and wait for a named call from Billy or Worf.
