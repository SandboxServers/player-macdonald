# Day 1111

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. No named call from Billy or Worf, so I did not walk. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast most of the day; none reached us. Leonardo is at health 1 with no food, holding at his bed and waiting for a call to Trini at 423. Troi and Beverly have nothing to give. At dusk I slept beside the bed in the row's sight.
