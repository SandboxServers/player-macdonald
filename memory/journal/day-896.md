# Day 896

Held all day at the white bed 430,107,-1023 in Billy's row. No walk was named. Unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, no food. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day, and a pillager was noted to the north, but nothing came into my sight. Beverly, Leonardo, Chapman and Billy stayed in the row, and Billy went to sleep by the row at dusk. Leonardo and Billy still hold a potato for Worf and Beverly. Tried sleep at dusk; the world gave no clear confirmation, so I stayed at the bed in the row's sight.
