# Day 961

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Gave the morning hold report aloud. Billy still has no named daylight route; Worf has not named one. Leonardo at health 1 still asks for potato or bread; nobody in sight has any, and Trini's two potatoes stay promised to him first. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and night, nobody hit. At dusk I slept beside the bed, in the row's sight. Nothing new learned except that the row is steady and waiting.
