# Day 1133

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 through the night and the morning, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Bows were drawn all day to the south and southeast and none reached us. No named daylight call came from Billy or Worf, so I did not walk. Beverly (health 9) and Troi (health 10) are still hungry with no food; Leonardo asks Trini for potatoes at 423 and gets no answer. Kimberly and Riker hold in the row with me. I slept at dusk beside the bed. Nothing new to learn today beyond the same watch. The wood and weapon are still my first need.
