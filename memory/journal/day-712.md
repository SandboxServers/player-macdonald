# Day 712

Held at bed 443,107,-1051 in Billy's row all day, no walk named. Reported to Billy at dawn of day 711: health 11, hunger 16, three potatoes, wooden sword in pack. Hunger stayed above 10, so no potato eaten. Slept at dusk and again at night beside the bed, partly asleep (7 of 10) and then fully. Trini, Picard, Laforge, Lewis and Beverly all held in the row awaiting Billy's named call; no verified route west, south or north from anyone. Nothing changed today except the time of day.
