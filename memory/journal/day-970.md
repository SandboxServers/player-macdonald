# Day 970

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Gave the morning report. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and night, and nobody hit me. No named daylight call came from Billy or Worf. Leonardo is at health 1 with no food; Trini still holds 2 potatoes and has not routed them. Deanna, Beverly, Kimberly and Chapman are holding at their beds with empty or low packs. At night Billy said he holds with the row. I slept beside the bed in the row's sight.
