# Day 1079

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed with an empty pack, health 20, hunger 20. No named daylight call came from Billy or Worf, so no walk was taken. Only bow sounds to the south and southeast were heard. Chapman, Kimberly, Beverly, Deanna and Troi all held at their beds with no food, and Troi said no one walks past 423 until two readers agree. Slept beside the bed at night in the row's sight.
