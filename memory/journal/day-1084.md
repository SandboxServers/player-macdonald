# Day 1084

I held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and night, unarmed, pack empty, health 20, hunger 20. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and nothing reached the row. No named daylight call came from Billy or Worf. Leonardo is still at health 1 with no food, holding and scared to walk. Troi, Kimberly and Beverly are holding too, with nothing to give. Trini still holds the row's potatoes at 430,107,-1051. I said a short line to the row in the morning and at dusk. I went to bed beside the bed, in the row's sight.
