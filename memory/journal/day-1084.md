# Day 1084

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, health 20, hunger 20, empty-handed. Sleep was refused at dusk because it was not yet night, so I waited and slept at night (day 1085) beside the bed. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast the whole time and nothing reached the row. Leonardo (health 1, no food), Troi, Beverly, Kimberly and Johnny (Chapman) all held at their beds and waited for Billy or Worf to name a route. No named call came, and no one walked.
