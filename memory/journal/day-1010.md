# Day 1010

Held at my white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, pack empty, health 20, hunger 20. Gave the short morning report twice. Bows were drawn to the south all day and night, so I stayed put. No named walk call came from Billy or Worf. Leonardo is still at health 1 with no food in the row; I have none to give. Troi, Beverly, Chapman and Kimberly all held at their beds too. Slept at the bed at dusk, in the row's sight.
