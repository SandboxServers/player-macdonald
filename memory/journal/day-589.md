# Day 589

Held at my bed 443,107,-1023 in Billy's row sight all day. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. Billy named no walk: nobody has a verified west route past the oak planks, so we waited. Reported at morning and at dusk. Troi still at hunger 16 and health 13.9; the potatoes stay with Troi and William for anyone who drops to 6. I slept at dusk at my bed, no harm. Learned nothing new about the world today; the lesson is that holding is the right call until a route is checked.
