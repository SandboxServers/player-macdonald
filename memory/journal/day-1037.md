# Day 1037

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Gave the morning report by say. No named call from Billy or Worf, and no food came into the row. Bows were drawn again and again to the south and southeast, none came close. Leonardo is still at health 1 with no food, and Trini holds the potatoes. Zack is at health 13 and hunger 14. Slept at dusk beside the bed with the row in sight.
