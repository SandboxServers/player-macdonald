# Day 1037

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Gave the morning report by say. Bows were drawn to the south all day and nothing came into the row. No named call from Billy or Worf, and no walk or food named for me. Leonardo stayed at health 1 with no food; Troi reports Trini holds the potatoes for Leonardo and Worf. Chapman, Kimberly and Troi held nearby and spoke of the same wait. At dusk the light went and I stayed at the bed in the row's sight for the night.
