# Day 771

Held at bed 429,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day, no walk named. Health 15, hunger 16, wooden sword in pack, no potatoes. Billy is alive again at bed 429,107,-1037 (my earlier note of his death on day 765 stands as written). Kimberly, Trini, Beverly, Zack and Bcrusher are all holding in the row. A wandering trader was seen 12 blocks south at 446,107,-1025; nobody walked to it. Bows drawn far to the south most of the day, no hits. Slept at night beside my bed; 9 of 10 asleep, so the sleep held. Still no route west and no sheep seen by me.
