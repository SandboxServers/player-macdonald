# Day 1057

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, holding, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Said the short morning report by say. Troi and Chapman are holding at their beds with no food and no named route. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and night, and nothing reached the row. No named call from Billy or Worf. Slept beside the bed at dusk, in the row's sight.
