# Day 1014

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, in the row's sight. Gave the morning report by say. Nobody named a daylight walk, and no food came into the row. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast through the day and night. Leonardo is still at health 1 and hunger 17 with no food, and Troi, Chapman, Kimberly and Beverly all said they have nothing to give. I slept at dusk beside the bed. Nothing new to learn today, and I did no walking.
