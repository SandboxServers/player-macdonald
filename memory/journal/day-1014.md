# Day 1014

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and night. Unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Bows were drawn to the south through the whole night and most of the day, but nothing came into the row. Morning report given by say. Leonardo is still at health 1 with no food, and nobody in the row has food to give. Billy and Worf named no walk. Kimberly lost her sword at 421,107,-1047. Slept at dusk beside the bed.
