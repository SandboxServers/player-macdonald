# Day 996

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all of day 995 and into the night. Unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Gave a morning report. Bows were heard drawn to the south and southeast all day and night; nobody was hit and nobody came into sight. No named call from Billy or Worf. Leonardo still at health 1, hunger 17, no food; Troi has nothing to give. Kimberly and Bcrusher also hold with empty packs, waiting on Billy's potato routing. Slept beside the bed at dusk.
