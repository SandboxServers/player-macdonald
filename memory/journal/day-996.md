# Day 996

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Gave the morning report to the row. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast through the day and night; nobody was hit. No named walk came from Billy or Worf; Troi said Worf had looked in on the row. Leonardo is still at health 1 with no food, and Trini holds his two potatoes. I have no food to give. Slept at the bed at dusk, in the row's sight.
