# Day 987

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all night and day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast through the whole day and night, and nobody was hit. Gave the morning report. Leonardo is still at health 1 with no food; Trini holds his potatoes, and nobody has named a daylight walk. Troi, Johnny and Kimberly are holding too. Slept beside the bed at dusk (partial sleep). Goals unchanged.
