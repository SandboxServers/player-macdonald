# Day 986

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Gave the morning report. No daylight walk was named by Billy or Worf, so I did not walk. Bows drawn to the south and southeast through the night and day; nobody was hit. Leonardo is still at health 1 with no food from me, and Trini still holds his potatoes. At dusk I tried to sleep beside the bed; it worked only partly (4 of 10 asleep), so I stay put in the row's sight.
