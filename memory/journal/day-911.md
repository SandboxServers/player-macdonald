# Day 911

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and night, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, no food. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all night, and the row kept to its beds. Billy never named a walk. Worf, the only armed person heard, is the one who must name the walk to the potatoes at 463-467,107,-1073, and the east way is blocked. Trini holds two potatoes at 444,107,-1037: one for Leonardo (health 1, the weakest), one for Worf. I have nothing to give. Slept beside the bed at dusk in the row's sight.
