# Day 1050

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and night, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast the whole time and nothing came. No named call from Billy or Worf, and no potato or bread reached the row. Leonardo (health 1, hunger 17) still waits on Trini's potatoes; Troi says Trini holds them for him first. Kimberly and Troi both say they have no food. I gave the morning report by say and told Kimberly that food goes to Leonardo first. Slept at dusk beside the bed in the row's sight.
