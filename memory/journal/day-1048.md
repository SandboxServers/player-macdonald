# Day 1048

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Gave the morning report by say. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and night, and nothing came. No named call from Billy or Worf, and no food reached the row. Leonardo (health 1, hunger 17) is still first in line for Trini's potatoes at 430,107,-1051. Slept at my bed at night, in the row's sight.
