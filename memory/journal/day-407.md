# Day 407

Quiet day at the white bed near 401,107,-1077. Health 12 and hunger 13 the whole time, so I kept my 6 rotten flesh for when hunger drops to 6. Billy never called the cow walk, so I stayed put and waited. Jason, Zack, Clark and Chapman spoke from the beds: Jason and Clark slept at the bed row, Zack was fed, and Chapman still had oak to hand to Data. Ripe wheat stands near the beds at 403,108,-1076 and on the shore at 463-466,107,-1082 to -1087. I slept at dusk on day 407 beside the bed.
