# Day 586

Woke at bed 443,107,-1023, health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. Billy counted the row at dawn and held us all in place. Leonardo was sent to check the oak planks to the west, but the route is still not verified, so no walk was named all day. Troi received two potatoes from Billy at the row. At dusk I held at my bed in the row's sight and slept there. Nothing hurt me today.
