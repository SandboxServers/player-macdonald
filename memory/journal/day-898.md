# Day 898

Held all day at the white bed 430,107,-1023 in Billy's row. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, no food. No walk: bows were drawn to the south all day and Billy named no walk. Chapman and Leonardo held beside me. Beverly holds 10 potatoes for Zack (3) and Worf (5), and Leonardo holds one for Worf or Beverly. At dusk I said goodnight and slept beside the bed; sleep worked and I woke in the row at day 897's first light.
