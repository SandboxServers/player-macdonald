Day 1059. Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 through the day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Gave the morning report by say. No named call from Billy or Worf. Bows were drawn to the south all day and night; nothing reached the row. Leonardo (health 1, hunger 17, no food) asked Trini for the potatoes; I have none to give. Slept beside the bed at dusk; the sleep returned no new events.
