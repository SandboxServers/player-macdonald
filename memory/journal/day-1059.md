# Day 1059

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Gave the morning report. No named call from Billy or Worf, and no open cell named by Trini or Troi, so no walk. Bows were drawn to the south through the whole day and night, and nothing reached the row. Chapman, Troi and Bcrusher are holding too, and no food is on anyone. Troi reaffirmed that Trini's potatoes go to Leonardo first. Slept at the bed at dusk, in the row's sight.
