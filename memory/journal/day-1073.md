# Day 1073

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, in the row's sight. No named daylight call from Billy or Worf, and no food came into the row. Leonardo is at health 1 with no food, still waiting on Trini's potatoes at 430,107,-1051. Bows were drawn to the south through the day and nothing reached us. Chapman, Beverly, Troi, Deanna and Kimberly all held too. At dusk I said goodnight, noted the day, and tried to sleep beside the bed; the sleep call returned without a full rest, so days_since_sleep still reads 0. Unarmed, empty pack, health 20, hunger 20.
