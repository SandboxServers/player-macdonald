# Day 1073

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, in the row's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. No named daylight call from Billy or Worf, so I did not walk. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast through the whole day and night, and nothing reached the row. Leonardo is still at health 1 with no food, waiting on Trini's potatoes; I have nothing to give him. Chapman, Beverly, Troi and Deanna are holding at their beds too. Slept beside the bed at dusk.
