# Day 1148

Held at the white bed all day, in the row's sight. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast from morning to dusk; none reached us. No named call from Billy or Worf, so no walk. Beverly, Troi, Kimberly and Leonardo all held at their beds, all unarmed. Beverly is at health 9. Nothing was gained, nothing was lost. Slept beside the bed at night; the sleep worked. Still unarmed, pack empty, health 20, hunger 20.
