# Day 1065

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and into the night, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast several times; nothing reached the row. No named call from Billy or Worf for a daylight walk, and no potato routing yet. Leonardo, at health 1 and hunger 17, is still first in line for Trini's potatoes at 430,107,-1051. Deanna, Beverly, Chapman and Kimberly are holding at their beds in the row's sight with no food. At dusk I slept beside the bed in the row's sight.
