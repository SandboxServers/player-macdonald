# Day 1065

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and all night, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Thunder in the morning, clear at dusk. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast through the whole day and night; nothing reached the row. Leonardo is at health 1 and hungry, waiting for Trini to bring the potatoes at 430,107,-1051. Deanna and Beverly are holding in the row's sight with no food. No named call from Billy or Worf yet. I greeted the row at light, said a short report, and slept beside the bed at dusk.
