# Day 1065

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 through the day and the night, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. No named call from Billy or Worf came. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day. Nothing reached the row. Leonardo is at health 1 with no food and asked for any spare food; I have none to give. Deanna and Beverly are holding at their beds in the row's sight. I said a short hold report at dawn and slept beside the bed at dusk; the sleep was partial (6 of 10 asleep).
