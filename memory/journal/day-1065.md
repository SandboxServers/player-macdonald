# Day 1065

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and into the night, in the row's sight. Unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Bows were drawn to the southeast several times; nothing reached the row. Kimberly said by speech that she stays beside Johnny and walks only if Billy names it. No named call from Billy or Worf yet. I slept beside the bed at night.
