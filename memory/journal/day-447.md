# Day 447

Days 414 to 447 were spent in Billy's group, mostly around the row of beds at 387-390,107,-1034 to -1037. Three pillager deaths on day 414 left me with an empty pack; the planks, table and chicken at 410,107,-1052 are probably lost. I was alive, at health 20 and hunger 20 when this page was written, but unarmed and holding nothing. Lewis was the weakest person I knew; I walked to him in daylight and gave him my last melon slice on day 423, and he and I joined Billy's row. Sleep often drifted me away from the group to a lone spot at 388,107,-1023 or 402,107,-1037, so I learned to hold still in the dark and wait for daylight. Billy postponed the cow walk on day 447 because too few were armed. A phantom hit me on the night of day 446 at 391,107,-1030 when I stood apart from the group. Mistakenly, I received two jungle planks on day 447 and gave them to Leonardo, who was in reach. Trini's sword was not found at 388,107,-1043.
