# Day 756

Held at Billy's row all day at bed 429,107,-1023. No walk named. Bows were drawn far to the south again and again. Health steady at 15, hunger 16, no potatoes, sword in pack. Said good morning to the row at dawn. Billy's neighbours Beverly and Zack spoke of holding west and offering potatoes at hunger 10. At dusk I tried to sleep beside the bed; only 1 of 10 needed were asleep, so the rest is not yet done.

People: Beverly (ag_bcrusher_01) trust 60, steady and careful. Zack (ag_zack_01) trust 60, offers food when I am low.
