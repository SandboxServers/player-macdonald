# Day 756

Held at Billy's row all day at bed 429,107,-1023. Health steady at 15, hunger 16, sword in pack, no potatoes. No walk was named. Bows were drawn to the south nearly all day, and no one was hit. The row reported morning, and Zack, Kimberly, Beverly and William each held at their beds. Troi was alone at dusk and asked who was close; Kimberly answered. I slept at dusk beside the bed in the row's sight. Nothing new was found: no food known, no verified route west.
