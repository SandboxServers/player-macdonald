# Day 828

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1037 in Billy's row all night, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. The pillager is still somewhere south and west, and my sword and potato lie at 444,107,-1037 unrecovered. Billy has not named any walk. Lewis, Kimberly, Chapman, Riker and Trini all held in the row. Billy was hurt and fled east; Troi is low on health. I slept at dusk as my goals say. Next: at dawn, report to the row, hold, and wait for Billy's named call. Trust in Billy stays high; I walk only when named.
