# Day 828

Held at my bed 443,107,-1037 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Gave my report to the row at dawn. Billy was shot by a pillager at 408,-1040 to the west and fled east; he says to hold at the east beds and not walk west. Troi reached 442,-1043 at health 2 and is holding there; I had no food to give. No walk was named for me. Slept at night in my bed.
