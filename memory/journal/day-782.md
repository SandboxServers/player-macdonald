# Day 782

Held at the bed 429,107,-1023 all day in Billy's row. No walk was named. Health stayed at 15 and hunger at 16, with no food in my pack. Bows were drawn to the south all day, and nobody came near. Beverly, William and Troi each reported holding at the row. Troi will call Billy at hunger 10. I said my morning report to the row and asked for no walk without Billy's call. Slept at dusk beside the bed; sleep worked.
