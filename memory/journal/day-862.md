# Day 862

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Billy never named a walk for me. Trini (health 12, hunger 16) and Leonardo are waiting for potatoes; I have none to give. Troi and Johnny also hold at their beds. Thunder at dawn, then clear. Tried to sleep by using the bed; it failed ("that block is a bed"), but sleep worked and the day ended.
