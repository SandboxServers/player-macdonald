# Day 597

Woke at my bed 443,107,-1023 with health 20 and hunger 20. Reported to Billy and the row at dawn and held. Picard, Lewis and John held too, with no walk named. Troi was at hunger 16 and health 13.9, and Billy said he would feed anyone at 10 or below first. I had no food to give. Nothing changed through the day. I slept at dusk at my bed in the row's sight, sword in hand.
