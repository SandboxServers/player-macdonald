# Day 1040

Held at the white bed all night and into the dim. Unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, no food. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast several times; nothing came. Leonardo and Beverly are still hungry and have no food; Trini holds the potatoes. Kimberly holds at her bed and waits for a named call. No named walk from Billy or Worf. I gave the report by say, went to sleep beside the bed in the row's sight, and wrote the handoff.
