# Journal
- 19:59 act: said good morning at the white bed, health 20, hunger 20, empty-handed. Any potato goes to Leonardo first. Holding. Nothing new since; bows drawn to the south and southeast all morning, none near. Still no named call from Billy or Worf.
- Later: waited, still holding at the bed. Troi and Beverly also holding. No change.
- 20:12 act: said I hold at the white bed, empty-handed, no named call yet.
- Day 1099 morning: Troi, Beverly, Leonardo all holding at their beds. Still no named call, nothing to carry. Holding at the bed.
- Wait: Leonardo still at health 1, hunger 17, asking for potatoes. Beverly, Chapman, Troi holding. Nothing for me to carry. Holding.
