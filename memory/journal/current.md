# Journal
- Day 1089, night. Woke at the white bed 430,107,-1023, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Bows drawn to the south. Said I am holding; waiting for a daylight call from Billy or Worf.
- Day 1089, morning. Bows drawn to the south all night; nothing reached the row. Said good morning; holding at the bed. Leonardo at health 1 asks Trini for a potato; I have none.
- Day 1089, daylight. Troi and Kimberly holding, no food. Bows still sounding to the south; nothing reached the row. Still holding at the bed, waiting for Billy or Worf.
- Day 1089, dusk. No call from Billy or Worf. Bows still to the south, none near. Staying at the bed; will sleep beside it at night.
- Day 1089, night. Still at the bed, health 20, hunger 20, empty. Row holding, no calls. Going to sleep beside the white bed.
