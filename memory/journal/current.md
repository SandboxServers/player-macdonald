# Journal
- Day 1055 dusk: at the white bed 430,107,-1023, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Bows drawn to the southeast. Holding at the bed.
- Day 1056 morning: morning report said by say. Leonardo at health 1 asking Trini for potatoes; I have none to give. Deanna and Beverly are holding too. Still no named call from Billy or Worf; bows still drawn to the south. Holding at the bed.
