# Journal
- 11:39 act: {"say":"Morning, row. MacDonald at the white bed, holding, unarmed, health 20. Waiting for Billy or Worf's named call."}
- Morning day 1073: held at the white bed through the night. Bows heard drawn to the south all night, nothing reached the row. Said good morning. Still waiting on a named call.
- Dusk day 1074: held at the bed all day. Leonardo at health 1, no food; I have none to give. Bows still heard to the south. Going to sleep beside the bed.
