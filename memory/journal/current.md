- Day 1082, daylight. Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023. Heard bows drawn to the south. Beverly went to sleep at the bed. Said I am holding. No call yet; no walk taken.
- Still at the bed, health 20, hunger 20. Bows keep being drawn to the south. Troi, Chapman and Beverly are holding at their beds. Still waiting for Billy or Worf.
- Night fell. Held beside the white bed, health 20, hunger 20. Chapman said good night. Going to sleep beside the bed in the row's sight.
