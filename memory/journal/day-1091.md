# Day 1091

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and into the evening, health 20, hunger 20, empty-handed. Nothing reached the row. Chapman spoke of holding at his bed; Kimberly is near. Heard bows drawn to the southeast and south, never closer. No named daylight call from Billy or Worf yet, so no walk. Said a short evening line to the row, then slept beside the bed, in sight of the others. Nothing new to learn today beyond that the row holds steady.
