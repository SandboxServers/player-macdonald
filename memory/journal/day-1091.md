# Day 1091

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, health and hunger full, pack empty. Said my holding line once in the morning. Nobody named a daylight walk, bed or food from Billy or Worf. Leonardo (health 1) asked Trini for food and I had none to give. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day with nothing near me. At dusk I slept beside the bed.
