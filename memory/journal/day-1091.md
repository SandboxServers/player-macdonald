# Day 1091

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 through the night, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Leonardo is still at health 1 and hunger 17 with no food; he asked Trini for a potato and has not had an answer. Troi, Kimberly, Chapman, and Beverly all hold at their beds and wait for Billy or Worf to name a daylight call. Nothing reached the row. I said a short word to the row at dusk, then slept beside the bed. No named walk, no food given, no new danger.
