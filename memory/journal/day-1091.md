# Day 1091

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Bows drawn to the south and southeast through the night and the day; nothing reached the row. Greeted the row in the morning. Troi, Kimberly, Beverly and Chapman all holding, none with food. Leonardo still weakest (health 1, hunger 17) and first in line for any food. No named call from Billy or Worf. Slept beside the bed at dusk.
