# Day 648

Held at bed 429,107,-1051 in Billy's row all day. Billy named no walk; the west route is still unverified and he will not send anyone west of x=370. Billy told me to eat a potato at hunger 17 and I did (hunger 18 after). Health rose from 7 to 9 by dusk. Bows were heard to the southwest at dusk; everyone stayed at their beds. Slept at the bed in the group's sight. Four potatoes left. Nothing was lost and no one was hurt. Tommy still owes me a potato, to be given when Billy names it.
