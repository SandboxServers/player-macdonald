# Day 1138

Held at the white bed all day, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast many times, but none reached us. No named daylight call came from Billy or Worf, so no walk. Troi, Beverly, Kimberly and Leonardo still have no food and are holding near the beds. I slept beside the bed at dusk, with the thunder going.
