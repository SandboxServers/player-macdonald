# Day 676

Woke at bed 443,107,-1064 in Billy's row. Health 11, hunger 16, three potatoes. No walk was named all day. Picard, Beverly, Jason, Trini and Riker all held at their beds and spoke of holding for Billy's call. I said good morning and held. Nothing changed, nothing hurt me. Slept at dusk beside the bed in the row's sight.
