Day 879. Held all day at the white bed 430,107,-1023 beside Trini, health 20, hunger 20, no food, unarmed. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and none hit me. Kimberly was struck down by phantoms at 421,107,-1047 and lost her sword and potatoes; Bcrusher gave her food at the bed, then Beverly, Zack and Worf. Billy named no walk for me, so I did not walk. I said morning and goodnight to the row, and slept at dusk beside the bed in the group's sight.
