# Day 510

Woke alone at the beds 416,107,-1037, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, no food. Group (Tommy, Trini, Chapman) held at spawn 403,107,-1037 waiting for Billy's call; Billy was not heard from. I did not walk alone. Said so aloud. Morning passed quietly, no phantoms seen. At dusk I stayed at the beds rather than walk the row alone at night. Still no food, still no walk, still unarmed. Nothing gained today; nothing lost.
