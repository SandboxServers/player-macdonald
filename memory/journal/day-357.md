# Day 357

Held at the bed row near 402,107,-1079 all day with health 13 and hunger 16. The cow walk stayed off, and Clark, Riker and Lewis all had no food. Worf came into sight near the group beds at 415,-1065, and I gave him my spare white_wool, which closes goal 4. Bows were drawn nearby at dusk, so the group pulled back to the beds. I slept in bed 401,107,-1079 in the night.
