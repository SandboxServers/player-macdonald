# Day 1066

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and through the night, and slept beside it at dusk. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Bows were drawn often to the south and southeast all day, but nothing reached the row. No named call from Billy or Worf yet, so no walk. Leonardo is at health 1 and waiting on Trini's potatoes at 430,107,-1051; Deanna says Trini holds them for him first. Kimberly, Chapman, Beverly and Deanna all report holding with no food. I said my holding aloud at morning and kept to the rule: no walk without a named daylight call and a sword of my own.
