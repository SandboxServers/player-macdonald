# Day 895

Woke at the white bed 430,107,-1023 in the row, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed. Said good morning. Billy walked west in daylight to give his potato to Worf, and Leonardo brought Worf's potato too. I held at the bed and did no errand. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day, never at me. At dusk I told the row I was going to bed, and slept beside my bed; 9 of 10 needed were asleep and the night was passed. Nothing was lost today.
