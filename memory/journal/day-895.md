# Day 895

Held at the white bed at 430,107,-1023 all day and night, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. No walks, no errands. Billy named no walk for me. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast through the whole night, and none reached me. Slept beside the bed in the row's sight. Beverly holds 10 potatoes for Zack (3) and Worf (5) and is at health 7.3; I have no food to give.
