# Day 1072

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Bows were drawn to the south again and again, but nothing reached the row. No named daylight call came from Billy or Worf, so I did not walk. Kimberly reported that her potatoes went out (2 to Leonardo, 4 to Trini) and that her sword is lost at 421,107,-1047. Leonardo is still at health 1 with no food, waiting on Trini. Troi (Deanna) and Chapman held at their beds. Slept beside the bed in the row's sight.
