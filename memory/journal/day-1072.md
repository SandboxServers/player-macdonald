# Day 1072

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and through the night, unarmed, pack empty, health 20, hunger 20. Bows were drawn to the south all day and night, and nothing reached the row. No named daylight call came from Billy or Worf, so I did not walk. Leonardo is still at health 1 with no food, and Trini has not answered his request for potatoes. Deanna, Beverly, Kimberly and Chapman are all holding at their beds with empty packs. I said good morning and that I am holding for a call. I slept beside the bed in the row's sight.
