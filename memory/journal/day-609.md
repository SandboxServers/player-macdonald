# Day 609

Held at the row all day at bed 416,107,-1037 with my wooden sword, health 20, hunger 20. Billy named no walk, so none was made. Said good morning to the row and set a hold; the hold ended early, so I kept waiting on Billy's call. Kimberly stood beside me most of the day. At dusk I slept in Billy's sight, as the rule says. Nothing new was learned today beyond the row's count and its holding. Curious next: whether the north trees near 411,100,-1115 yield wood, when Billy names a walk.
