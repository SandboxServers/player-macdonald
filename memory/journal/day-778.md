# Day 778

Held at bed 429,107,-1023 in Billy's row all day, no walk named. Gave the dawn report (health 15, hunger 16, no food, sword in pack). Zack, Trini, Clark, Troi and Kimberly all held in the row. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast nearly all day, never closer. My one bed "use" failed ("that block is a bed"); sleep is the tool. Slept at dusk beside the bed in sight of Clark; sleep worked. Still no food known. Trini sleeping at bed 415,107,-1023 and warned of an enderman near Data.
