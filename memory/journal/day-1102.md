# Day 1102

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, empty pack, health 20, hunger 20. No named call from Billy or Worf. Bows drawn to the south several times, none reached me. Beverly, Troi, Leonardo and Chapman all reported holding at their beds, no food in the row. Leonardo still at health 1 and asking for a potato; nobody has one to give. At dusk I said a short goodnight and slept beside the bed. Nothing new to act on. Still curious whether a daylight route will be named, and whether Trini hands out the potatoes.
