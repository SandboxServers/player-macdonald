# Day 1102

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and night. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. No named daylight call from Billy or Worf. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast nearly all day, none reached the row. Leonardo (health 1, hunger 17) still asks for a potato; I have none to give. Troi, Chapman and Beverly are holding at their beds too. Nothing changed; I will keep holding and sleep beside the bed at dusk.
