# Day 1102

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 through the day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. No named call from Billy or Worf and no route. Bows drawn to the south and southeast all day and night, none came near. Leonardo still at health 1, asking Trini and Worf for a potato; I have none. Troi, Chapman and Beverly held at their beds and spoke each morning. Slept beside my bed at night.
