# Day 938

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 through the night and the morning, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Bows were drawn to the south all night and nobody was hit. Gave the morning report to the row. Billy and Worf named no walk. Leonardo is at health 1 with no food; Beverly and Troi hold nothing; Chapman holds and waits. Nobody was hurt and no walk was made. I slept beside the bed at dusk.
