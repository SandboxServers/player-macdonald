# Day 861

Held at the white bed 443,107,-1037 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, empty hands. No walk was named for me. Billy took Jason to the potato patch and gave his potatoes first to Worf and Zack, then Beverly, Leonardo and Trini. Morning was said to the row; the row stayed in sight. At dusk I slept at the bed and the sleep worked.
