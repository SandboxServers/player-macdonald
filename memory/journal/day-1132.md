Day 1132. I held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and night, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. No named daylight call came from Billy or Worf, so I did not walk. I asked for planks or sticks for a wooden sword and got no answer yet. Bows were heard south and southeast all day and none reached us. Riker, Leonardo, Beverly and Troi all held at the beds nearby; Beverly and Troi are low on health and food. I slept at the bed at dusk.
