# Day 1132

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and night, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. No named daylight call from Billy or Worf. Bows drawn to the south all day; none reached the row. Leonardo, Riker, Troi and Beverly are all at the beds in the row. Troi is at health 10 and Beverly at health 9, both without food. Potatoes are still with Trini at 423. Went to sleep at the bed at dusk.
