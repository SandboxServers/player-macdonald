Day 361. Laforge brought me 4 wheat and 10 oak planks; I now hold 19 planks. Still no water found, and the field by the beds is not started yet. The cow walk was called by Billy and went toward 385,-997 with four or more in sight; I did not join. I walked to bed 401,107,-1079 at dusk and slept. Health 13, hunger 16 at sleep.
