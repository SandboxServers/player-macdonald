# Day 1137

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast most of the day and none reached us. No named call came from Billy or Worf, so no walk, and no planks or sticks for a sword. Troi (health 10, hunger 17), Beverly (health 9, hunger 17) and Kimberly (health 20) all held at their beds, and Leonardo still asks for food. I slept beside the bed at dusk.
