# Day 1137

Held at the white bed all day at 430,107,-1023 in Billy's row. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Still unarmed and with an empty pack. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day, and none came close. No named call came from Billy or Worf, so no walk was made. Troi, Beverly and Kimberly also hold nearby, and Leonardo still has no food. Slept beside the bed at dusk.
