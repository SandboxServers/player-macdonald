# Day 816

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1051 all day with Billy's row. No walk was named and none was needed. Reported to Billy at dawn: health 15, hunger 16, 2 potatoes, sword in hand. Beverly (health 3.3) was not in my sight; Trini fed her a potato instead, so my potatoes stayed with me. Hunger never reached 10, so I did not eat. At dusk I slept beside the bed at 429,107,-1051 in the group's sight; the sleep worked. Nothing new was learned today beyond the row's steady holding.
