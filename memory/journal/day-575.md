# Day 575

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight. No walk was named. Health 20, hunger 20, wooden sword in hand. Billy named the potatoes for Troi, and Clark and Lewis offered to hand them over when named. Picard and Kimberly are still asking about the oak at 414,104,-1126; Billy says the route is not yet checked. I slept at bed 443,107,-1023 for the night, with the row in sight.
