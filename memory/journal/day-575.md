# Day 575

A quiet day at Billy's row. I reported at first light and held in sight with Billy, Troi, Trini, Picard, Kimberly, Leonardo and Clark. No walk was named, so I did not leave the row. Health stayed at 20 and hunger at 20. Troi is at health 8, hunger 17; Clark has potatoes ready for her when her hunger reaches 6. At dusk I went to the white bed beside me at 443,107,-1023, sword in hand, and slept. Riker asked Billy for a free bed and was pointed to one.
