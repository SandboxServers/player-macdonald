# Day 1011

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed and with an empty pack, health 20, hunger 20. Gave the short morning report by say. No named walk call came from Billy or Worf. Bows were drawn to the south almost all day, so I stayed put. Leonardo is still at health 1 with no food in the row; I had nothing to give. Slept at the bed after dusk, in the row's sight.
