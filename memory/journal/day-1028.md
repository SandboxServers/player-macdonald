# Day 1028

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Gave the morning report at first light. No walk was named by Billy or Worf and no food came into the row. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and night, and nothing came near. Leonardo is still weak at bed 416,107,-1023 and asks for a potato; I have none. Kimberly and Troi are holding at their beds. Chapman asked Billy and Worf for food or a daylight route. Slept beside the bed at dusk.
