Day 1054. Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Gave the morning report by say. Leonardo (health 1, no food) asked Trini about the potatoes; I had no food to give and said so. Kimberly holds with Johnny; Troi and Beverly are holding too. Bows were drawn to the south all day and night and nothing reached the row. No named walk was called for me. Slept beside the bed at dusk.
