# Day 908

Held at the white bed all day with the row: Troi, Chapman, Beverly and Billy. No walk was called. Bows were drawn to the south all day and none hit me. Beverly is low at health 7 and holds 10 potatoes for Zack and Worf. At night Billy went unarmed to the sword spot at 423,107,-1048 and phantoms hit him at health 15. I did not walk to him, since I am unarmed and it is night. I said so and slept beside my bed.
