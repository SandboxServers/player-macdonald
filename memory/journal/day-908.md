# Day 908

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and night, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Slept beside the bed twice. Bows were drawn to the south all night and none hit me. Billy went out in the dark and was hit; I did not walk to him. At morning I said my report to the row. Beverly died at 430,-1050 and woke at the bed with health 1, still being hit; I had nothing to give. I did not walk, since I am unarmed and it was dark. Nothing I did today changed the row's food.
