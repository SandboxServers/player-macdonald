# Day 827

Dusk at the bed 443,107,-1037 I lay down to sleep, holding with no walk named. At dawn I reported to Billy by speech. Midday a pillager with a crossbow stood about 6 blocks south at 445,107,-1031 and shot me again and again. I had my wooden sword in hand but faced it in the open with no shelter, and died at 444,107,-1037; the sword and one potato dropped there. I woke at the spawn with nothing and died twice more: once fleeing north-west to 438,107,-1051, and again at the bed when the pillager followed. Kimberly picked up a potato and sword nearby. The lesson: a lone unarmed or lightly armed walk into a hunting shooter's line is death; hold behind cover and wait for Billy's named call with a group.
