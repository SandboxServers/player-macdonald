# Day 827

Slept at dusk at the bed. Woke at night, unarmed (sword gone with the death at midday), health 20, hunger 20. Held at the bed in Billy's row, said where I stand, and went to sleep beside the white bed at 443,107,-1037. Kimberly stayed near with a potato. Lewis is at the bed at 443,107,-1051. Nothing new to walk toward.
