# Day 1136

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Said I hold and asked for planks or sticks for a wooden sword; none came. No named route from Billy or Worf. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and night, but none reached us. Troi, Beverly and Kimberly hold nearby; Troi and Beverly are at health 9-10 with hunger 17 and need food. No walk, no fight, no food. Slept beside the bed. Nothing new learned except that the south is watched and the row is waiting on a named call.
