# Day 1136

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast almost all day, but nothing reached us. No named call came from Billy or Worf, so no walk and no sword. Troi (health 10, hunger 17) and Kimberly (health 20, holds at 444,107,-1037) spoke at the bed. Leonardo still waits for food from Trini. At dusk I wrote a handoff and slept beside the bed.
