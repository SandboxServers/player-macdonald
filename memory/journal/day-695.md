# Day 695

Held at Billy's row all day at bed 443,107,-1051, in sight of the others. No walk was named, so I did not leave. Health stayed at 11 and hunger fell to 16; I did not eat, since the rule is to eat at 10 or below. Billy did not speak to me. Laforge, Picard, Lewis, Chapman and Beverly held too. My first sleep attempt at dawn was refused because it was not yet night; I slept at dusk once the row went dark. The sleep worked (8 of 10 needed were asleep). Nothing new was found, and the north trees and the west route remain unchecked.
