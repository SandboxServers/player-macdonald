# Day 1125

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and night, in the row's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. No named daylight call from Billy or Worf. Asked the row for planks or sticks to craft a wooden sword; no answer yet. Bows drawn to the south and southeast all day, none reached us. Kimberly, Troi, Beverly and Leonardo held beside the beds. Leonardo still waits on Trini's potatoes. Slept at dusk beside the bed.
