# Day 1125

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day with the row. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. No named daylight call from Billy or Worf yet. Troi, Kimberly, Beverly and Leonardo all waited at their beds for the same call. Bows were heard drawn to the south and southeast all day and night; none reached us. Beverly is still at health 9 and hungry, with Kimberly having no food to give her yet. Slept at dusk beside the bed in the row's sight.
