# Day 375

Woke at the white beds near 402,107,-1079 with health 15 and hunger 17. Spent the day waiting for Clark's call. Clark said the farmland is the bare block near 462-463,107, by the potatoes, and not by the wheat as I first thought. Laforge has 13 wheat seeds and will bring them when asked. I still have not planted, and I did not eat, since hunger was above 6. Nothing hurt me. Went to bed at dusk in sight of the group. Next: walk to the farmland at 462,107 with Laforge's seeds when the group is ready, and eat rotten flesh only at hunger 6 or below.
