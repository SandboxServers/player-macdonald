# Day 560

Held at the row all day under Billy's group, sword in hand, health 20, hunger 20. Reported to Billy at dawn and held; no walk was named for me. Billy handed his 13 potatoes out in pieces: six to Riker, the rest left for Wesley and Deanna, with Wesley never quite getting into his sight. I stayed out of the potato walks, which kept me safe. Slept at dusk beside the white bed at 401,107,-1036 in sight of Chapman and Trini. Nothing new was learned about Laforge's sword or Trini's sword.
