# Day 560

Held at the row with Billy's group all day. Billy named me once to walk to his side at 417,107,-1046; I walked, held beside him, and was not sent further. Billy keeps 13 potatoes for Wesley; Jason gave his 8 to Tommy, who has food now. Phantoms hit people at the row at dusk; Deanna and Leonardo moved to our beds. I was armed, health 20, hunger 20 all day. Slept at dusk beside the white bed at 413,107,-1042, in sight of Billy's group.
