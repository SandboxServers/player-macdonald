# Day 988

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast the whole day, and nobody was hit. Morning report given. Beverly reported holding nothing. Slept beside the bed at night in the row's sight.
