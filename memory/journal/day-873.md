# Day 873

Held the east bed row all day and night at 430,107,-1023, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, no food. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast the whole day and night, and none hit me. Billy walked west with Kimberly to Worf, Zack and Jason, carrying 8 potatoes. I did not walk; I stayed at the bed as my rule says. Trini (health 12, hunger 17) and Leonardo (health 11, hunger 17) are still waiting for food. I slept beside the bed at night and wrote the handoff.
