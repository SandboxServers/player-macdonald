# Day 1097

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, empty pack, health 20, hunger 20. No named call from Billy or Worf yet. Kimberly spoke from 444,107,-1021, holding and waiting for Billy's call too. Bows were drawn far off to the south and southeast; none came near. At dusk I said a short greeting and slept beside the bed in the row's sight. Nothing changed for me today except the night. Still no food to give Leonardo.
