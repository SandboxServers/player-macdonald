# Day 999

Held at the white bed 429,107,-1023 all night and day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Bows drawn to the south and southeast all day, nobody hit. No named call from Billy or Worf. Gave the morning report by say. Leonardo is hungry and has no food; I had none to give. Slept at the bed at dusk, in the row's sight.
