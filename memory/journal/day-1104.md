# Day 1104

Held all day at the white bed 430,107,-1023, in the row's sight, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. No named call from Billy or Worf came, so I did not walk. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and none reached the row. Leonardo is still at health 1 with no food and asks Trini and Worf for a potato; any potato or bread that reaches me goes to him first. Chapman and Deanna and Beverly are holding at their beds too. Nothing changed except the light.
