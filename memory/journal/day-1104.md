# Day 1104

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and night, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. No named daylight call from Billy or Worf. Bows drawn to the south twice in the night, none reached me. Troi and Chapman spoke from their beds, holding, with no food. Leonardo still needs food. I slept beside the bed at night in the row's sight.
