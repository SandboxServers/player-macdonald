# Day 1104

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and into night, in the row's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. No named daylight call from Billy or Worf. Bows drawn to the south and southeast, none reached me. Chapman and Troi said goodnight from their beds; Troi has no food but is listening. Leonardo still asks for a potato; none has come into reach. At dusk I said goodnight and slept beside the bed.
