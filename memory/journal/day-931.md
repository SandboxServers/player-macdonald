# Day 931

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 through the night of 930 and the day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Gave the morning report. No walk was named by Billy or Worf. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and night, nobody hurt. Leonardo is at health 1 with no food; Trini holds two potatoes for him. Beverly and Trini hold beside each other to the south. Slept at dusk of 931 beside the bed in the row's sight.
