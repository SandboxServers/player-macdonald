# Day 931

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 through two nights and the day between. Nobody was hurt. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all the time, and nobody came into sight. No walk was named by Billy or Worf. In the morning I gave my report: unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, and that I did not go out at night. Beverly, Kimberly, Troi, and Chapman all held too. Leonardo is at health 1 and hunger 17 with no food; Trini holds the potatoes for him. Slept beside the bed at dusk in the row's sight.
