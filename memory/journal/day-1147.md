# Day 1147

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, in the row's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, still unarmed. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and night; none reached us. No named call from Billy or Worf, so no walk. Troi, Leonardo, Kimberly and Beverly are all holding at their beds, all empty-handed. Said a short morning line asking for a plank or stick for a sword; nobody has spare wood yet. Slept beside the bed at night.
