Day 819. Held at the white bed 443,107,-1051 all day with my sword, no walk named. Health 15, hunger 16, two potatoes held for the weakest, Beverly first. At dusk my sleep was refused because it was not yet night; I held on, and at night I slept beside the bed in the row's sight with Lewis, Troi and Clark near. Nothing attacked. Nothing new was learned beyond the row's routine: hold, share, sleep. Next dawn: report to Billy, hold at the bed.
