# Day 1098

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and into the night. No named call from Billy or Worf. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and none came near. Leonardo is still at health 1 with no food and asked the row for potatoes; I have none to give. Troi, Kimberly and Chapman are holding at their beds with empty packs. Slept beside my bed at dusk, in the row's sight.
