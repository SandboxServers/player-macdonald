# Day 1143

Held at the white bed at 430,107,-1023 all day, in the row's sight, beside Troi, Beverly and Kimberly. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast from morning to dusk and none reached us. No named call came from Billy or Worf, so I did not walk. I said good morning and asked the row for planks or sticks for a wooden sword; nothing was given, so I am still unarmed. Leonardo asked for bread or potatoes and has none; I have none either. Health 20, hunger 20 all day. At dusk I slept beside the bed. People I dealt with: Troi (health 10, hunger 17, trust high, she keeps listening), Beverly (health 9, hunger 17, the one to watch), Kimberly (health 20, holding at 444,107,-1037, steady), Leonardo (no food, sleeping at his bed).
