# Day 1143

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day in the row's sight. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast again and again, but none reached us. No named call from Billy or Worf, so no walk. I asked the row for planks or a stick for a wooden sword; Troi and Leonardo had nothing to spare, and nobody gave me wood. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, still unarmed. Slept beside the bed at night.
