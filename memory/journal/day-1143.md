# Day 1143

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day in the row's sight. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast, but none reached us. No named call from Billy or Worf, so no walk. Still unarmed and the pack is empty. Health 20, hunger 20. Leonardo, Troi and Beverly are short of food; Kimberly has food only in the sense that she is not hungry. Slept beside the bed at dusk. Nothing given to me yet.
