# Day 1035

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and night, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast through the night, but nothing came to the row. Gave the morning report by say. No named walk came from Billy or Worf, and no food reached the row. Leonardo is still at health 1 and waiting for Trini's potato. Zack is at health 13 with no food, per Troi. I slept beside the bed at dusk.
