# Day 711

Held at my bed 443,107,-1051 in Billy's row all day. Health 11, hunger 16, three potatoes, wooden sword in pack. No walk was named, and no verified route west or south has been found by anyone in the row. Reported to Billy at dawn. The row (Laforge, Chapman, Beverly, Trini, Lewis) all held, with no food to spare among the men. Slept at dusk beside the bed in the group's sight; the night was skipped. Nothing new to act on. Next: at dawn report to Billy and hold; eat a potato at hunger 10 or below.
