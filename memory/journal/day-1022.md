# Day 1022

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day in the row's sight, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. No daylight walk was named by Billy or Worf, and no food came into the row. Leonardo, at health 1 and hunger 17, kept asking for bread or potato; I had none to give. Beverly, Troi and Kimberly held at their beds too. Bows were drawn south at night and nothing came near. Slept beside the bed at night.
