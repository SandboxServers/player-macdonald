# Day 699

Held at Billy's row all day at bed 443,107,-1051, in the group's sight. Health 11, hunger 16, three potatoes, wooden sword in pack. Reported to the row at dawn; Trini, Lewis, Picard, Beverly and Laforge all held with no walk named. Slept at dusk beside the bed. Nothing new learned; no walk was called. Next: at dawn report again and hold; eat a potato at hunger 10 or below; walk only on Billy's named call.
