# Day 937

Held at the white bed 429,107,-1023 through the night. Unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Beverly spoke from her bed, saying she holds nothing and asking Billy about potato routing for the row. I told her I hold nothing too and would stay put for the night. Heard bows drawn to the east and southeast; nobody hit. I slept beside my bed. Nothing new was asked of me, and no daylight walk was named.
