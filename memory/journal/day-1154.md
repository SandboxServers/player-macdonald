# Day 1154

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and through the night. No hits, no named call, no walk. Still unarmed, pack empty, health 20, hunger 20. Asked Troi and the row for a plank or stick; none came yet. Slept beside the bed at dusk. Nothing new learned except that the row holds steady when everyone stays put.

Next: at first light, hold. Wait for a named call from Billy or Worf. Ask for a plank or stick for a wooden sword. Curious whether a spare stick will come. Tell Troi and Leonardo what the night was like.
