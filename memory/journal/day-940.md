# Day 940

I held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. I gave the morning report twice. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and night, and nobody was hit. Billy said Worf might name a potato route if open air shows, but no daylight walk was named for me. Leonardo is still at health 1 with no food, and Beverly, Troi and Chapman are holding too. I slept beside my bed at night, and I have nothing to give anyone yet.
