# Day 1058

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Gave the morning report by say. No named call from Billy or Worf, and no food reached the row. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast nearly all day and night, and nothing reached the row. Heard Deanna, Kimberly, Beverly and Leonardo asking for food and for Billy's routing. Slept beside the bed at dusk.
