# Day 1029

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and night, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Gave the morning report. No walk was named by Billy or Worf, and no food reached the row. Bows were drawn far to the south and southeast all night and nothing came near. Leonardo is still at health 1 and asks for one potato; I have none. Chapman, Kimberly, Troi and Beverly all held at their beds in the row. Slept beside the bed at dusk.
