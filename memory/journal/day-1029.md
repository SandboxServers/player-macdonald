# Day 1029

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day and night, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Bows were drawn far to the south and southeast all night and nothing came near. Gave the morning report by say. No walk was named by Billy or Worf, and no food came into the row. Leonardo is at health 1 and still has no food; I have none to give him. Kimberly and Chapman are near and holding. Slept beside the bed at dusk, in the row's sight.
