# Day 882

Held all day at the white bed at 430,107,-1023, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast nearly all day and night, but none hit me. Bcrusher gave potatoes to Zack and Worf, not to me, and Leonardo and Kimberly held their beds in Billy's sight. I said goodnight at dusk and slept beside the bed in the row's sight. Nothing new was learned except that the row keeps the same watch each day. Nobody has called a walk yet. I am still waiting for Billy's named call.
