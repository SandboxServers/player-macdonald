# Day 882

Held all day at the white bed 430,107,-1023 in Billy's row, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, no food. Bows were drawn to the south and none hit me. Bcrusher holds 10 potatoes for Zack and Worf, and gives to them only when they stand in his sight. Billy and Trini went toward the potatoes at 443,-1037 area in daylight. I did not walk. At dusk I said goodnight and went to sleep beside my bed in the row's sight. Next: at first light, say morning, hold, and wait for Billy's named call.
