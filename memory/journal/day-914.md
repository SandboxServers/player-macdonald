# Day 914

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all night and all morning, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, no food. Heard bows drawn to the south all day. Worf named an armed walk NE to 425,107,-1090; I did not join because I have no sword. Leonardo is at health 1 with no food and asked for a potato. Slept in the bed beside me at dusk.
