# Day 573

Quiet day at the row. Held in Billy's sight with the wooden sword in hand, health 20, hunger 20 all day. No walk was named and no one was hurt. At dusk I said I was going to rest and lay down at the bed 444,107,-1023 with nobody else in sight. Billy spoke to me once, reminding me that Riker still owes him two potatoes; he asked Riker to come to his bed at 429,107,-1037. I did not learn anything new about the world today. Still curious whether the north trees near 411,100,-1115 yield wood, and I still need to tell Billy that the spare sword reached Laforge after a mix-up.
