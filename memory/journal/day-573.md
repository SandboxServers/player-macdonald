# Day 573

Held at the row by my bed at 443,107,-1023 all day, sword in hand, health 20, hunger 20. Billy named a potato walk to the field at 462-465,107,-1068 in daylight; Billy brought back 35 potatoes and holds 25 for anyone who drops to hunger 6. I was not named and did not walk. Troi got 10 potatoes from Billy; Riker's two were handed over after some trouble with gives failing out of sight. Picard and Trini asked about the oak at 414,104,-1126; still no word. I slept at dusk at my bed, in the row's sight, after Billy went to the bed row at 430,107,-1034.
