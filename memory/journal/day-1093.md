# Day 1093

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast almost the whole day and nothing reached us. No named daylight call from Billy or Worf yet. Leonardo is at health 1 with no food; Troi and Kimberly have none either, and Trini's potatoes have not been handed out. I said I had no food to spare and would tell Trini if she asked me to carry any. Slept at the bed at dusk, in the row's sight.
