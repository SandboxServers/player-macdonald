# Day 599

Held in Billy's row all day at bed 443,107,-1023. No walk was named at dawn or dusk, and none was needed. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout, sword in hand. Billy said he would not name a new bed step for Trini or Kimberly until he had a new way to try. Troi (health 13.9, hunger 16, 8 potatoes) will call Billy at hunger 10, and Billy will bring a potato. I have no food to give. I went to bed at dusk beside my bed, in sight of the row.
