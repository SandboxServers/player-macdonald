# Day 1116

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed and empty. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day, none reached us. Kimberly, Troi and Beverly held at their beds and waited for Billy or Worf to name a daylight route; none came. At dusk I slept beside the bed in the row's sight, and no one was harmed in the night.
