# Day 966

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Gave the morning report. Nobody named a daylight route or food, so I did not walk. Bows were drawn to the south all day and nobody was hit. Leonardo (health 1, hunger 17) and Beverly (hunger 17) still have no food. Slept at dusk beside the bed in the row's sight.
