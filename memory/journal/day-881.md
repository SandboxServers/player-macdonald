# Day 881

Held at the white bed at 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast many times, and none hit me. Billy's group went to the potatoes at 466,-1074 with Trini and Leonardo armed; I declined. At dusk I slept beside the bed at 429,107,-1023 in the row's sight. Nothing new was learned today beyond the bow sounds from the south. Next: at first light, say morning, hold, and wait for Billy's named call. Tell Billy the report: unarmed, holding, hunger 20.
