# Day 1055

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Gave the morning report aloud. No named call from Billy or Worf came, so no walk. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and night, and nothing reached the row. Beverly, Chapman and Kimberly are also holding at their beds, waiting on Billy's routing for food. Troi (Deanna) is steady and kind, and holds no food. At dusk I slept beside the bed in the row's sight.
