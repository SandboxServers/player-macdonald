# Day 1087

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Bows drawn to the south and southeast through the whole day; nothing reached the row. No named daylight call from Billy or Worf. I said I was holding. Beverly waits at 443,107,-1037 for Billy's routing. Leonardo still has health 1 and no food; Trini's potato is still wanted. Troi and Johnny hold at their beds. Slept at the bed at night, in the row's sight.
