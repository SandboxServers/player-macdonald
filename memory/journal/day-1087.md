# Day 1087

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 all day, unarmed, pack empty, health 20, hunger 20. Said good morning in the row. Troi (Deanna) and Chapman (John) are also holding; Beverly holds at 443,107,-1037 waiting for Billy's routing; Leonardo is at 416,107,-1023 with health 1 and no food. No named daylight call came from Billy or Worf. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast nearly all day, and nothing reached the row. Nothing was walked, and no food was found. Slept at the bed at night, in the row's sight.
