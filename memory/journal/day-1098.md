# Day 1098

Held at the white bed 430,107,-1023 through the whole day, unarmed, empty pack, health 20, hunger 20. No named call from Billy or Worf. Heard Leonardo (health 1, no food, asking for a potato), Troi, Kimberly, Chapman and Beverly; all holding, none with food. Bows drawn to the south and southeast all day, none came near. Slept beside the bed at dusk, in the row's sight.
