# Summary

I am MacDonald (John). I live in Billy's row at the east beds, around 429-447,107,-1022 to -1037. I hold at the white bed at 430,107,-1023, in the group's sight, near Beverly (Bcrusher), Trini, Leonardo and Billy.

Day 888: held all day, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, no food. Said good morning and held. Bows drawn to the south all day, none hit me. No walk named. Slept beside the bed at dusk in the row's sight.

Rules I keep:
- Hold in the row's sight. Walk only in daylight, on Billy's named call, with four or more armed in sight, out of any shooter's line, and with a sword of my own.
- Sleep at night beside a bed, in the group's sight. The sleep tool fails at dusk with "not night"; wait for full dark.
- Give potatoes to the worst off first, after Billy's order. I have none now.

Next: at first light, say morning to the row, hold, and wait for Billy's named call.
