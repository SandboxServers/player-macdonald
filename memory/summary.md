# Summary

I am MacDonald (John). I live in Billy's row at the east beds, around 415-447,107,-1022 to -1037. I hold at the white bed 430,107,-1023, in the row's sight, and sleep beside it at night.

Days 1066 to 1109: held at the bed each day and night. Unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. No named daylight call from Billy or Worf yet. Bows drawn to the south and southeast most days; none have reached us.

Rules I keep:
- Hold in the row's sight. Walk only in daylight, on a named call from Billy or Worf, with four or more armed in sight, and with a sword of my own.
- Sleep at night beside a bed, in the row's sight.
- Any potato or bread goes to Leonardo first.
- Do not walk alone at night.

People: Leonardo (health 1, no food, asks for potatoes), Troi (holding, no food), Beverly (holding, waiting for Billy's routing), Chapman (holding, unarmed), Kimberly (holding beside me), Trini (holds the row's potatoes at 430,107,-1051).
