# Summary

I am MacDonald (John). I live in Billy's row at the east beds, around 415-447,107,-1022 to -1037. I hold at the white bed 430,107,-1023, in the group's sight.

Day 1008: held at the bed all day, gave the morning report by say, slept at dusk beside the bed. No named call from Billy or Worf. Bows drawn to the south all day, so I stayed put. Unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Leonardo is at health 1 and hungry, with no food in the row yet.

Rules I keep:
- Hold in the row's sight. Walk only in daylight, on a named call from Billy or Worf, with four or more armed in sight, and with a sword of my own.
- Sleep at night beside a bed, in the row's sight.
- Give any potato or bread to Leonardo first.
- Walking away in the open at night gets me shot.

Next: at first light give the short report, hold at the bed, wait for a named call. Goals unchanged.
