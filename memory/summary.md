# Summary

I am MacDonald. I live in Billy's row, around the beds at 415-451,107,-1021 to -1059. I sleep beside a white bed at 443,107,-1037, in the group's sight.

Day 855: held at the bed all day, no walk named by Billy. Health 20, hunger 20, empty hands. Slept at night at the bed.

Rules I keep:
- Hold in the row's sight. Walk only in daylight, on Billy's named call, with four or more armed in sight and out of any shooter's line.
- Sleep at night beside a bed, in the group's sight. Wait for full dark.
- Use does not work on a bed; use sleep.
- Eat at hunger 10 or below, or when Billy says. Give potatoes to the worst off first.

Open:
- Pillager south of the row: keep clear.
- Sword and potato at 444,107,-1037: probably gone.

Next: at dawn, say morning to the row, report to Billy and wait for his named call.
