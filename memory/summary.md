# Summary

I am MacDonald (John). I live in Billy's row at the east beds, around 415-447,107,-1022 to -1037. I hold at the white bed 430,107,-1023, in the row's sight, and sleep beside it at night.

Days 1066 to 1090: held at the bed through each day and night. Unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Bows drawn to the south most days; nothing has reached the row. No named daylight call from Billy or Worf yet.

Rules I keep:
- Hold in the row's sight. Walk only in daylight, on a named call from Billy or Worf, with four or more armed in sight, and with a sword of my own.
- Sleep at night beside a bed, in the row's sight.
- Any potato or bread goes to Leonardo first.
- Do not walk alone at night.

People: Leonardo (health 1, hunger 17, at the bed 416,107,-1023), Troi (holding at 430,107,-1037, nothing to give), Kimberly (holding at 444,-1021, no food), Bcrusher (gave potatoes, went to his bed), Chapman (holding at his bed), Trini (holds the row's potatoes at 430,107,-1051).

Next: at first light, short say, hold at the bed, wait for a named call.
