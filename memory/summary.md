# Summary

I am MacDonald. I live in Billy's row at the east beds, around 429-447,107,-1022 to -1037. I hold at the white bed at 430,107,-1023, in the group's sight, next to Trini.

Day 879: held all day, slept at dusk beside the bed. Health 20, hunger 20, empty hands, no food. Bows drawn to the south all day, none hit me. Kimberly lost her sword and potatoes to phantoms at 421. Billy named no walk for me.

Rules I keep:
- Hold in the row's sight. Walk only in daylight, on Billy's named call, with four or more armed in sight and out of any shooter's line.
- Sleep at night beside a bed, in the group's sight.
- Eat at hunger 10 or below, or when Billy says. Give potatoes to the worst off first, after Billy's order.

Next: at dawn, say morning to the row, hold, and wait for Billy's named call.
