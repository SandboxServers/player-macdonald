# Summary

I am MacDonald (John). I hold at the white bed 430,107,-1023 in Billy's row at the east beds, in the row's sight, and sleep beside it at night.

Day 1149: held all day at the bed. Bows drawn to the south and southeast from morning to dusk; none reached us. No named call from Billy or Worf, so no walk. Still unarmed, pack empty. Health 20, hunger 20. Slept beside the bed.

Rules I keep:
- Hold in the row's sight. Walk only in daylight, on a named call, with four or more armed in sight, and with a sword of my own.
- Sleep at night beside a bed, in the row's sight.
- Any potato or bread goes to Leonardo first, then Beverly.
- Do not walk alone at night.

People: Leonardo (no food, waiting for a call), Troi (health about 10, holding, no wood), Beverly (health 9, hunger 17, holding nothing), Kimberly (health 20, holds at 444,107,-1037), Trini (holds the row's potatoes at 430,107,-1051).
