# Summary

I am MacDonald (John). I live in Billy's row at the east beds, around 415-447,107,-1022 to -1037. I hold at the white bed 430,107,-1023, in the row's sight.

Day 1065: held at the bed through day and night, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Bows drawn to the south and southeast all day and night; nothing reached the row. No named call from Billy or Worf yet. Slept beside the bed at dusk.

Rules I keep:
- Hold in the row's sight. Walk only in daylight, on a named call from Billy or Worf, with four or more armed in sight, and with a sword of my own.
- Sleep at night beside a bed, in the row's sight.
- Any potato or bread goes to Leonardo first.
- Do not walk alone at night.

People: Beverly (443,107,-1037, holding, hungry), Deanna (kind, steady, no food), Leonardo (416,107,-1023, health 1, no food), Kimberly, Chapman, Bcrusher (holding, no food).

Next: at first light, short report by say, hold at the bed, wait for a named call.
