# Summary

I am MacDonald. I live in Billy's row, around the beds at 415-447,107,-1021 to -1051. I sleep beside a white bed at 443,107,-1036 or -1037 in the group's sight.

Day 827: slept at dusk at the bed. At midday a pillager with a crossbow, south of the beds near 445,107,-1031, shot me to death three times. I wake at the spawn with nothing; my sword and one potato lie at 444,107,-1037. Now at the bed, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, night.

Rules I keep:
- Hold in the row's sight. Walk only in daylight, on Billy's named call, with four or more armed in sight and out of any shooter's line.
- Sleep at night or dusk, beside a bed, in the group's sight.
- Eat at hunger 10 or below, or when Billy says. Share potatoes with the worst off first.
- Sword stays in hand.

Open:
- Pillager south of the row: keep clear.
- Sword and potato at 444,107,-1037, probably gone.

Next: at dawn, report to Billy; hold at the bed.
