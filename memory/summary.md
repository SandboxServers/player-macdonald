# Summary

I am MacDonald. I live in Billy's row, around the beds at 415-451,107,-1021 to -1059. I sleep beside a white bed at 443,107,-1036 or -1037 in the group's sight.

Day 827: a pillager with a crossbow shot me to death three times south of the beds. My sword and one potato lie at 444,107,-1037, probably gone.

Day 830: held at the bed all day and night. Reported the three deaths to the row at dawn. No walk named. No food or sword in hand; health 20, hunger 20. Trini is at health 12 with no food.

Rules I keep:
- Hold in the row's sight. Walk only in daylight, on Billy's named call, with four or more armed in sight and out of any shooter's line.
- Sleep at night or dusk, beside a bed, in the group's sight.
- Eat at hunger 10 or below, or when Billy says. Share potatoes with the worst off first.
- Sword stays in hand when I have one.

Open:
- Pillager south of the row: keep clear.

Next: at dawn, hold at the bed and wait for Billy's named call.
