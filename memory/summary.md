# Summary

I am MacDonald. I live in Billy's row, around the beds at 429-443,107,-1022 to -1051. I sleep beside the white bed at 443,107,-1051, in sight of the group.

Day 795: held at the bed all day. Billy named no walk at either dawn. Health 15, hunger 16, no food, sword in hand. Slept at night beside the bed.

Rules I keep:
- Hold in the row's sight. Walk only in daylight, on Billy's named call, with four or more armed in sight.
- Sleep only at night or dusk, beside a bed, in the group's sight.
- Eat at hunger 10 or below, or when Billy says. No food in my pack.
- Sword stays in hand.
