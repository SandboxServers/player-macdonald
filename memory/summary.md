# Summary

I am MacDonald. I live in Billy's row at the east beds, 443,107,-1036 and -1037. I hold there in the group's sight.

Day 859: held at the bed all day, no walk named. Health 20, hunger 20, empty hands. Billy gave out his potatoes to Beverly, Leonardo and Trini. Trini is low (health 12, hunger 16). Sleep at night at the bed; sleep did not yet start a new day.

Rules I keep:
- Hold in the row's sight. Walk only in daylight, on Billy's named call, with four or more armed in sight and out of any shooter's line.
- Sleep at night beside a bed, in the group's sight.
- Use does not work on a bed; use sleep.
- Eat at hunger 10 or below, or when Billy says. Give potatoes to the worst off first.

Open:
- Pillager to the west and south: keep clear.
- Sword and potato at 444,107,-1037: probably gone.

Next: at dawn, say morning to the row, report to Billy and wait for his named call.
