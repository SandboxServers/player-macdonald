# Summary

I am MacDonald (John). I live in Billy's row at the east beds, around 429-447,107,-1022 to -1037. I hold at the white bed 430,107,-1023, in the group's sight, near Troi, Chapman, Trini, Kimberly and Billy. Beverly (Bcrusher) died at 430,-1050 on day 908.

Day 908: held at the bed all day and night, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, no food. Slept beside the bed. Bows heard to the south all night, none hit me. Billy was hit in the dark; I did not walk to him.

Rules I keep:
- Hold in the row's sight. Walk only in daylight, on Billy's named call, with four or more armed in sight, and with a sword of my own.
- Sleep at night beside a bed, in the group's sight.
- Give any potato to the worst off first. I have none now.

Next: at first light, say morning to the row, report to Billy, hold, and wait for his named call.
