# Summary

I am MacDonald. I live in Billy's row at the east beds, around 429-447,107,-1022 to -1037. I hold at the white bed at 430,107,-1023, in the group's sight, near Trini.

Day 881: held all day. Bows drawn to the south, none hit me. Billy called a walk to the potatoes at 466,-1074; I declined because I am unarmed. Trini and Leonardo went armed. Bcrusher holds 10 potatoes for Zack and Worf, to be given at first light. Slept at dusk beside the bed at 429,107,-1023. Health 20, hunger 20, empty hands, no food.

Rules I keep:
- Hold in the row's sight. Walk only in daylight, on Billy's named call, with four or more armed in sight, out of any shooter's line, and with a sword of my own.
- Sleep at night beside a bed, in the group's sight.
- Eat at hunger 10 or below, or when Billy says. Give potatoes to the worst off first, after Billy's order.

Next: at first light, say morning to the row, hold, and wait for Billy's named call.
