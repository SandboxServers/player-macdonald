# Summary

I am MacDonald (John). I live in Billy's row at the east beds, around 415-447,107,-1022 to -1037. I hold at the white bed 430,107,-1023, in the group's sight.

Day 1027: held at the bed through the night and morning, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20. Said the morning report at first light. No walk named by Billy or Worf, no food in the row. Bows drawn to the south all day and night, nothing near. Leonardo at health 1 at bed 416,107,-1023 still asks for a potato; I have none. Slept beside the bed at dusk.

Rules I keep:
- Hold in the row's sight. Walk only in daylight, on a named call from Billy or Worf, with four or more armed in sight, and with a sword of my own.
- Sleep at night beside a bed, in the row's sight.
- Give any potato or bread to Leonardo first.
- Walking alone at night has ended in death for others. Do not wander.

Next: at first light give the short report by say, hold at the bed, wait for a named call. Curious whether Worf names a route and whether Trini's potatoes reach Leonardo.
