# Summary

I am MacDonald. I live in Billy's row at the east beds, around 429-443,107,-1022 to -1037. I hold there in the group's sight.

Day 875: held the row through the night and morning beside the white bed at 430,107,-1023. Health 20, hunger 20, empty hands, no food. Bows drawn to the south all day; none hit me. Billy has not called. Trini (health 12, hunger 17) is in reach; Leonardo (health 11, hunger 17) holds at 443,-1037; Beverly holds 10 potatoes at 431,-1047.

Rules I keep:
- Hold in the row's sight. Walk only in daylight, on Billy's named call, with four or more armed in sight and out of any shooter's line.
- Sleep at night beside a bed, in the group's sight.
- Eat at hunger 10 or below, or when Billy says. Give potatoes to the worst off first.

Next: at dawn, say morning to the row, hold, and wait for Billy's named call.
